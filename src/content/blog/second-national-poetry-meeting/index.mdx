The Writers Association of Cuba invites you to participate in the 2nd Poetry National Meeting which will be celebrated from March 20th to March 22nd, 2017

Program

> **March 20th**
> 
> 9.30 a.m.: Introductory Speech: Alberto Marrero, President of Poetry Section from the Writer’s Assoc.
> 
> 9.35 a.m.: Masterly Conference by Nancy Morejón, Literature National prizewinner.
> 
> 10:00 a.m.: Remembering the 50th anniversary of _Casa que no existía (The house that not existed)_, from Lina de Feria y _Cabeza de zanahoria (Carrot Head)_, from Wichy Nogueras. Panel: Virgilio López Lemus, Jorge Ángel Hernández Pérez and Neyda Izquierdo.
> 
> 11 a.m.: The Cuban poetry from lasts decades. Panel: Basilia Papastamatíu, Caridad Atencio, Israel Domínguez and Geovannys Manso.
> 
> 12 m: Debate.
> 
> 1 p.m.: Lunch.
> 
> 2 p.m.: Readings by Ediel Pérez, Grisel Echeverría, Israel Domínguez and Esbérito Rosendi.
> 
> 3 p.m.: Poetry readings: Liliana Celorrio, Clara Lecuona, Mariela de la Caridad Pérez, René Aselo Valdés, Geovannys Manso, Víctor Rolando Bellido, Reinaldo García Blanco, Zoleida Iglesias, Leidy Vidal.

> **March 21st**
> 
> 9:30 a.m.: Speech by Mrs. Katherine Müller Marín, Principal of UNESCO Regional Office, on World Poetry Day.
> 
> 9.45 a.m.: Panel: Current poetry international panorama. Participant Roberto Méndez (Present Hispano-American Poetry), Víctor Fowler (Norte American Poetry), Ricardo Alberto Pérez (General contemporary poetry), Francisco Díaz Solar (poetry of Aleman tongue), Lourdes Arencibia (Haitian poetry: an arrow with fixed destiny).
> 
> 1 p.m: Lunch.
> 
> 2 p.m: Colección Sur catalog launch. By poet Karel Leyva.
> 
> 3 p.m: Poetry Readings: Reynaldo García Blanco, Jámila Medina, Roberto Méndez, Mirtha Yáñez and Carlos Zamora.
> 
> 4 p.m:  Show “Resistance”. Coordinated with Diego Guerra and Alexys Díaz.

> **March 22nd**
> 
> 9.30 a.m.: Panel about current ‘decima’ on Cuba. Participant: Virgilio López Lemus, Pedro Péglez and Juan Carlos García Guridi.
> 
> 11 a.m: Reading of ‘decimistas’ poets: Pedro Péglez, Ediel González, Reina Esperanza y Rolando Avalos.
> 
> 12 m: Presentation of young improvisers: Alex Díaz Hernández and Rolando Avalos.
> 
> 1 p.m: Lunch.
> 
> 2 p.m: Poetry Readings: Roberto Fernández Retamar, Nancy Morejón and Lina de Feria.
> 
> 3 p.m: Show by Group Mimoclán Theater: Performance from texts of The Song of Songs, of Salomón and Asela of Eraclio Zepeda.
> 
> 4 p.m: Short concert of Cuban songs from singer Verónica Velázquez.

Everything at the Villena Room from UNEAC (17 y H,Vedado)
