CONVOCATION

The Organizing Committee of Havana's International Poetry Festival, sponsored by the Cuban Writers and Artists' Union, Cultural Project Sur , the Hermanos Saíz Association, the Cuban Commission for the UNESCO, Havana City's Historian's Office, Andalusia's Sociedad de Beneficencia , and the Dulce María Loynaz Cultural Center; convokes to the 14 th Havana's International Poetry Festival , which will take place on May 25 th through the 30 th , 2009. This event will be dedicated to the poetry of the Middle East .


MAIN TOPICS

The poets and the struggle against war in the Middle East
Poetry and civic responsibility


ORGANIZING COMMITTEE
Pablo Armando Fernández, César López, Nancy Morejón, Miguel Barnet, Carilda Oliver Labra, Virgilio López Lemus, Edel Morales, Aitana Alberti, Zolani Mkiva, Malak Sahioni, Alex Pausides

MAIN ACTIVITIES
• World Meeting of poets for the peace in the humanity's defense and the preservation of the planet
• Meeting of the water
• Laboratory of writings
• “Cubapoesía”, the travelling poetry
• Seminary on Arabian literature
• Readings, exhibitions, concerts, plays, sample of cinema
• Readings in cultural centers, public spaces, communities, schools and universities
• Planetary Performance Word of the world
• Sow of the Tree of the Poetry.
