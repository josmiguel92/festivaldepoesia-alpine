María de el Carmen González Chacón, 13 August of 1963, City of Havana, Cuba. Poet, essayist and playwright. A degree in pedagogy, English-language specialty.
He collaborates in the Exchange project UNEAC-Hampshire College as a tutor of projects from the year 2007.
Member of the Committee organizer of the Festival of poetry of Havana. Member of the Redde Comisionde communication and publishing projects of the network "Our America" of international festivals of poetry. It is part of the editorial team of "Movement", the Cuban rap magazine. She is coordinator of the interdisciplinary community project Speak out.
He won the prize "memoria" of the Center Pablo de la Torriente Brau in its Edition 2006, with the work Speak out, break the Bank. RAP and female speech.
It has published the booklet A girl is always a privilege, Collection SurEditores, (2010).
His articles and essays have appeared in print and digital publications such as Movement and Skiff.

From the divine to the profane. The gods and goddesses of Africa

and suinfluencia in the literary creation of the Afro-descendant writers.

My grandmother's grandmother was dressed in white and his body smelled of patchouli, santa cane and rose Apple. They have that their sick starting roots from the bowels of the Earth with his hands healed, as it corresponded to his race's African Queen. They say also that his large black eyes volvianse rivers, jungles and ancient trees. You could say blue, Red, Yellow, Green, in a language that your master removed, taking also the virginity of their sex.

Poor master. He and his descendants; in erected hatred; they could not prevent to me the spiritual testament that mother Francisca kept under the roots of a great ceiba. That way I knew of its tributaries, its odor, the accurate prayer for healing, the orishas that harbored it in the distress caused by the enslavement of his body and the alienation of his soul.

When I wrote the poem to my grandmother's grandmother, I felt that the word is filled with a deep strength. As if it were made and repeated in other fields. As if a law of life, hitherto unknown, lead me in the way of feelings of Ma' Francisca, to create with the mine the words that she hoped their offspring. It has been one of the most beautiful experiences I have lived. Looking for the essence of the family spirituality we find roads denied by colonization and the traumas of the historiography.

The religions of African origin; cultural memory and tool against the post-colonial hegemony.

One of the first repressive acts of the enslaving power was to prohibit religious practices to enslaved blacks. However, the philosophy of the existential reality of black people enslaved in Cuba, as in other parts of the Caribbean and Latin America; He knew how to preserve for future generations the narrative subject within the religiosity of the multiple Africa which we fertilized.

As analytical tool Black not he contextualizes as shape or skin color as a result, is a referential construction, It is supported from the religiosity, the cultural accumulation, the woven psychosocial, or versions of the historical reality that is assumed. The construction of White It has been a process conflict in which the racial and their assumptions are political positions. In the criollization raised from the praxis of classist national construction programs (Black meaning the colonized class, White political hegemony, Economic and cultural) links and links with Africa have happened through religion or anthropologists look (overwhelmingly white, men) or in the popular imagination.

It is a tumultuous tissue, While Africa has been unified in its belly role[1]. Structure that allows the construction of stereotypes, centralizations and collective subjectivities. The interrelationship ascendencia-descendencia sharpens the consequences. Then, mother, dramatized by the patriarchal system, handled to the point of hiding it within relationships of subordination, pairing and syncretic paternalism.

Is at this point that we have to explain what we understand by post-colonial traumas. We refer to the syndrome of simplification and concealment of reality (SSOR). With the objective of bleaching us to become children of the colony we reinvent Cuba. Giving the afrodescedientes estimated values between the sublimation of a burdensome past, the passive victim complex and the sexual erotic Foundation. I mean, without a doubt, to the intended speech by the end of the colonial period. Uncritical, without addressing the forced taking of awareness about its impact on the social construction of thought and its pedagogical implementation in which our nationality has been erected.

The conquistadors stereotyped to the enslaved in a single ethnic group, livestock. They did not do distinction to the language of each of the dialects difference, or to the cultural accumulation of the enslaved regions. We can see the consequences in the mass of alienated people of African descent, alienated, lost in conflicts of a daily life built in stereotypes, concealment and deletions of reality.

The presence of texts belonging to rites and ritual ceremonies of African history in our literature is mainly due to the relevance of writers, musicians, the plastic artists, to the functionality of these living traditions in the collective memory.

When in 1967, Havana's Mella theater, Cuba, premiered Maria Antonia gender studies were conditioned to the bourgeois vision of the female problems. Eugenio Hernández Espinosa[2], forever entered Cuban literature by placing a tragedy that took place outside the colonial houses, outside of the hegemonic language built and established as canon by the elites of the country.

Maria Antonia, written and directed, a man is the first step of breaking with the dramatization of the characters of Black established by the exclusion, racism and sexism. Any similarity, is pure dialectics.[3]
