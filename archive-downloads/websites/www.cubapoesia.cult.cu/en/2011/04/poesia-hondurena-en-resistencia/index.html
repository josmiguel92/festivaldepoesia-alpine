Honduran resistance poetry

21 April, 2011 Cubapoesíathe shift of the offended, Home

Message from Honduras: With poetry, we also construct socialism.

by: Samuel Trigueros
Tegucigolpe, 18 April of 2011

Almost a year ago, while I was reading some poems in the Cultural House of the Alba, within the framework of the 15th Havana International Festival of poetry of, the news coming to the island from Honduras reported the murder of more resistant fellow, fallen in the fight against the dark tentacles of the Savage Empire. I remember at that moment I asked for a minute of silence for murdered colleagues and I read the poem "before the explosion" - from the homonymous book-, one of whose verses says: "I have been to the Hill a Crown / made with perfume that beauty hurts", / deadly, / the iniquity; / "and I've touted / to injustice dead / need is finished".

Today, the State of helplessness is maintained in this hit Honduras, but also remains the will, the thought and action of a people who fight for their liberation. In this context, poetry occupied an important place given symbolic meaning, humanism and depth sensitive to constant insurrection that occurs in the streets, in the neighborhoods, in universities, in the field, in all the spaces where we fight this fight. With poetry, we also build revolutionary consciousness and unity of struggle.

The Honduran poets that we oppose the Creole oligarchy and the vassalage of the capitalist system of USA, We make poetry with words; but only words are not what we put in the popular fighting game, but the same life of men and women who we hold poetry with the coherence of that each poem, Each verse, each word, each sign, It must be endorsed with the specific action. Never as now shown in Honduras that poetry is not an act of intellectual onanism, but our war machine. Because we are at war, fellow; We are in a war for centuries that Cuba holds and wins from long ago against obscurantism and voracity of the Empire and the human misery and that now we have to also deliver to us in front way.

Fifty years ago, the Empire Bay pigs received its first Latin American defeat; and the sands of Playa Giron seem to extend in time and space to be the same territory where poets Hondurans, together with a brave people, tired of supporting the humiliation, the looting and slave chains, fight for their liberation. Suddenly, "There are who says goodbye in the sand", but we remain in it defending freedom, Justice, the right to dream and build a socialist country.

This small memory of struggle is only a way to greet, fellow of the National Union of writers and artists of Cuba, companions of the Tertulia "El Hurón Azul", fellow of the International Festival of the poetry of Havana, companions of the Cuban revolution; It is a way of celebrating our struggles and victories, a way to thank them for our poetry reading and also for its live poetry, "the mystery that accompanies us".

Long live the Cuban revolution!
Long live the building of socialist homeland!
Live men that populate the Playa Giron!
Viva the Honduran resistance!
Live poetry and poets of the revolution!
Until victory always!

Fabricio Estrada
(Sabanagrande, Francisco Morazán, 1974)

Poetry in resistance has risen throughout the Honduran, a gale of verses that come together in a single cry of wounded dignity, but also, It has come as a transparency that makes flaming, cleanly, flags and slogans founding a nation distinct from the well known up to then.

Few visions have been superior in our land as that where the words emerged between the cracks, on the walls, like angry ants. Few times there were among us the evident need for a language to the historical muteness, the murmur and the fast-paced morse of teeth pinched by the fury.

The brutality of the coup d'etat in Honduras could not be greater. From the first hours of Sunday 28 June 2009, the country was castrated in all its communications and everyday relationships. All media that denounced what happened were removed from the air and has established a cult to civics to wishful thinking and folklore as identity. The State cultural institutions were taken under this vision, as it happened in all manifestations of our citizenship. The repression señoreó waters, but poetry refused to shut up.

The people empowered in the poetic language and wielded it to express themselves through the few radio means that they endured the onslaught. When believed, spoken in verse and when they wanted to mourn terror, was sung. The Honduran people with their gouges, found poetry in his breath and scaffold, He realized that its orality was always poetry to reclaim dignity.

And it was for this reason that walls spoke openly by means of graffiti. The sleaze, words sinking as chisels and outburst gave the tone, the metric, broken toes, the sweet redondillas of front insult and the skin burned by the Sun of the gigantic marches.... It was not time for delicacies: the word was in front of the poets and had to reach it. Honduras was the poem, your pain; and us, the letter that over on the stained paper, the alphabet that the town spelled with their newborn language.
