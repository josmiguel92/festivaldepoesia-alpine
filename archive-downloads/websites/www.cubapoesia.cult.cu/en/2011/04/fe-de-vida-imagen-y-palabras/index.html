Havana, 26 April of 2011

I
THE CITY OF GRANADA IN THE VOICE OF THE POET LUIS GARCÍA MONTERO

("Cover letter" sent to Aitana Alberti by the poet Luis García Montero, on the occasion of the screening of the documentary Granada, South Moon, on Tuesday 26 April of 2011, in the faith of life space: Image and words of the Cultural Center Dulce Maria Loynaz of Havana.)

A city of their own is the scenery of the feelings. Granada, the city that made me as I am, with open squares and corners very secret, is the landscape of my feelings. In the cities where we have been children, We learn to deal with the time. Poets adopted that lesson with Baudelaire, While he walked to Paris and saw how turned into large avenues closed and secular territory of childhood. A new Paris was born before their eyes to offer the spectacle of the time, things are going or coming, life happens and that is born.

I walk around Granada between memories and reality. The places are an allegory because, next to that you can touch and see, is what has gone, what exists in the memory. Many corners of my childhood (a pastry shop, a library, a makeshift camp games, a football stadium) they have gone. Other realities came to occupy the city. The two fields, the living memory and present, call to disappear one day, they live in the eye of the poet.

This is the look that I interpret my city in this documentary. I am part of a tradition. Born in Granada and being a poet means feeling heir of Federico García Lorca. The adventure of my adolescence and my youth was to recover the city erased with the Civil war and the death of García Lorca. But that anyone confuse. Being heir to this poet is to bet on the future, know to look at the roots the chill of a forefront concern, Discover the best way to walk to the future in the energy of the past.

The city where I was student, the Granada of the years 70, It was a lively place, very hectic, a meeting point, a crossroads. Their culture not paid too much attention to the Localism. I met there almost all my teachers. And they came out. Rafael Alberti, just return to Spain after an exile of 38 years, entered Granada to comply, with many years of delay because of the Franco dictatorship, the promise that had been made to his friend Federico García Lorca. He was invited from the years 20 of the last century to know the city. In Granada it hoped the new poets. I have always believed that the friendship close to that you established with us, a generous teacher who gives her confidence to young poets who published his first poems, was it due to the nostalgic significance which he had the city, a symbol of age, plenitude and the tragedy that finally is exceeded. With Rafael Alberti, Thanks to your generosity with me, I learned a lot of what I know, what I am, what I have lost, what I wish to ask the future.

It makes me very excited to send, through my sister and friend Aitana Alberti, a hug to my friends in Havana to present my city. Most of the towns and cities in Andalusia have to do with the Sun. Granada live next to the snow and was marked by the Moon. It is the Moon of the South, the place where dreams are meditating, where thought coexists with passion, where fresh water sources feel to deal with disappointments and hopes. This is the Granada, Dear friends, trying to introduce my memory. Welcome to it.

Luis García Montero
Rota, Bay of Cádiz,
23 April of 2011

ABOUT THE COLLECTION

With the general conduct of Aitana Alberti, in March of 2005 It starts in the Dulce Maria Loynaz Cultural Center, newly opened, space Faith of life. Image and words, which combines the audiovisual materials screening and discussion, both related to a writer or literary event. With a quarterly frequency and a circulation ranging between 120 and 200 numbered copies, a copy of the collection is delivered to attendees Booklets Faith of life, sponsored by the Instituto Cubano del Libro. These signatures collected unpublished texts, rare or unknown prominent Cuban authors and foreign. Tax collection is the List of poetry, series of short unpublished poems and the call Blade wheel.
