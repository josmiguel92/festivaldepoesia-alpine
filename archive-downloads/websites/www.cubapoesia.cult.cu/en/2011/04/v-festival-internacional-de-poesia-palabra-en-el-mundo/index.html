Under the name of Word of the world we call five years ago, to the realization of a planetary poetic action for world peace. In 2010 Poets of 500 cities responded to the call and organized readings involving some 2000 Poets. This year we work to something similar. In Cuba, the Association of writers and community culture of UNEAC address sponsored by, next to the Havana International Festival of poetry of, a reading in the most important cities of the country, whose highlight will be in the minor Basilica of the convent of San Francisco de Asís, in the historic centre of old Havana, the next day Tuesday 24 may, When Cuban poets read their work in an evening in which the singer Bárbara Llanes will offer a brief concert with the work of master Roberto Valera José Martí text-based, Juan Ramón Jiménez, Nicolás Guillén, Angel Augier, Roberto Fernández Retamar, Reynaldo Gonzalez, Humberto Arenal and Nivaria Tejera.

Of the 19 to the 24 may of the 2011. Give a chance to peace. From more than 500 cities in 50 countries, to widen the world through poetry. Organizing readings in schools, universities, theatres, cafes, amphitheatres, parks, squares, streets, houses of culture, restaurants, radio stations, television studios, shopping centres or where the imagination advise it.

On behalf of the poets we organize this planetary poetic action born five years ago in support of Cuba, We invite you to participate in these unique readings that will take place in hundreds of cities around the world.
