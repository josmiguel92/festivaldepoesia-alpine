Jun 20 2011

Cuban hip-hop in medellin.

Categories:

global actions, delivery quick/fast delivery, breaking news, poets in defense of humanity

The representation of Cuban poetry at the International Festival of Medellín will count with the presence of one of the most outstanding poets of hip hop on the island, leader of the Agencia Cubana de Rap, Magia Lopez; next to Waldo Leyva y Julio Mitjans, winner of the poetry of the Gaceta de Cuba, they will travel in the first week of July to the most important festival of poetry of the world. The West Indian participation is completed with the assistance of Alex Pausides to participate in the Foundation of the World Federation of poetry festivals. The poet Aitana Alberti, who also attends the festival, He taught a class in the school of poetry in Medellín, inspiring project of the laboratory of writings, that lead in Havana the poets Jesus David Curbelo and Roberto Manzano.

 

Magia Lopez Cabrera born in Havana, the 23 of June of 1970.

He begins his artistic life in the year 1987 as a dancer with a group of Afro-Cuban dance, staying in this four years. In 1992 graduated from technical means in communication. The following year again relates to the art through the manual work with the mud until at 1996 Integra group Obsession by Alexey Rodríguez (the type of this). Founder of the fabri_k project, community art project founded in 2004.

Loyal and proud representative of the afrodescendencia reflects an interesting balance of genres with his group, stylistic diversity and musical, which passes from working with elements of jazz, Cuban music and a wide range of currents of African origin latent in America, until the creation of very dissimilar texts.

In their 15 years of work has achieved with his group Obsession participate in various events and festivals. His music has reached countries such as us.UU, Great Britain, France, Mexico, Venezuela and Brazil.

mail: dirrap@icm.cu
