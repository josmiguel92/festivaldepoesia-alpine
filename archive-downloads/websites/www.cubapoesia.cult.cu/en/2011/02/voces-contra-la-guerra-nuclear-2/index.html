Feb 10 2011

voices against nuclear war

Categories:

global actions, Home, Poets against nuclear war, poets in defense of humanity

Dozens of foreign and Cuban poets will participate during the Havana's international book fair in the readings against nuclear war, convened by the International Festival of the poetry of Havana. Sessions, It will begin on Friday 11 February in la Casa del ALBA Cultural, they will also have space in other prestigious Cuban institutions such as the Casa de las Américas, UNEAC, the Cuba Pavilion and the José Martí Memorial. The closure will be the 18 February in the room Nicolás Guillén of the precinct of San Carlos de la Cabaña.

Manifesto of Havana: Voices against nuclear war.

Civilization lives on the edge of a huge war. The aspiration of the people of good will around the world to live in peace, He has found in the word of comrade Fidel Castro, historic leader of the Cuban revolution, a spokesman of the first magnitude. The poets we support all action that calls the global awareness of this cardinal issue of our time, When is at stake, not only cultural heritage accumulated by the man in its historical evolution, but the survival of the human species and the fate of the planet as we know them.

In a poetic high projection media action we propose to make the defense against Nuclear war with the participation of poets from Cuba and the world, in the context of the Havana's international book fair. With these words appears in web cubapoesia.cult.cu the call to a global poetic action against nuclear war, in defense of life, for peace, in defense of humanity and the preservation of the planet.

Determined to incorporate our action to the universal consensus to create a planetary sensitivity that man is you ethically natural and inherent war fighting, against terrorism, against the depredation of the environment and against any exclusion on grounds of race, religion, culture, ideology or political creeds. We are convinced that the world has to be transformed with urgency, that man has to change their mentality and their mode of fighting for life and human dignity.

The Earth and the species are in danger.

Accept the hunger, disease, the ignorance and injustice as something inherent in human existence fatal is an attack on the very essence of man. Life on Earth is threatened as ever in the history of civilization. Then save the man, the birds, the trees, the butterflies, the seahorses and dolphins, outdoor, to the sky, to Honeysuckle, odors and colors, the creatures and elements, together in this beautiful Blue House on your wonderful adventure in time and the cosmos. Open the possibility of breathing uncontaminated oxygen and drinking water for generations of men and women of tomorrow.

That are destroyed for ever all atomic bombs. Money spent by Governments on arms used for food, medicines and knowledge be returned them to their original owners, the beautiful inhabitants of the Earth, for the healing of soul and body.

Save water and fertile land. Save the forests and cities of man and schools and factories. Save the sunsets and the Aurora. Save the miracle of life in the universe. Poets have the duty of singing and to glorify the beauty of the world, reveal the human spirit in all its grandeur and at the same time fight against regressive forces that push the world into barbarism. We put life and work on the Foundation of a world better, It is not only possible, but essential. It would be a crime to be indifferent. We have a lot to do.
