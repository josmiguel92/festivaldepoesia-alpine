Venezuelan poets against the war

15 February, 2011 marythe shift of the offended, Home

Poets of the solidarity and the highest commitment to peoples

Here we have poets whose artistic sensibility were not allowed to remain silent before the horror. They are the poets of solidarity, poets who chose to not be elusive, they know that the war can not be resolved with only close my eyes or crossing arms. <why we have before us the courage of these verses that fix position to peoples suffering, that you go up fist in the fight before that image now, Unfortunately, We have become universal: the man before the war, to death without end. This forces us also to make universal the picture of the need for peace, the urgency of life and stressed to us that we must defend it. These poets do a permanent exercise of back to humanity, This is a work of high collective spirit.

Eddy Gomez Abreu

RAMON PALOMARES

WORDS BY LEBANON

(Merida, August of 2006)

Ah who are now alone and lost in desolation,

troubled homes, pieces of windows, chairs by debris

and the absent, those who filled all?

Ah looking nowhere in the midst of the noise

those who do not listen to any noise despite the dark rumblings

The people who thrive in this orgy

they do not belong to what is called human race

Death employs them, the world hates them, they are Entelechy of misfortune

On their screens, pointing out their new properties

Open carefully bloody maps

Owners of the world!

The poets of Afghanistan entered with guns among the stones

And Iraqi poets backtracking between the diaspora and the resistance

And Palestinian poets dream with a load of explosives above his chest

And the poets and the poets of the Lebanon today are guard how paramedics and guerrillas

Arab, Persians, the dry walls ,the Simun and the desert

The great Omar Khayam blesses them, Khalil Khalil Jibram blesses them

Apart from pray for women, men and children of tenderness and love

Apart from exclaim No more children traps by bombs

The feeling and why wonder

Where were the Lords of the powerful States,

small, but very important States

And the Supreme peace and concert agencies?

They have died as always in this season of hurricanes,

dead of plague, scary complicity

However with DIN and fanfares

United States, England, Israel

they appear in the proscenium, They provide for the friendship of the assassins

and promise to clear their land to Islam, the Arabs and Persians,

and throw them into the sea.

The table is served - they say

And how many living souls, buildings, shelters, rooms, airports and bridges

powder and Boulder will be fly - are they promised!

We will see. It is not so easy.

When it comes to the mortar, iron and stone, pass, It can happen

In terms of love, the sacrifice and unwavering faith is something else

How can you be equal?

JUAN CALZADILLA

IF WAS ANNOUNCED FROM A GAME ROOM (2003)

What makes the wave of panic more terrible

unleashed following the decision to call the war

When announces it to the world through fifty

microphones, is the lack

absolute humor who declares it.

If this decision was taken less seriously

If announced from a bingo room,

with casual and less judgmental air

( and even in shirt sleeves) people

It could be better prepared to understand

the war, In addition to being a deep business,

It is part of a great game where only

those who are sent to fight in it

they are the lost.

But who after hearing

pathetic advertisement devoid of humor,

you will believe that the world will improve because

It affirms that war is the only way

change it?.Tell him singing, Mr!

And it's not that I don't think any of you:

Who wants that the world remains as it is,

I don't want you to remain...

GUSTAVO PEREIRA

THE GAME

As one who weighs every minute of his funeral to split it into pieces

Came a time of dressed horror of glares

Came a meticulous time as imperial analyst

whose brain every night climbing enemies

Came a time ajar to deception, the sulfur and sludge

Came a time in litres of poison

dispensed as a rose

Came a time of sad drinkers and dismantled vessels

under the stars

Came a time of erected metal for filling all dream

Came a time disgraced by the bottomless pit of the rivers in penalty

Came a time in drowned in his outburst rock music. A time

cherished only by the bitter. A time of ghosts without a shadow

Came a transient and spent time away from all Prairie

new dawn

A time implanted to cry and shrapnel hits

Thickening is emptying cracking down towards his grave

because life will win you the game

STONES OF JERICHO

We rest arms to pray

for a moment

domains

in a harsh land

A destroyed map

In the winters

Prevented from exercising mercy

The final was to abolish all exile

be a rain

or a tent

Starry

at midnight

martyrdom is a sky

invisible

betrays
