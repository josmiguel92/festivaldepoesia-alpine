global concern for incarceration of the poet and journalist Colombian angye gaona.

13 March, 2011 Teresa HillHome, The Tower of Babel

Santiago de Chile, 29 in January of 2011.

SR. President Juan Manuel Santos

Republic of Colombia

Present

His Excellency:

Respectfully and unrelated to his country's contingent policy, We express our concern and surprise over the arrest of the Colombian poet Angye Gaona. Currently prey in a prison compound in the city of Cúcuta.

We ask for information about the events, procedures, the causes on which is based the charge and at the same time we are asking a good deal, transparency and certainty of a fair trial. The presumption of innocence must prevail above other considerations while the investigation is carried out.

We know about his talent as a poet and is appreciated by us. This is why we are interested to inform our apprehension and that is not an Assembly and an injustice that - if so- We denunciaríamos around the world.

Us poets not interested in power, but we have the power of the word. We hope that this dysfunction between power and poetry that comes from the times of Marco Valerio Marcial and his epigrams, is not a nightmare in the present.

Really, we would appreciate that you had a gesture, within its multiple tasks posed by the Presidency, and we respond with sincerity in this regard, ensuring due process.

Kind regards

POEMS OF ANGYE GAONA

When the war

You go to morning or die

Eunice Odio

Do not provoke the lion

He rests in its field.

What you could get involved

his slow gesture,

his calm truth?

If you can not resist that,

your inclination of more,

and are looking for a lion which serve

his own head at your table

and just a couple of claws,

yours,

admit in land,

nothing can protect you from that,

your intention of more,

and some trap,

some mechanical Eagle will bring

hunting the lion.

The lion Queen

even if you enjaules it

and take him away from them

to roar to your circus,

to hide its claws in your factories,

to unleash the wrath of the beast in the Sun

you treasure vaults.

The lion Queen and Queen sword,

the only shrub that grows wild

in the land of the lion,

It will be not given you exterminate

even if ordenases manar fire

to your throat.

Older brother

The indigenous nations of the Americas

To dance is the Sun the skin,

Golden God.

To dance,

ring of gold teeth,

They cavort soils

the nails of the bull.

It runs along the roads,

in the arteries.

Live blood,

the last body's blood.

It hosts my blood;

enter on your behalf.

I am a mix,

I am a bread,

I am mixed.

Your ancestors and your children

they throw stones at me.

When the stones reached me

join my body,

become loaves.

Take this bread,

take this life,

take the Earth

It's yours.

Earth gave birth where all our mothers,

where live drink milk from the star.

To dance, comes the Sun

with your gold teeth.

It jumps the skin you're in

Golden by the gods.

Take this blood;

is what I know sacred

for a compact.

Old blood is.

It comes from two rivers,

two streams,

perhaps three or four tributaries.

It is a silent River,

waits for its time to roar.

The time when the rivers meet,

Open under the Sun,

in secret mood of dancing

and be one with the gods,

in a high Pact

that is called Earth,

Mother is called,

you call us brothers.

***

Viva Barca

Loose ties, my soul

the candles that is forced to split cam.

We'll not charge.

Only the name and not must weigh.

Not mend in what remains,

will be only havoc in the current.

We set sail now

because, If it is not thus, then when??

This is the only aurora.

AND, If the sky,

that wind is elusive.

It will be only, then,

the murmur of the heart

to reach the wound to transfer,

that blaze forward.

Do not be afraid,

does not burn.

We are a living boat

born own propulsion

as the waters.

***

Small trick

Remember your guardian

René Daumal

Save me from me,

great slight silence you inhabit

beyond the shadow,

among the tumults of the brain

and you know lies.

Save me from my headwind

as the breeze is no longer

your Messenger of purity.

What will you bring, What will offer,

more here from the shadows,

in a time of disappearance,

When return separate heads

ask descreidas

If not let

a secret under the tongue.

I hear you, Oh Guardian,

but I cannot get your clear approaching spells headband,

therefore do not belong to this side of the shadow,

where perhaps you take by a drunk in the street.
