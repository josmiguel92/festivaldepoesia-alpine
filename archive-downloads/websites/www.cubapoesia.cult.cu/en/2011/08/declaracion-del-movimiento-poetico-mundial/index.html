Manifesto of the WPM / Statement of the World Poetry Movement.

Tuesday, 12 July of 2011.

Within the framework of the twenty-first international poetry Festival of Medellín, Directors and representatives of thirty-seven festivals of poetry from four continents have gathered for five days to talk about the State of poetry and poetry around the world festivals, analyzing and discussing the human concerns and aspects related to our difficulties and achievements as part of local organizations that promote poetry in each of our cities and countries.

The first session discussed the relationship between poetry and peace and the reconstruction of the human spirit, the reconciliation and recovery of nature, the unit and the cultural diversity of peoples, material poverty and Justice on possible actions to take in pursuit of the globalization of poetry and poetic.

The participants decided to set up the WPM, whose main purpose will be the increasing cooperation between the poetry festivals and strengthen our collective voice.

WPM recognizes that:

  • Poetry provides significant insight into the human condition.
  • Contrary to the idea that languages divide the world, It is this diversity of languages which enriches the poetry festivals.
  • WPM will strengthen every festival in its local and global approach their challenges and concerns.
  • The exceptional connection with the audience, evidenced in the international poetry Festival of Medellín does highlight the value of poetry to the people.

The main objectives of the WPM are:

  • To all the poets, poetic initiatives and related organizations are invited to join WPM.
  • Promote the Foundation of new festivals of poetry around the world in all its diversity.
  • Improve communication between the festivals of poetry and poetic organizations.
  • Promote the development of schools of poetry and poetic initiatives.
  • Explore initiatives for audience development and expansion of public access to poetry.
  • Direct efforts to the publication and translation of poetry around the world.

This movement begins as a meaningful process that goes beyond the individual claims and create promising opportunities for poets and the events and projects of poetry throughout the world and for this reason we must raise us with humility and protect the birth of this new project

Statement of the World Poetry Movement


At the twenty-first Medellin Poetry Festival, Directors of International Poetry Festivals worldwide have conducted a 5-day meeting on the status of poetry and poetry festivals across the world, analyzing and discussing human concerns and issue regarding difficulties and achievements as part of local organizations promoting poetry in each of our cities and countries.

The first sessions discussed the relationship between poetry and peace and reconstruction of the human spirit, nature reconciliation and recovery, unity and cultural diversity of the people, material misery and poetic justice, and actions to take towards the globalization of poetry.

Participants decided to establish the World Poetry Movement, whose main purpose would be to increase cooperation between poetry festivals, poets and poetry projects, thus strengthening our collective voice.

The World Poetry Movement recognizes that:

  • Poetry provides meaningful insights into the human condition.
  • Contrary to the idea that languages divide the world, it is precisely the diversity of languages that enriches poetry festivals.
  • The World Poetry Movement will strengthen each festival in their local and global approach to their challenges and concerns.
  • The exceptional connection with the public evidenced in the Medellin Poetry Festival highlights the value of poetry reaching gpeople.

Main objectives of the World Poetry Movement are:

  • That all poets, poetry initiatives and organizations are invited to join the World Poetry Movement.
  • To promote the growth of poetry festivals across the world in all their diversity.
  • To enhance communication between poetry festivals and poetry organizations.
  • To promote the development of poetry schools and poetry initiatives.
  • To explore audience development initiatives to broaden publics and access to poetry.
  • To address issues such as publishing, translation and the general concerns of poets worldwide.

This movement begins a significant process that goes beyond individual concerns and creates exciting possibilities for poets and poetry events worldwide – we stand with humility and care for the birth of this new project.
