INTERNATIONAL FESTIVAL "WORD IN THE WORLD" IN CUBA.

PROGRAM

During the month of may were carried out in Cuba dozens of poetic actions in the context of the word the World Festival V. More than one thousand readings in a 260 cities of the world illuminated globe with the voice of their poets. It was on the West Indian island. On the evening of the Basilica of San Francisco de Asís was awarded the prize Rafael Alberti to Rogelio Martínez Furé poets, Cuba, and Roberto Sosa, of Honduras.
