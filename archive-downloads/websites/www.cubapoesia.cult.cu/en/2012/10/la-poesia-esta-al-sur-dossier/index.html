Poetry is to the South. Dossier

17 October, 2012 Cubapoesíaitinerant cubapoesía, delivery quick/fast delivery

(Pylon three days, in the Sierra Maestra, was the scene of a singular poetic feast with motivode the celebration by the 110 anniversary of the Foundation of the town and the first twenty years of his literary group. This festival, articulated to the our America network of Latin America and the Caribbean poetry festivals and WPM, It is an example of the sober dignity of poetry that goes in search of the most simple people in communities and schools in this town in the South-East of Cuba).

 

Words of welcome

 

Guest poets, all friends:

On behalf of the authorities of the party, the Government and the people of pylon, We welcome you to the meeting "to the South is poetry" in its twentieth Edition.

A fraternal hug to all the poets, especially to Pablo Armando Fernández, Aitana Alberti, Alex Pausides, Alpidio Alonso, Rivardo Alberto Pérez, Nelson Gudín and other important literary work.

When comrade Fidel said that the first thing that had to save was the culture, It attracted our attention to the value of spirituality in the support of Cuban nationality. The South group, as so many writers, He understood that poetry inhabits the soul of the people.

Bring poetry to the districts, work centers, communities, It is a way of nurturing the culture of wonder and imagination.

The cultural effort of the South Group adds and multiplies throughout the cultural movement who lives in Granma Province.

Pilon feels proud to greet you from a tradition of deep roots: This is land of Celia Sánchez and Félix Lugones, land of farmers who opened the doors to the men of the Granma and the Sierra Maestra.

Here lives the poetry in verses and the silent poetry of men and women who love the homeland from this Cove which is the daughter of the sea and the mountains.

Come to "give the verses of the soul", the people already listen to them with respect and affection!

Thank you once again for the Groove where all planted the dignity of culture!

Welcome!

(Words of the Vice President of the municipal Assembly of the Popular power of pylon)

 

South live poetry.

A grain of poetry season a century

José Martí

 

When we say, "The South is poetry", We have the 'poetic fraud"that concerned Edmund Radiguet to recognize that it was say that the roosters only sing at dawn? I do not know, but by this South line stretching from Cape cross to the Maisí puntica, have been tremendous and interesting events.

To the South of the more than, became the greatest poet rowing in the bow of the boat very hard, the poet he would say Lezama, his most dazzling phrase is one that confirms: "I come from everywhere and everywhere I'm going". But equally gripping are the volcanic words of the pages of the diary of campaign where it says: "Jump. Big Bliss".

To the South of the more here, very close to pylon, remaría boat, The Mambi, bringing with him to Lieutenant Rowan with a message that would send the President of United States to General Calixto García. Between a landing and another had opened up an insurmountable antinomy. The message from Mackinley was the signal to take over ripe fruit; the landing of Playitas de Cajobabo was to die for the freedom of Cuba, and become the poet Emilio Ballagas call what, "the Lamb's two rivers".

By these banks also came the Granma with the quixotic folly of human redemption.

In the Cove of Mora, or Mulberry?, We will not know; weave a rare story filled with the mythical and unusual. This was a place walled in the sierra; before that Mr Buchanan, Open a Groove to the mountain, There was hardly a trail where people entered, or they expelled it with that final phrase: ¡"Pick up the Taratana and give pa Camaguey"!

The town made its celebration of birth around a sugar refinery, next to him, come pain and dreams, hope, the communion of the impossible. Arrived the days in which the people went out with San Carlos Borromeo, in procession to pray for rain that he calmed the thirst of the powder and gray.

And in the days of the dance, someone asked for a screen, because not reaching women, or the ten-cent cost part. Wit another called a sieve to take the truck for a few hours to win a pan buchito. The more astute bought a bottle of Bacardi by 35 cents to get drunk to the unwary and then take his place of work. They say that in this village appeared one day a man with a machete and said, "O let me work, or short head to anyone". Were very strange things this town, son of agony, and hope.

But the biggest mystery was the coca fields, There walked Barranquilla, on his white horse Headless Horseman. Many times heard stories which were by coconuts and frightened before the warning of the rider who dragged chains: Tomb and leaves!

Of my childhood days move me the stories of Leonor, the old woman of the neighborhood that we filled with stories taken from the fourth night; Once she told us that she saw a row of shackles marching with their violins; another, He did lose the sky with a cross of ashes to a young witch flying with your broom. The funny thing is that we never belied Leonor, even in the days of childhood, nor at the time in which they fell to us age.

Then I knew that what Eleanor told us, It would be the soul of a book by Alex Pausides: Bad magic; There, between the moringos and the pitajayas, late the fascinating soul of the poetry of these lares, Yes, because the poet Alex Pausides is the singer of the mysteries of this land, of its people, and the names of the vintage.

It is true that already in Pilon, the coca fields are not, Neither Barranquilla scares children, also killed wit launching the 100 whistles of his last pain, such absences will be for other verses, There is not even time for lamentations; Martí warned us that Jeremiah complained as well that after it not worth more lamentations, Why here poetry is also alive in the soul of those who do not write verses, endures the magic of biting along mountain, the waves come with its tune and his caresses, and in one of them, Proteus brings the answers to the riddles; While, Poets invent their answers and leave the jatias in a sign of the detained foliage, the wind direction.

To the South is the poetry? We could find the answer in a tale of the Sahrawi people: A teacher requires students to paint in their pizarritas a tree and a bird, It threatens his knees does not comply to the task in only two minutes; to pass the time, the master gave a blow on the table and said: Is finished the time!, raise their slates!

In all there were a tree and a bird, but at the end of the classroom a slate that loomed just had drawn a tree; the master, threatening, asked: And the bird, where is? The child with the innocence in the voice answered him: Master, When you gave the coup on the table the bird got scared and began to fly.

Poetry will always be, here and there, in the hand of a child who lift his slate only with the tree and its music, the tree waiting for the nest of the bird which began to fly, This will be the beginning of another verse, the open window of the next utopia.

 

(Words of the poet Julio César Sánchez-Guerra. Member of the group South during welcomes you to the event, in the main Park of pylon.)
