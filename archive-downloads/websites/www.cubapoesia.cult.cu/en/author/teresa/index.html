message to the Rio 20 Summit of the network "in defense of humanity"

8 June, 201226 June, 2012 Teresa Hillstatements, delivery quick/fast delivery, The Tower of Babel, breaking news, poets in defense of humanity, Without category

"The solution may not prevent the development to those who need it most. What is real is that everything that today contribute to the underdevelopment and poverty constitutes a flagrant violation of the ecology. (…) If you want to save the humanity of this self-destruction, necessary to better distribute the wealth and technologies available on the planet. Less luxury and less wasteful in a few countries so there is less poverty and less hungry on much of the Earth. (…) Cessation of selfishness, stop the hegemonismos, cease the insensitivity, the irresponsibility and deceit."

Fidel Castro, Rio de Janeiro, 1992.
