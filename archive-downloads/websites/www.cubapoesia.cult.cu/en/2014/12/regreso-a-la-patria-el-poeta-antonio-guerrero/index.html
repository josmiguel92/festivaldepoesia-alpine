He returned to the homeland poet antonio guerrero

Categories:

the shift of the offended

By María de los Angeles Polo Vega.

"We don't have the freedom that we all", but in our hearts and thoughts are very free men but to compensate for that lack of freedom, We have sufficient courage and dignity (…) We are to overcome and have all the reasons in the world to go with an upright front look to the future, insurance of a better time (…)”

These prophetic words wrote the young Antonio Guerrero Rodríguez, to Mirtha, his mother, the day 20 October of 2001, When viciously brutal justice American, folded to the interests of stateless Cubans from Florida made many think that he and his four brothers victorious return to Cuba was simply a utopia.
It is true that the struggle has been long, 16 years of unjust imprisonment that the young engineer at aerodromes used in grow morally and spiritually to the stature of a giant.
The hero was intended to offset their lack of freedom, even in the most difficult hours, reading, painting, writing verses, but always maintaining high in value, the dignity.
Today we all live proudly return to the homeland of Antonio, Gerardo, Ramon, three Titans convinced that all the reasons in the world attended them to erect staring receive the love of millions that, in Cuba and elsewhere in the world, we are sure that the best time is to come.
Today, by way of gift, We share with you some of those poems written by Antonio Guerrero in the cell in FCI Florence, Colorado, USA.

Butterflies painted
Some have white spots,
other, hopscotch in black,
The butterfly that I paint
flutters in my chest.
I look at it, moves its wings;
look at me, laughs, and I'm glad
perches when gentle
in flower in me.
As a sigh of love
jumps open calyx
and with his divine grace
give colors to the sky.
Spring without gardens
under clouds of closure,
but they fly butterflies
in watercolors and in verses.

They say that it was Mr Liborio Nobal, renowned Cuban photojournalist, who photographed the collection of 25 butterflies in the Institute of ecology and did you get those images to Tony.
That was how he began to paint in watercolors endemic butterflies of Cuba, here turned into verses.
Also during one of those cruel closures in the so-called "hollow" the poet, by way of journal, wrote, during each day, Since the 26 from January up to the 12 February, These poems

Breaking silences
Tuesday, 26 in January of 2010

It happens that the injustice continues.
It happens that I have a black box.
It happens that I am to say it.
It happens that I would believe me.
It happens that you flight without fear.
It happens that I shake my veins.
It happens that two Suns cross.
It happens that they obscure the stones.
It happens that the smell is not of fields.
It happens that my hour has an owner.
It happens that the walls are attached.
It happens that a brother smiles.
It happens that it is born in the chest
a serenity and happens.

Wednesday, 27 in January of 2010

When closed the iron gate
whistling winds of Hurricane
over me they miss and I turn off
del candil, flashing in my hands.
The cell becomes a Lake
that lie dead pigeons
and my climbing its smell of fear
as if the shadows I embistieran.
With my harmless heart
tear the frozen silence,
I losing extended absences
until the long night spent
and all the darkness disappear
in the morning light.

Thursday, 28 in January of 2010

This source of aimless day
subject to the wind of life
on my scatters light of dawn
It is never lost, but gain.
This succession of sunsets
without fragrances or colors or echoes
that fixed my imperfect substance
is never loss, but gain.
This time full of pauses
no longer my love matures,
Twilight in a dark place
like an endless path
between shadows that grow and tremble,
is never loss, but gain.

Friday, 29 in January of 2010

He evening while reading a book
(He was able to reach my hands)
whose rarefaction and sinister plot
just for me it made sense.
I thought of the great writers
able to pick up the light
and the beauty of the world
in Evergreen pages.
I closed the book and closed my eyes,
at that moment I was surrounded
a glow of Sun and moon,
of love and peace, and I realized:
you are looking for in many books
We treasure it within the soul.

Saturday, 30 in January of 2010

I am blessed by this isolation
where on my own holgazaneo
while I contemplate my soul wandering
because of the remoteness to lose it.
I am blessed by this silence
When I listen to myself
without that nothing me troubled outside,
without the time I look and I find.
I am so happy, neither more nor less,
without thinking about it, breathing
the atoms that we interspersed,
in the heart of a being
owner of an incalculable breath,
spreading by space.

Sunday, 31 in January of 2010

Not stop me looking at the Moon
or the slow dry tree winter,
You forbid me to set the fire,
play the dream with ash,
does not mean that it is lost
that pure love that with my eyes
and with my hands I forged and forge
for other eyes, for other hands.
Love that expires is not love.
True love belongs
to all the time, to the Earth,
without fear of facing storms,
resists up to the edge of death
and, as the natura, is eternal.

Monday, 1 February's 2010

I said no to the musty stillness.
I stopped on the bare soil,
I gave the first step, then the second,
slowly returning me rain.
At the beginning, was skepticism
and then came the dependence.
Later appeared the antithesis
and with it the development.
I started to spin for hours
with spirit and rhythm that drive
and they slip through the ether.
Surrounded by a tedium relentlessly,
in the place of my heart
I was happy, singing and dancing.

Tuesday, 2 February's 2010

Seductive night call me
from the abyss of silence.
!Ah, night magnanimous and magnetic,
still-inviolate leisure wedding!.
Wandering night, sorceress of eyes
they have the color of the Rainbow.
Crazy night, full of whiteness
to the desire to add insomnia.
Nude night on Earth,
before going to the other side without a name
Hug me, look at me and let me
touch your body to feel
the swell of beloved beaches
so hidden in the dark.

Wednesday, 3 February's 2010

Girl giving pills
addicted to painkillers
has the face of angel, reminds me of
a girlfriend of the past.
She makes every night a round,
his voice is breaking the silence
and I leave what I then do
to see it pass, an instant.
She never looks me in the eyes
that very hidden guardo
many faces of ages and worlds,
so many nights star without a name,
so many bouts of comings and goings...,
girl giving pills.

Thursday, 4 February's 2010

Looking for words and experiences
answers to nothing and the happening,
they come to the site of the present
like bees to diaper.
The beat of its wings floods
the air vibrating flames
and a Crystal honey wraps
the echo of the word love.
With passionate thirst for light,
aromas, voices and forms,
After the invisible soul is going
and you only see the face of a yesterday
by turning until it disappears
in slow spirals of smoke.

Friday, 5 February's 2010

The silence is green, suddenly,
My fertile heart, slowly
accustomed to natural things,
dressed in green leaves of desire.
Memories of the forest and the rain
they emerge with its hidden breath
and again I feel in my throat
a burning of jasmine and blood.
Non-stop, my heart evokes
true to the pure love looks,
kisses to the embrace of the night,
caresses that gave peace to the soul.
But, by where to walk your eyes,
his lips, pure soft hands?

Saturday, 6 February's 2010

Woman, break in my thought
as the waves on the beaches;
you suddenly enter my sands,
then to sea again return.
So are you, unruly swell,
relentless back and forth of waters
where my heart drowns
telling your body wave to wave.
You already know that you return,
However, don't know your name
nor know I remember your kisses.
Perhaps, a day when your skin
destroy the castle of absences
you will open the plot of my verses.

Sunday, 7 February's 2010

I have today written to several friends,
narrated any del rosario
of vicissitudes that I spent
(more than that all imagine).
In my lines I do you know
that this cell, nothing and no one,
for me it is a place of retreat
where I book my chains.
Like others of my life, This gap,
slowly fill it light
to that of its hollow to surface
that glow to my heart
becomes supplier of love,
This love that I write to all.

Monday, 8 February's 2010

Last night, When a sullen silence
as the blanket of darkness
I covered, and uncertainty
of raw snowstorms me intoxicated,
love caused a ramalazo
in the fibers of my heart,
pangs of perpetual absences.
I was awake for a good time.
Last night, against loneliness
resonant of yesterdays told me:
All the endeavours of my life
they have cleared this long path
where the dangers of death
they have not been able to spoil light.

Tuesday, 9 February's 2010

In my solitude I say your name
and his lyrics are a rising sun
among the clouds of dawn
where the flowers are opened with fire.
I mean it by walking the corners
forged and rolled back the thought
and in my soliloquy with your name
Neither lights nor shadows distinction.
I say that without that nobody listen to me
in a voice of intimate emotion
that just hurts the air and silence.
The questions of the firmament
answer, raising the eyes
to the eternity of your name.

Wednesday, 10 February's 2010

Insurance, in the midst of the storm,
I can imagine walking the world
as if you walk with me
shoulder to shoulder arms invincible.
You will pass with its dark wings
other so many frigid nights
without being able to annihilate the aurora
the final day of injustice.
Brothers and sisters of the fight
solidarity that multiplies
which leaves in spring:
You will give us the victory,
you will be with us
in the day of the return!

Thursday, 11 February's 2010

From my childhood of home and school,
in my youth from site to site,
all my life, and even in my dreams,
you've inhabited in my heart.
Island of Suns that everyone you give
Palms and beaches, birds and fruits,
that scorching summer I love,
that tree in the countryside,
prepared every day to take care of the flower
essential of hope
in me you always breathe peace.
Your love drives me, your love fills me with
light, oh, Homeland!, will come back
and the kiss from you waiting for us.


Friday, 12 February's 2010

Crossing the heart of the Riviera
unfathomable, confusing route,
more, accompanied by hopes
insignificant but certain,
look at the quiet geography
submerged in a glare of snow,
territory of the indefinite,
illusions of freedom.
There is fatigue, but I do not give up.
There are cuts, but I do not bleed.
So much fatigue, so many headaches
calm with the love of my dreams
an invincible matter fact
that do not recognize the guardians.

His other poems are dedicated to his ideas brothers René, Fernando, Ramon and Gerardo and fidelity that characterizes them, to the homeland, his mother, or the children, but love is present in all, that feeling led him to sacrifice the best years of his youth convinced, as Fidel said that "never a village had such sacred things to defend, "not so deep convictions that fight".

Faithful

I am true to my word every day,
all love to your untouchable waiting,
the same austere morning,
that gray afternoon, cold and dark.

For you alone, Remove and empty
the verse takes form of flag.
For you the foreign is not afraid
blood of the soul open, yours and mine.

If the silence tells me that I clarify
the second of every feeling,
to love to rise up and declare it,

Open the Earth and your vote
you confirm that the sky: I never lie!
I am still faithful to you, the more devout.

My mother

Call you by your first name, flower and friend.
Your hands make world every day.
Uninhabited woman, rebellion
to make love to arrive and follow.

You tear the fruit and stem
solemn is filled with joy.
Dynamic island in geography
where the heart peace forces.

Your name is an immense nudity
light and life. Without you the lucidity
I could not find the most sublime.

Where I you mention there is courage,
and in doing so, you avoid is hurt
my peace and my integrity, my mother.


The firmness of your soil.

In this life between dark days
And clear days, I lift up my song.
Cry with a voice that I terror
All my loneliness, breaking walls.

Here are the mature in the shade
Moments that once said so
And so secretly in the charm
Fleeting life they were safe.

Sometimes spent the day next to the sky,
Choosing a cloud, the first,
I want to bring in their high-flying

The site where I like waiting.
I will that the firmness of your soil
Preserved for my spring.


You're


My son.

You're my hand,
If distant friends can not greet.
You are my voice,
If stands of ideas I can not report.
You are my laughter,
If the purest time can not comfort.
You are my dream,
If the time could not dream.
