Cuban pilgrims in Mecca

Categories:

By Jorge Elías Gil.

The praise is for God. Although I have repeated this phrase many times, at different times and part of speech and ideas, I discovered the beautiful experience of feeling it spring from the heart as a thank you. It is not just a phrase, is the aleya (1) appearing at the beginning of the first Sura (2) of the Noble Quran (3). The last verse (4) the last of the Psalms (5) the Bible connects in your sense with that one aleya. Through their names, His books and numerous situations and experiences, the remembrance of God accompanied me as a balm on this journey of pilgrimage (6), because that's the pilgrimage, a trip, to Mecca (7) to complete (8) the religion of Islam (9) in the lives of Muslims and Muslim women (10), and it is also a journey back to nature (11) original of the human being.

Where I know I can say that six Cuban Muslims, five men and a woman, they walked with their bare feet on the places sacred days of the Hajj (12) of 2014, for the year 1435 of the Hegira (13), or Islamic calendar. The sacred precinct of Mecca, It contains the great mosque (14) with the Kaaba (15) in its Center, and the Valley of Arafat (16), the plain of Muzdalifa (17) as well as the desert of Mine (18), all of these places close to Mecca, they were visited by the Prophet Muhammad (19) – peace and blessings from Allah (20) they are with the -, When performed the pilgrimage, For more than 1400 years. Since then the Muslims around the world, If you have resources to do so, visit Mecca, in the listed months (21) and they thus comply with the fifth pillar of Islam. Walking past millions (22) men and women from many Nations – different cultures and ideologies, rich and poor, young and old, all, in one direction and with one purpose, the go to the Kaaba and directed towards God thinking say ” Mr, I am here “ -, It generated the reflection of the guide in our hearts to be better human beings, as with religious processes of the pilgrimage, the excellent performance of the Muslims inherent to the ethics of Islam left an indelible seal within us.

The count that I share below speaks of preparations the travel, scales made before coming to Saudi Arabia and visit Mecca and Medina. It also speaks of the encounter between cultures, the understanding about the essential aspects of human existence and the pride of being Muslim and being Cuban, but mostly talks about the importance of bicycling life along the straight path of good, moving towards a world best with good deeds. I checked one more time, in Mecca, something that I had learned from small through the work of José Martí, and that tells us that it's good because if, When does a good feels something beautiful inside.

 

The day before.

 

 

26 September of 2014, 10:14 am. Although he took for the Muslim brothers who were present some photos, I felt worried because Yamal (23) not reached, the meeting had been set for the 10 am. The comfortable waiting room of the Embassy of Saudi Arabia in Havana seemed a bit uncomfortable this morning, the reason was of course I, on the afternoon of the next day, Saturday 27 September, our steps would open the path of a trip craved by every Muslim sacred places. A detail printed the fact with a special dye, We were the first in pilgrimage from Cuba and although seemed happy and full of emotion for this reason, in fact, we were so, Sometimes we were caught in the middle of attitudes and projections of uncertainty and stunning interior before a big gift, and that surprise among us caused real laughter and enjoyment, coming as a completion and a relaxation.

 

 

 

At that point that I was became Yamal, moments later went up to the second floor of the diplomatic headquarters to get together for a few minutes with the Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in Cuba, His Excellency Mr. Saeed Hassan Saeed Aljomae, receiving us once again with the love and affection of Islam. We share impressions and ideas related to the impending journey and met about what had worked to facilitate our visit to Mecca, on more than one occasion the eyes of the Ambassador showed some tears of emotion as he spoke of the importance of the pilgrimage and asked us to Cuban five Muslims to do prayers for them and their families once we pisáramos the soil of the sacred precinct in Mecca and were facing the Kaaba. Before saying goodbye the Ambassador gave us each passports containing visas for the Hajj, and that they indicated that we traveled as guests of King Abdullah Bin Abdulazeez Aal Saud, as part of the program for the pilgrimage of the custodian of the two Holy Mosques this year 2014 received 2400 Muslims from more of 70 countries from all continents, including 1000 Palestinian pilgrims specifically in the territories of Gaza. Our group was integrated by Yamal, Ibrahim, Yahya, Samir and Isa (24).

At the end of that morning Ibrahim, Yahya and Samir completed their health documents for travel. I visit the House Museum of the Arabs and did the Friday prayer or Yuma (25) in this place. Then Ibrahim, Jamal and I change the little money that we had to use some us $ during the trip, These resources were vital later. On the 4 PM. I visited the House of Yahya, in Marianao, where would I find myself with some brothers of whom needed their hug before leaving, Muslim brothers who have worked much for Islam in Cuba and for the well-being of our society. At dusk was coming to my parents House to be with them a time and goodbye to receive his good wishes for the days that are coming. That night, Once in my house, I could sleep better. The previous days he spent hours and hours awake, within my hundreds of thoughts they monopolized all the attention without being able to sleep.

 

 

 

The day, the trip.

 

 

 

Muslims believe in the Decree (26) Divine, that includes the good and bad, and that indicates about situations and events that occur, from the more General to the smallest details, as the amount of the rain drops and the number of the leaves of the trees. In that conviction, we knew that our trip to Mecca had already been decreed, When Allaah decrees something more than just say, and is. A few minutes after the 5 PM. Saturday 27 September of 2014 We are located in the terminal 3 from the international airport Jose Marti of Havana, the flight would leave to the 8:45 PM. We check our tickets at the box office of the Russian Aeroflot line that would take us to the next level, for me much more than that, as Moscow meant reunion with Russia, My second home, two decades after having lived several years in that city.

 

 

 

In the waiting room, before boarding the plane, call two people from my mobile phone, to Professor Ricardo Selman, son of the Lebanese tailor Said Selman and creator of the Cuban guayabera, and my Muslim sister, Naaima, who had promised water of Zamzam (27), Mecca. While talking with them I watched people who, like us, were waiting to board the plane, among the Russian passengers was a young, Obviously drinks past, I enjoyed approaching all, crossing that line that sobriety conserved among people. To my surprise that young man sat down next to me on the plane and printed it to most of the 12 the flying an atmosphere containing a mixture of ethyl breath, reviews funny and sometimes disrespectful sentences without the awareness that I understand clearly the Russian language, all this coupled with my desire to disappear at times. The hours were going up to the terminal D of the Sheremetevo airport, Sunday 28 from September to the 2:20 PM. Moscow time.

 

 

 

The next destination was Dubai, in the United Arab Emirates (28), but the flight was leaving from another airport, in this case Domodedovo, located at more than 100 kilometers of which we had received. It was necessary to then go to the city and this me to enjoy internally the circumstance with an intensity that only I understand and that more than once, in a few minutes, my eyes full of tears. We are then looking for a taxi and to investigate with various drivers is was freezing I a little initial joy making me actually landing next to the memory of the title of that Russian film of the 80 that much like in Cuba, ” Moscow does not believe in tears “. Taxi to airport Domodedovo cost 150 $, amount that we got not even among all. Was a single possibility: use public transportation in the city, I felt useful because I was the only one of the group who knew Russian language. Ask a lady at the stop and let me explain with details that make, first bus 851, 50 rubles each ticket, station Rechnoi Backsal Metro, means that we also use, This time to 40 rubles per person. Those prices generated me several sensations, including a great astonishment because I remembered that in 1989, When you arrive for the first time at Moscow, the Metro cost 5 copecks, 25 years later the price of this service of urban transport had increased 800 times. I was actually in another country, This Russia was very different, Once again the certainty that everything changes. However the goodness of being there again remained inside of me, I love to see the people of Russia once more in life, with his peculiar way of being, hard and tender at the same time, like kids with character.

 

At last we arrived, already evening, to the station Paveleski where should take the express up to Domodedovo. We buy tickets, at the cost of 400 rubles each, Noting that the train was leaving in 10 minutes for what was needed to run, without knowing almost to go in the middle of the Stampede. We are entering the corresponding wagon and three minutes after sitting, the doors closed, the next stop was our airport and travel time: less than an hour. For the first time since I left Cuba I could sleep a little, My dream was deep and restorative. The internal amplification of the train announcement the arrival and I felt like new, He began to understand that it was a pilgrimage, as I said at the beginning, It is simply a trip.

 

 

 

29 September of 2014, on Monday.

 

 

 

Dawn, through the window of the Emirates airliner, It showed the interesting urban organization that we saw from the air, close to the international airport DXB of Dubai. The EK0132 flight had left Moscow 5 hours before with all seats occupied. Russian passenger, Asian, Arab and other countries prepared to step on the soil of this famous Emirate, possessor of great works of engineering, construction and tourist centre par excellence not only in the area of the Gulf but of the whole world. The aeromozas were checking that the seat belts were properly adjusted, the landing was imminent and the arrival in another culture assailed us excitement every moment with the images that we saw, the languages that we listened and odors, above all the aromas that were unique. We tried to walk together out of the air ship in a momentum of its own for those who travel together and are introduced in new and different places, a sublimated form of self-preservation. Our everything around was in order, the smiling faces, the clear and broad road, We were still together. Suddenly I remembered that I could do photos and videos, I didn't want to lose the moment and began to record the descent the ladder. A person occupied my attention, first in the camera screen and a few seconds later before me, It was the nearest guard forbidding me to keep filming, He did so with respect but with determination and caught the message accurately, had to turn the camera off. Airport security standards are strict, in fact, in each of border controls, at each check-up during the trip, review of baggage and passengers was careful. Dubai is a world of its own famous anonymous book of ” The thousand and one nights “, only that all fantasy and wonder belonging to this work have been updated there, without losing its charm, through the latest technology, more advanced variants at the service of people and the more dynamic thinking are conceivable. Products, trades and environments surrounding us were the expression and extension of a way of being and of a concept that no repairs on the object itself but in intension related it. The subject then acquires an enormous responsibility in the process of relations between persons where the first quality required is the courtesy. We buy some phone cards to call our relatives in Cuba and much walk, It was necessary to move from the terminal 3 to the terminal 1 from the airport, walking among them ones 2 km March with our hand baggage, between shops, restaurants, on rolling mats, escalators up and down with our sore feet that already started to protest, However the main reason, the real effort of this trip had not even begun. Flight check, passage to address, wait for a few minutes before entering the plane. We already began to feel something familiar recurring between scale and this process. At the very moment of waiting, came to pass something that frame the difference, the young man who had carried out the controls to our passports realizes that we were Cuban pilgrims for the Hajj (29) and it tells us that from Mecca had called to coordinate everything so that we vistiéramos from Dubai the Hijram (30) or the clothes that men wear during Hajj. White cloths were waiting for us. It was something that we impact much, We had not yet entered into Saudi Arabia and already felt the special spirit of this day. We literally ran to the small mosque or room prayer, some 100 meters of the former place. There we take off all the clothes, We purify water (31), We perfume body, We wear it with the hijram and please make our pilgrimage, asking God that we care and facilitate all matters to complete it successfully. We went hastily back to retrieve our passports and tickets that had been with the young, in that moment I remembered the race of the previous day in Moscow to take the train, We were still running even with the robes of Holiness. Interestingly, I was happy with my new outfit, He wore it for a few minutes but he perceived it as part of my nature, as if life had used it. Then we take the first photo dresses as well, the subtle weight of God's mercy was beginning to feel like an overflow from all sides, It was very little for the beginning of our pilgrimage.

The interior of the plane of the line Saudia, that we transported from Dubai to Jeddah (33), It was very different from the environment of the airliners Emirates, It stands out for its color, extreme comfort and elegance. The airline of Saudi Arabia (34), Although elegant and comfortable also, It was more sober in all its aspects who called the gathering, and the simple apprehension of the experience. Everything was available in the Saudi ship, without a doubt, but at the same time there was a silent message that you spoke about the impermanence of what you surrounded, things, services and facilities were doors to the invisible, that was the reality ” capital letters ” and that it depended not at all from anyone, only God's. Three hours between us of that important port city in the country that saw the birth makes 15 centuries the Prophet in the year of the elephant (35). Three hours that went by very fast, at times we rested, at times we read texts that allowed us to study the steps of the pilgrimage and the rest to reading surprised me notice that we were flying over the mikat (36) appropriate for what is called the hajjis (37) to repeat the talbiya (38) and to make the Declaration of Hijram. At that moment we came across sacred in which cannot be cut a tree, not even damage a small plant, or deprive of life to no be, No offense to anyone. Standards of the State of Holiness in which we were demanded of us an attitude which later would change our lives forever. We still enjoyed calmly, laughed, and we communicated among us as if we stopped being separated individuals to become a single be, This allowed the possibility of sharing the most beloved subjects and disagree over the issues more difficult without the most minimum look of fear or concern. There was no possibility of hypocrisy among us as Muslims, misrepresentation or simulation, We experience freely what we had learned from the Prophet Muhammad, the taught us that lying is what a brother hides him to another, which in truth was basically as an attitude.

 

 

 

Landing, to the 4:25 PM. Monday 29 September of 2014, at Jeddah's King Abdulazees airport was fast and accurate, also the exit from the plane to the bus that we aproximarían the S Terminal of the airport facilities. Muslims from various countries were travelling with us, the color of the mass of passengers began to dye white compared with the vision of Dubai which featured some Hajjis from the crowd of travelers. In Jeddah, the bulk of people devoted to the Hajj excelled in its number. I watched from the window the landscape around the nearby buildings, It was all desert and the fine yellow sand appeared everywhere, only covered by the residential areas and roads.

 

 

 

Already in the halls of the terminal the guides of each group or country began to find his people, a few minutes appeared on our, a young Saudi, If we were all wondering in English, He had our names and they began health controls checking our international health documents updated in Havana 2 days ahead. Then we went out in search of the buses that would lead us during some 3 hours to Mecca, but before entering them, I saw something that recur in many ways during the two weeks that we remained in that country, near a sale of cards for mobile phones office stood a young man with a cardboard box in her hands, inside had umbrellas to protect themselves from the Sun than the would deliver free to all. I saw him do this with every one of the dozens of people passing in my view while I see it, days later I saw several men with their resources buying water, juices and fast food to give to the pilgrims seeking this blessing.

 

 

 

The Yutong buses of Chinese manufacture in which we traveled were very comfortable, for our digital ad, we could know that the temperature in the interior of the bus was of 21 degrees, on the outside was of 38 degrees and the relative humidity was of 0 per cent. While the night was falling the tranquility it owned group of travelers, a few 30 passengers. Some broke the silence by repeating the talbiya (39), the exhausted body is disconnected at times. In that peace and that tired we entered in the city. In a twist of the bus we wake up far away discovering the great tower of the clock in Mecca, It is currently the world's highest watch, located in front of the holy site of the Kaaba and visible from any part of the city.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

MECA.

 

 

 

Go out and enter our hotel when my hands have difficulty to hold several things at the same time. Seconds later cameras of television and press ahead cause me more confusion to make me feel like a child. In fact the presence for the first time in Cuban pilgrims in Mecca was as news in various media, Al Arabiya, Arab news and even to Al jazira devoted space to include aspects about our visit, a week later in the desert of mine cameras accompanied us for hours, even when we were not aware of his presence.

 

 

 

Sheik Fawaz was waiting for us at the door of the Umm Alqura Makarim Hotel, a beautiful modern building of ten flats located a fifteen blocks of the site of the Kaaba, sacred, a comfortable five-star hotel. Sheik Fawaz had been with us throughout the month of Ramadan (40) in Havana. For the first time the community of Muslims from the Cuban capital had a reciter sent directly from the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in order to review the Holy Qur'an and learn about Islam during the evening prayers of the Tarawit (41). These nights of Qur'anic recitation were for us a special blessing, I would drop everything to help and save the days of this sacred month of fasting which I devoted myself to visit my brothers in other provinces of Cuba, It prioritised these prayers and recitation of the Quran Shaykh addressed even sick the last nights of Ramadan (42). Then my brother Ibrahim and I could collect some newspapers which featured items from our arrival, among them that included photos from our first meeting with Sheik Fawaz in Mecca, which us full of joy. This Shaykh behaved with us, and with many other pilgrims coming from various Nations, as a true father, worried every minute of simpler topics and answering any question with patience and tenderness. This was the attitude of many people who in one way or another we attended during the stay in Mecca and in Medina. I appreciate the company of the young Saudi Khalid Alhamad, excellent brother whose memory comes to me many moments, but mostly from the first moments, a few minutes after arriving at Mecca, I tried to explain something about the trip, the listened to me patiently, When I finished to inform you my idea in English, intending impeccably exposed, told me smiling: I have worked in Buenos Aires – Argentina – for four years, well I speak Spanish.

 

 

 

So we are entering this city, with emotion filling our hearts, surprised, glad to see again the Sheik, happy to meet new Saudi brothers and other parts of the world. Once installed in our room, We refresh our bodies and once again dressed in the hijram first visited the restaurant which almost closed at dinner time, We ate something and quickly went out to the street to take one of the mini – buses from the hotel that every 10 minutes and during 24 hours were guests as closely as possible the great mosque. Five minutes later we began to walk, two blocks from the sacred enclosure in order to visit it for the first, do the Umrah (43) or visit lower and raise our prayers to the Almighty to see the Holy Kaaba. I knew that a very special moment approached, in a few minutes I would be in the midst of an extraordinary event, I didn't know what I was going to live the most intense experience of my life.

 

 

 

In the House of God.

 

 

 

We had left everything at the hotel, We only had our garments, the documents in the hijram belt and knobs of water that we people delivered by road. TO 50 meters to reach outside the mosque Esplanade my view begins to see the crowd of thousands of pilgrims who suddenly are product of the inclination of the access road. The heart starts beating very strong and lips pronounced praises without being able to contain. I stood for a few minutes observing the symbol representing the whole of humanity, people of all Nations, speaking all languages, coming from all directions, converging at a single point, towards a single idea, covered in a single concept: the oneness of God.

 

 

 

Obeying the call of my brothers to continue and listening to the guards who repeated in Arabic without getting tired ” Hajji Tariq, Hajji Tariq “, which in Spanish can be something as well as ” Pilgrim paves the way, Pilgrim paves the way “, continued my March already within the Esplanade, an area of immense of hundreds of square meters that surrounds the Grand Mosque around the perimeter, and that during the stages close to Ramadan and the pilgrimage houses many more visitors than usual compared to the other months of the year. Hundreds of people sleep at that time on the floor, others pray, others walk in different directions, converse, they stop to decide what to do, those who walk in groups are organized, movement and calm at the same time. They are about the 11:30 PM., us moving into a crowd of two million men and women, all intimate of the most high, It allows your friends visit your home.

 

 

 

Shoes sandals, to not lose them, or lose much time later looking for them, I put them close to the belt and make sure be coming through the center of the large door. Everything inside is light, marble, Golden hues, spotlessly clean, hundreds of thousands of Muslims in peace. My heart beats with great force, It seems that it is going to come out of the chest, but Interestingly I do not feel bad or uncomfortable by this, is a peculiar State, hard to explain. The tears begin to run, but there is no crying or mourning, It is a very intimate moment with my Lord, I realize realized that beside me brothers are living something similar but I can not repair much in them, It is impossible to, I only remember that Ibrahim was crying intensely as we walked, on our side were Samir and Yahya who remembered their previous Mecca visit indicating the details that he already knew. I looked at my feet that were going down towards the Kaaba and gave thanks for the opportunity of stepping on sacred ground that was previously honored by millions and millions of Muslims and our prophets, from Abraham 4000 years until Muhammad 1400 years. We are slowly approaching the central courtyard, as we move the crowd is shoves before us without letting us see beyond people who are located front. We got other steps and the space between people becomes clear to let me see at the moment the Holy Kaaba, as a descent which not is what to do, I just cried and thanked repeating over and over again ” the praise is for God “, in Arabic ” aljamdulillah “.

 

 

 

Umrah.

 

 

 

Theless visit is arriving at Mecca, It includes the seven laps the Kaaba starting from the corner of the black stone. Once completed are performed two rakaats (44) behind the site of Abraham (45) or the closest thing possible to this place. Subsequently it covers seven times the distance between the hills of Safa and Marwa, remembering the difficulty of Agar (46) When trying to find water for her infant son Ishmael. God had mercy upon hearing the crying of the child, and gave them drink from the spring of Zamzam (47) that after 4000 years continues to provide water to quench the thirst of those who come to the mosque. On several occasions we took water there and I could fulfill the promise to bring her two Muslim sisters from Havana that made me such a request.

When we finished we cut a little hair thanks to the collaboration of a Muslim who helped all the possible brothers in this detail out of one of the side doors of the mosque. To the 2:20 am. We returned again taking the mini buses from the hotel and rest until the next day. On Tuesday 30 in September we did another Umrah, This time I brought my camera and took photos and videos of all keeping a very useful visual testimony to show all the brothers in Cuba an idea of what happened to the millions of Muslims who then came to Mecca.

 

 

 

Following the Tuesday and Wednesday included rest and food necessary for our bodies and visits to places like museums, in particular to the institution where the kiswa is done (48) or fabric that covers the Kaaba. Two days to get to know other brothers, receive the support of several of them and feel the love of some very distant in geography and culture, but nearby in the tenderness that emanates from the heart. It was the case of Muslims in Serbia and Kosovo that traveled together as part of one group and that not neglected its care for us, the Cuban pilgrims. Many we wondered about Cuba, their situation, of the economy, they wanted to know first hand and through our words that really happened in our country, specific about Islam and Muslims Cuban on the island. They were hours that we really enjoyed, they gave us their vision of a war in the early 1990s I lastro the Muslim population of those territories, learning over time to extract the best of experiences for the future. During the dialogue the words and the eyes of those brothers were clean despite Recalling violent years, moments of death and desolation.

 

 

 

They were thus exploited the daylight hours, intense heat on the street, but very nice in the interior of the hotel. Evenings were dedicated to visit the holy site of the Kaaba and then buy some simple gifts in the souks (49) close to the mosque, that even at dawn he kept open. During these purchases, we visited a small shop, Call Center Al - Khalij, located in Al, on the main street and next to the Elaf al - Mashaer. This place would be frequented by me more sometimes even become must-see to greet the clerk, a Yemeni in Sana'a which became friend. There buy nuts for my parents, perfumes to give to friends in Cuba and in particular a perfume of roses for my mother and brothers, to know our friend from Yemen that was for her gave me the same. The logic of its action was not based on business, It actually gave me two products for the price of one, their intension was looking for special blessings that any pious person receives when it makes actions by parents of others, again I remembered José Martí.

 

 

 

Mine, Arafat, Muzdalifa.

 

 

 

Leaving most of our belongings in Mecca, we headed to mine, Arafat and Muzdalifa as our next stop. These sites gave us many experiences from which I extract some much unattended to the order in which occurred or the hierarchy of importance, rather as the heart remembers them, with tenderness and simplicity. Prophet Muhammad transmitted to ” Arafat is the Hajj ” (50) Hence the importance of staying in that place, a plain to which needed to travel from the city. We arrived at night on the bus number 12 that was our being in the days of Mecca. Big tents, tens of meters of length and width, with all the facilities, including air conditioning, they amazed me much because we were in the middle of the desert. There we enjoyed all the same comforts of the hotel of Mecca, the hundreds of meters of green on the sand mats printed environment a somewhat unreal vision, as the color given to a drawing on the whim of a toddler. Most importantly this place weren't the comforts of our program as guests of the King or the surrounding beauty, but the examination of conscience of those who were there. More beyond the limits of our camp million Muslims moved or rested, they prayed to leaning to the Almighty or raised their hands in supplication, they ate what they had to recharge or purified for the next prayer, actions United forming the structure and dynamics of a single act of worship of the one God who deserves it. More than once I saw appear to Yahya who was amazed at the edges of the camp, at the sight of the hundreds of thousands of people who have not ceased to move to and from all directions.

 

 

 

It was beautiful to see the Cuban flag next to other countries, indicating all the place where should rest pilgrims who joined in a number close to the one hundred and of 10 o 15 Nations on average in each of the big stores. It was also our flag, in the midst of the others, in the thousands of schemes scattered at every moment and in every place, in the maps indicating the details of the Hajj of 2014. It was the flag of our fathers, Vicente Aguilera, one of the richest of the island in the 19th century give it all to the independence of Cuba, the flag of lawns, Martí and the General Maceo. The flag of Juan Gualberto Gómez, Bonifacio Byrne and Julian del Casal. That has lived in the hearts anonymous of many sons of Cuba who no matter where they are not forget their homeland, and God knows what they contain hearts. The flag of Alejo Carpentier, Dulce María Loynaz and Guillermo Cabrera Infante, Jose Antonio Portuondo and Leví Marrero. The Antonio Guiteras and José Antonio Echeverría. Of millions of Cubans than in the streets of Havana, of Santiago de Cuba and other cities of the world unite with their steps day hours. The flag of our country and of all, all Cubans.

 

 

 

The Friday 3 October was a bit difficult. I saw last night a yellow mist in which repair only as part of the magical vision of the place, in my attempt and desire to sublimate every moment, each experience. Then I knew that was sand very fine suspended to dozens or hundreds of meters high, but that occasionally approached to the surface entering our lungs as part of respiration. I was useful to take some pills of an antihistamine called Loratadine, that is good because it does not sleep and that I had given my father as a preventative if it was necessary to, but in the desert of Saudi Arabia that prevent almost saved me. I was still hours lying down, trying to replace me. At noon my brother Ibrahim reminds me that it is part of the Sunnah (51) or tradition of the Prophet Muhammad listen to the sermon or speech on Friday during the stay in Arafat, then we are looking for a group of brothers that included this speech in English, but we did not find any. Half an hour later we sat among a score of Muslims from Kosovo by listening to the speech in their native language, without understanding a single word, but connected with the spirit of the Group and once more welcomed with tenderness by Muslims in this nation.

 

 

 

But Arafat showed the highest expression at the time of the concentration of people, Muzdalifa surpassed it, being surpassed in turn by Mina. The intensity, increasing, the crowd destroyed the logic. Where so many people came out and where would, endlessly for days and through hundreds of roads and highways, travelling thousands of kilometres, It simply posed a challenge to the sensible apprehension of the possibility itself. In Muzdalifa, we stopped for a while, We ate there, We pray and we collected small stones which we blaze as part of the symbolic stoning of Shaitan (52). It was dark while taking some pictures to my about, on occasions such as repetitive and mechanical action before both fatigue and so much pain in my feet. The reality of a pilgrimage had materialized in us, in the middle of it, fatigue and constant interaction, my heart began to be understood all through the use of another language, immediate, He knows instantly. Many aspects that were once important lost its meaning to form part of another concatenation. The design had changed.

 

 

 

In mine we lost. Ibrahim lost is the only, For more than two hours walk dozens of kilometres, among millions of people, in a different direction to that take the other four members of the Group, to move in a circle until no more power. We were convinced that it was necessary to ask for help. Samir repairs on the poster of a store where you could read in English ” Guide to lost Pilgrim Center “. There we try to for half an hour to find the best references and advise at the same time the best variants Ibrahim that communicated frequently using modern mobile phones given to each as part of the program of the pilgrims. An hour later and thanks to the guidance of God and the assigned guides we found our camp and rescatábamos Ibrahim. In that State, completely exhausted, see the flag once again propagate pride. Eat, pray, sleep, It had concluded another day.

 

 

 

Three pillars indicate places in mine, collected by tradition, in which the Prophet Abraham expelled to Shaitan lapping it and preventing it to deflect it from what the Almighty, the God of all worlds, He had told, i.e., obey him. Before Abraham, starting from Adam, and after it, concluding with Muhammad as the seal of prophecy, all messengers were repositories of a same treasure, the knowledge that God had not created the humans except that they worship him, in an act of pure worship, without associating anything. We imitate the Prophet which was called the friend of God, Khalil, and as Abraham did, We make releases. We would thus advancing in the completion of our religion.

 

 

 

The next morning I find the Sheikh Fawaz who talked in a discreet corner with a pilgrim of Africa. When he saw me he asks me how everything goes, I mean you and me congratulates, It tells me that I shave my head and tells me that it was already in terms of common clothes, start to experience a strange joy. Minutes later did the tail because several Muslim brothers were waiting for the same. The Barber, a man of about forty years, set you fire to carrier blades, as a hygiene measure, between one and another client, In addition to using disposable blades for shaving. Samir and Yamal, sick of the flu, they spent most of the time lying in our room, that it was visited several times a day by camp physicians. They were slowly recovering, fevers and drugs that were not being able to read their names in Arabic. To mine they were to see us and greet us with several people that the Cubans knew of us through the press and television. Among them I remember fondly a man of stature coming from Gaza, Palestine, We seek only to give us a hug.

 

 

 

Once again we were leaving behind part of our baggage to recover them later in Medina. Tradition stated walking out from Mina to Mecca, throwing small stones at the three places again to then take the same path that the Prophet Muhammad used. At the end of the third pillar, we abandoned mine through inclined paths, between large Rocky Mountains and forming part of a vast human tide. The same rocks that had observed our Prophet thousands of years ago, they gave us his greeting in silence, and the Sun, the crowd and the pain of our feet already broken by the dryness of the skin – still wearing shoes -, We are entering Mecca.

 

 

 

The farewell.

 

 

 

There is no excuse for the pilgrims, men and women, leave Mecca without first making the last visit to the Kaaba and give the farewell ritual turns. Only women in the time of their menstruation or after childbirth are allowed to leave the city without fulfilling this requirement. Before visiting the sacred enclosure we take two hours to buy some earrings gifts. Ibrahim and I economize and thus acquire a few suitcases for the trip. We perform these steps walking which worsened the situation of my feet. Completed the purchase, we returned to the hotel and without wasting time I went out with Samir and Ibrahim for the mosque, Once again walk, increasing my pain, forcing me to support my steps in unnatural way that I helped at times, but eventually that my state out worse.

 

 

 

We did a waiting, sitting on the floor of the outdoor Esplanade of the mosque when I insist once again and call the technician Abdul Rashid, a man of the India based in Jeddah who had visited Havana months before. I can communicate and we both wonder, the my presence in Mecca and I know, through it, My sister Muslim Marilu, as always, I have called it with love, I was there on pilgrimage. I couldn't believe it, a Cuban woman receiving the same blessings to us and had arrived in Mecca before. God showed once again that it is merciful and fair. Minutes later we talked about it and I, with shared a joy and a surprise, were not even after 20 minutes of phone conversation. It was not possible to see us there, at that time she remained entered with asthma at a clinic, but on the return trip, flying between Moscow and Havana, We share impressions for a few minutes.

 

 

 

Then I went into the House of God for the last time. The first seven turns, then the prayer near the site of Abraham, then travel the distance between Safa and Marwa seven times. During these travels and my pain was unbearable, in the third of them Samir and Ibrahim, helping me on each side, I endured while this will change my situation. Ibrahim suggested me to take a little bit of Zamzam water but I tell her I have no thirst, the insists. Do you case, more to change a little bit and really relax. Approach me batteries that provide this spring water contact of my feet with the freshness of the water that fell to the ground completely provides pain relief me in minutes, all that remained was strong annoyance from a wound in the back of the right foot but the intense pain of the soles of both feet had disappeared. Prophet Muhammad taught that the water of Zamzam can become what you need. I could end the four remaining routes. Half an hour later and thanks to God and his mercy we had completed our religion, smiling we embrace and happily add to our names in Islam the word Hajji front, from that moment we would be Hajji Ibrahim, Hajji Isa and Hajji Samir.

 

 

 

Medina.

 

 

 

The oasis of Yathrib had played host to the Prophet Muhammad in the beginning of the Hegira (53) with joy and peace which continues to receive to all who come to this city. Shortly after the arrival of Muhammad began to call him ” the city of the Prophet ” to stay, resulting from continued use, with the qualifier of ” the city “, in Arabic ” Madina “so today the whole world known as Medina. Between Mecca and this oasis there is a little more than 500 kilometers we went in a few hours and buses. On the way we stopped to eat, pray and relax a few minutes. The Grand Mercure, only one block from the mosque of the Prophet – which constitutes the second most important sacred place of Islam after the sacred enclosure of the Kaaba in Mecca – gave us a special welcome. There existed a different speed, Mecca is a centrifuge of love, Medina, a cloud of peace.

The modern and huge umbrellas which provide shade in the external areas of the Medina mosque reminded me once again, in his image, the aesthetics of a child. Even watching them closely seemed unreal, tens of meters in perimeter and its high did each of these structures perishes to be moments of take-off of the ground. I am sure that Leonardo da Vinci had been muted against them. Night is closed to become masts indicating all the direction towards the starry sky or infinity. Inside the mosque thousands of columns created as a whole forms most beautiful dance. That all rugs, lamps, colors, the doors, lights and shadows in unison sang praises to the God of peace, to the only one that exists, called by their names in all languages and invoked by all hearts, even for those who are not aware of this. Pray there was the first impulse, then approach the tomb of Prophet Muhammad and provide not only my greetings but all Muslims and all Muslims Cuban greeting. Ask God for blessings to Prophet Muhammad, his family and to all the prophets, and ask that we always guide you to the straight path, they were pleas recurring within me. Many images, many memories and many people were returning to my mind. Awareness of numerous incorrect actions of the past demanded my person scrutiny and dialogue with my Lord.

 

 

 

During the stay in Medina visited the King Fahd complex for the printing of the Holy Qur'an which is dedicated to this important work makes 30 years. This complex produces 160 versions of Qur'anic text in different languages and more than 275 millions of copies of the Quran. Print also millions of copies of the biography of the Prophet Muhammad and produces audio recordings of known methods of recitation, promoting different fields of Koranic studies and supporting research and Islamic studies in Saudi Arabia and other countries. There we observed the process of printing of the Qur'an and receive copies of this book in Arabic and in his translation of sense to the Spanish.

 

 

 

The Quba mosque was another of the places visited. Known as the mosque of the two qiblahs, must its name to the fact that this place indicated to the Prophet Muhammad by God pray towards Mecca, until that time the Muslims prayed facing Jerusalem. Led by an impulse of curiosity I put in place one of the most innocent of my life questions, It occurred to me to find out where was the previous quibla inside the mosque, a brother answered me tenderly as possible that the first had been destroyed, the quibla (54) the Muslims was a single, towards Mecca.

I took advantage of the peace of Medina to go a little in the world of television stations in the Gulf. Could be enjoyed in the hotel room around 30 from channels of Egypt, Sudan, Morocco, Syria, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman and other nearby Nations in addition to the BBC in Arabic and in English. I also spent hours talking with Muslim brothers from other countries such as Finland, Comoros, Viet Nam, Libya and Palestine. Approaching the game by what indagábamos about the details, our flight schedules, how and where, everything you need. Before leaving the city of the Prophet, just a few minutes before, We received a few special gifts as part of the courtesies towards the hundreds of guests of the King that there we were. This group of present to every one of us was in a prayer rug with the logo of the program that had invited us, an exquisite perfume and a container with five liters of water of Zamzam. With this final detail our bus headed to the international airport in Madina, the 11 October of 2014, then Jeddah, Dubai, Moscow and Havana, just under three days of return and with so many anecdotes that they would duplicate the extension of this count only tries to share some of the impressions of a wonderful journey in its essence, as the same that have made millions of Muslims for thousands of years to reach Mecca and say ” Mr, I am here “.
