una joya occulta, libro de un adelantado

Categories:

The Tower of Babel

Por Jesús Sama Pacheco.

El libro rojo, de Guillermo Rodríguez Rivera (Santiago de Cuba, 1943), es uno de esos libros raros, poco comunes, que al leerlos, junto a la admiración que provocan, nos dejan un grupo de interrogantes, muchas veces incontestables. Written in the aftermath of one of the stages more you convulsed in Cuban literature, It has remained unpublished for more than forty years, until its recent publication thanks to the commendable work of the SurEditores collection of the Union of writers and artists of Cuba, directed by the poet Alex Pausides.

The book contains eleven texts covering just sixty pages. Poems, made in free verse, they are a hybrid of poetry and essay, within which underlie exponents of the note, the report, the interview and the Chronicle newspaper, it make it unique. In this sense, the author communicates to us: It is a fusion of genres that has a correspondence with changes of typography within poems. It was the way for me to overcome the conversational and the anti-poesia, not going to previous poetry but advancing to the future. It was the way that seemed consistent with that claim to "say everything" which is expressed in poetic art. (1)

Written between 1968 and 1969, It was a finalist for the Casa de las Américas Prize in 1970. It said the essayist and critic Jorge Luis Arcos:

ES El libro rojo Guillermo Rodríguez Rivera poetry which gives a very unique place in the history of Cuban poetry of the revolution. This book is its deepest creation and original...

It is here where the poet deploys his definitive voice. The happy and difficult verb for the poet and essayist makes of El libro rojo one of the most important time of Cuban poetry in any time testimonies. Is this book that deserves to speak of a poetry of history.

But to speak of this book today and assess it rightly - as advises arches in the preface to Canta (2)–, an approach to the stage in which it was created is necessary. Do not forget the testimonial sense of Cuban poetry, in general, and this book, in particular.

Other aspects to be considered are related to the form and content of the book. The stage in which it is written was dominated by the current of the anti-Poesia and colloquial style. I think that the book closest to this, in the formal, ES The daily daily, Nicolás Guillén; Although published in 1972, After writing El libro rojo from Guillermo. But nothing to do with one another and everything seems to be in tune with the mere coincidence. Another creation with certain formal features such is the poem"I explain to the reader why not all concluded that poem on the commune", Roberto Fernández Retamar, appearing in 1971; but, just as in the Daily daily, no direct link to the book by Guillermo.

In terms of content, still a clear, the author of El libro rojo distance of all his predecessors and contemporaries. On the other hand, We can say without fear of being wrong, Guillermo Rodríguez Rivera comes to the novísimos and other writers that later lead to his poetry a critical content of today within the context of the revolution. However, before imminent and overwhelming censure of the five years or decade grey, and the excesses of some officials and opportunistic beings, the author of El libro rojo It calls on the President of UNEAC, in 1972, out of the plan of the Editorial Union - words of Guillermo - before that he had to do it the own Guillén. (3)

If there is any influence, would have to think in Chose to Jesus Menendez (1951), Nicolás Guillén; in Hour zero (1960), Ernesto Cardenal; or in Tavern, Roque Dalton, appearing in 1969, When already has Guillermo written his book.

We must also think about the age that Guillermo was then, just twenty-five years. Old enough to write a good book of poems - an enduring book, as it is the case-; or discover the Law of relativity, How did Einstein. More, no age is good for the prediction of future events. If Guillermo kind-hearted from the first verses of the book to the express: “.. .viendo from atop the cars in Havana, / the destroyed cars, as beasts, / they wallow in the streets of Havana"; / is because I could not imagine, how nor no other mortal would imagine, now, more than forty years later, the streets of Havana would continue along those same cars (the country's roads) in the hands of one of the wealthiest groups of today. And those same vehicles - go paradox!–, as the old private trucks, they have assisted, and they help even, the State and the people in the titanic struggle for these times.. Nobody I could not imagine that many of the difficulties, inadequacies and errors then, referred by Guillermo in his book, they transcend to our days.

And is that El libro rojo from Guillermo (not of Mao-Tse-Tung), at times, seems to have been written right now, or that he is writing while we breathe or retraced our steps along the streets, anxious, in search of our bread of every day or the solution giving a definitive response to the sacrifice and heroism of a people who have played all their cards to the future. To that future than, conscious about e unconscious other, they have opted for a whole continent.

It might seem at times irreverent, pessimistic and even dissenting, When we said in the first poem (Poetic art): “I, reading a newspaper from four pages almost no news, / so alien." But it is, first and foremost, a text born of honesty, the wisdom and talent of a young man who managed to locate this book on the highest rungs of Cuban poetry of all time; a prestigious intellectual than, Despite all the setbacks and the ostracism they remained poems for more than forty years, It has managed to create a solid work as a poet, Narrator, essayist and critic.

For a proper reading of the book, I think, not only is enough an unprejudiced look, wide, criticism - in the best sense of the word-; but also, the deep analysis, the magnetisation of the senses to capture every image, each suggestion, the constructive essence of the message. ES, first and foremost, a book with a philosophical background, human and deeply revolutionary, It leaves out any suspicion, all mean-spirited thesis, all prison.

In the second poem (History of Cuba) verifies the intention of the author to want to leave us a testimony of time, a lucid appreciation of how it must look at the events and its protagonists. At the beginning of the poem tells us: “Here, from the dark depths of the stones / the city where I was born, story history."And later: “and this city (…) crumbling."And towards the end: “It is not necessary to darning new lies / nor shore up a myth with the remains of fighters. / We will not endiosar our fathers..." And time has given him the reason repeatedly, When to the top leaders of the country have adopted an attitude of self-criticism and not only it has been necessary to demystify the heroes, but also the Revolution itself.

In fact we have a book with many edges, with a worldview of the time you write. If critics of the book in the past were not able to, before accuse, wondering why people so visionary, intellectuals of left of the stature of Cintio Vitier, Ernesto Cardenal, Roque Dalton, Margare Randall and Washington Delgado, distinguished El libro rojo hundreds of original, at least, they had read it correctly. Yes; There is a critical look, an unprejudiced feeling, honestly - no punches - our reality. But there is also a patriotic feeling, membership, letters patent of nobility, It goes beyond all suspicion. And that can be seen all along the book.

In the poem titled"Close to Viet Nam" us says:

“…We are at war. In New York City, in Paris,

people are eating the world trembles

viewing photographs: Hanoi pumps

they are falling on the rocks of La Habana."

Precisely that verse me forwards to Fidel's speech at the burial to the martyrs of Barbados, to say: “When an energetic and virile people crying, injustice trembles."And almost at the end of that poem, When Guillermo tells us: "We will make birth another world”, I can't look at everything that happened in Cuba and on the continent during the last two decades.

Is can not ignore that we we have a poet connoisseur of philosophy and history; and also, Psychology and human behavior. As a student and as a race of Humanities teacher, the author of El libro rojo He had studied all the philosophers, from antiquity to the time in which he writes the book. Karl Marx and Federico Engels are, symbolically, co-authors of the ideas that are expressed in it. And what about Che, Marti and other masters of the truth and the patriotic sentiment that transpire in these verses. I also see Fidel when you told us: We can only lose our chains. It would suffice to quote a couple of verses of poems"To conjure the State" and"Looking forward to the surdo”. In the first we read:

You who you just begin to jump the great wall

And in the second (Looking forward to the surdo), It begins with a quote from Carl Marx which seems to allude to errors and negative trends, verified throughout the Cuban revolutionary process - anterior and posterior to the writing of the book - and still suffer, Reza: “Every right involves the application of a single rule to different men. As a result, the same law amounts to a violation of equality, to an injustice."And in the text of the poem - and reminding us to Mariana Grajales", the parents of Martí, Celia, of the hermanos Saiz, and many, many Cubans and Cuban and, Why not, known and anonymous beings from around the world-, humanist of the verses you can see the character. And once again Ernesto Che Guevara has bloomed to our memory when Guillermo tells us:

“When the new man appears,

When to put your feet on the road

from the day in which the memory was born

toured the heroes,

Remember to the simple, complicated, defective

human beings who gave him life.”

As it is possible to appreciate, the entire book is testimony. The entire book, more than a meeting, It is the purest and most consequential poetry relationship and life with the historical moment in which the poet deploys his voice. There is no affiliation with trends or fads. There are lyrical moments and other empowered by the word nude, for the scintillating verse, the scream loudly, the most sincere expression of the aching reality. Poetry of complaint, of full externalisation of feelings and the reason that the poet, Dark uncompromising, flying as a sword. For this reason, in the poem"Personal problems"us says:

A"Personal problems"this is followed by a poem that will not have death: “The Minister, the poet”. First, because here the Minister is a symbol representing the administration of the State, the entity that enacts the laws and enforces them. Which, even disappearing State, would be in the hands of the technocracy, which could just disappear with the end of the human species, so given to organize and create institutions as it is known since the emergence of primitive societies. And second, because the poet will always be the poet, and will always be on guard as a collective voice.

I shall confine myself to quote only some fragments: “Anyone willing to raise the jib of the poet”. And two stanzas later: “Of course it is much riskier to raise the jib of the Minister”. As can be seen, Guillermo tells us things I knew, expressions that others do not dare nor to meditating aloud, but who these days buy enormous brightness and a dimension, When some beyond custom attack attempt today to raise the jib to poetry as a genre. It would be, by its historical and social connotation, as drown the lyricism of a people, leaving an aesthetically stutterers era, disinherited images that express your beat and deepest feelings.

And if we look at with all the honesty that the word and the time required, the poem - like others of the book - was premonitory. They had only passed some months the publication of eight of the poems of the book in the anthology"Six poets", edited by Casa de las Américas, and someone already raised the jib of the poet. Which was multiplied in apocalyptic manner during the grey. But Guillermo was not only the Guinea pig extremism reached with his sights and claws. THE poem"The harvest"it is certainly a portrait of time, true allegory to a page of our history that will not be possible to find so much transparency and authenticity in the official books written with the aim, often breached or fulfilled half, pick up the story for the knowledge of generations. The poem is a kind of big story, synthesized by the expressive way of poetry, that attend various appointments, other journalistic genres and even a small Epistle brushstrokes.
