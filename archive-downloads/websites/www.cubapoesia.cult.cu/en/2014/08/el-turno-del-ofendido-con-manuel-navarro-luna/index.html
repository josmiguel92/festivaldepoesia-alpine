Navarro Moon, poet of his time and his people »

Aug 29 2014

the shift of the offended with navarro manuel luna

Categories:

the shift of the offended

By María de los Angeles Polo Vega.

The revolutionary poet Manuel Navarro Luna was born the 29 August of 1894 and a 120 years after its birth the Havana International Festival of poetry of pays homage with the presentation of a volume edited by SurEditores collection, collecting all the poetic work of the Bard manzanilla and has, as value added critical opinions of leading intellectuals from Cuba and other parts of the world, issued in the moments in which each of those books came to light, as well as other texts theirs that remained unpublished until now.

An extraordinary book, of more than 500 pages, by way of preface brings the words pronounced Roberto Fernández Retamar on the occasion of the transfer of his remains to the city of Manzanillo and which is subsequently published in the number 14 the magazine revolution and culture, in 1973, and expressing, in the opinion of the editors, with absolute today the right dimension of the great poet who is Manuel Navarro Moon.

In this poetic anthology, useful to scholars of literature, We found valuations of Bonifacio Byrne, Raúl Roa, Juan Marinello, Enrique José Varona, Nicolás Guillén and Pablo Neruda, just quote some names.

Is Manuel Navarro Moon one of the most important civil poets of the 20th century and his book Groove, of 1927 He is considered the first modern book of our literature. His prophetic and immensely humane tone has peers in León Felipe, Vladimir Mayakovsky and Aimé Césaire, says in its cover note.

Navarro Moon was a devoted Communist and by this ideology suffered from jails and marginalization in the difficult years of the Republic, He said our Nicolás Guillén in the far 1949 "There's the modernist Navarro of the early days, and the vanguard of progress times, and the vast social concerns of our times and all of them make a single true Navarro; the universal poet, telluric, built of own and others blood, and for whom many people outside Cuba know that in our brief territory there is a small port called Manzanillo, from where his great voice sounds on America".

And later, on the occasion of the commemoration of the first anniversary of its physical disappearance, the own Guillén expressed:"Although the years pass and from clumping in centuries, This voice will resonate impetuous. It will mark one of the deepest of Cuban poetry and also higher moments. "In that correlation between what is abyss and apex is poetry who was not only a great artist but a great man".

With more than 30 missing years of our libraries, again Luna Navarro, Thanks to the southern editors of the Havana International Festival of poetry of collection to be available to new generations of Cuban readers, social and energetic but also delicate and intimist poet, that he dedicated verses to the homeland, its heroes and its history, the mother, the father, the wife and daughter, to your friends, and for whom nothing human was alien.

The poems in this anthology are Mourners rhythms of 1919, Heart inside of 1922, Refuge of 1927, Groove of 1928, (considered the initiator of the avant-garde in the Cuban literature), Pulse and wave of 1932, Earth wound of 1936, their Poems Mambises of 1943, Tenths of 1949, So, written in 1949 and never published in life of the author, until at 2005 SurEditores did in coedition with the Ortho of the city of Manzanillo editorial, their THREE songs to life, also of 1949,Father poems, of 1950;its Chose to Dona Martina in 1951, tenths devoted to his mother, the generous woman who lived more than one hundred years.

We are also with The our father, written poem the 28 in January of 1953 published in the magazine Bohemia in February of 1953, dedicated to José Martí for its centenary and which wrote Rafael Soto peace "it is perhaps, the only thing large, the only thing worth the Maestro in this Centenary full of shadows and, above all, so much hypocrisy".

This poetic anthology of Manuel Navarro Moon includes also its SERENADES from 1955, dedicated to his daughter Ana Navarro Lauten, songs that as said Joaquin G. Santana, "They seem to madrigals. They are true madrigals.Rarely a lyrics resists being printed. They are usually written to be heard and not read. These however, It is clear by the poetic than them, they can withstand this trial by fire".

And as it is presumably could not miss his poems Mambises of 1959, where as Cintio Vitier, “Is Cuba whole, vibrating powerfully upright..."to conclude its Mambises Odes, its militants Odes and other poems that, as we said at the beginning of this comment, they remained unpublished until now.

Sample as we share some of his poems.

 

Socialism

(1915)

It is vain to fight: Sum rot
all invades it and it corrupts everything.
If the sea does not form in the ribera foam
is because the Bank has mud.

The stupidity of thereby prevails
that truth vanishes into his presence.
That fight is in a bend
Road, routing through the mist.

While humanity does not renew it,
While at the hunger for other aleve
the theme man as a black abyss,

will not boost his victorious banner,
its splendid bermeja flag,
the Supreme Ideal of socialism

 

Santiago de Cuba

(1957)

(Perhaps the best known of his poems)

Let the dead bury
their dead

It's Santiago de Cuba!
Not you surprised of nothing!

There goes the mother of heroes!
By Mariana anda!!
You'll be blind
If you do not see nor feel its strong and in-depth look…!
You'll be deaf if you will not hear his footsteps;
If you do not hear his tremendous Word!

"Outside! Out of here!
Not anymore tears!”

So he cried that day, next to the body of Antonio
-by Antonio, nothing less, He was bleeding
mortally wounded!-When all
women there moaned and cried…!

"Outside! Out of here!
Not anymore tears!”

It's Santiago de Cuba!
Not you surprised of nothing!

There mothers shine
as stars injured and bereaved.
They collected the body of their children
felled by bullets mercenary,
and, then, in the flame of the funeral,
they were singing the hymn of the homeland.

Also they were going, next to them,
the heart, without sleep, Mariana…!

"Outside! Out of here!
Not anymore tears!”

There are deaths that, Although dead, they are not at their funerals;
There are deaths that do not fit in the closed graves
and break them, in and out, with their bones knives,
to continue war in battle…!

Only buried the dead its dead!
But ever the country buries them!
Homeland alive, eternal,
not never bury their own bowels…!

It's Santiago de Cuba!
Not you surprised of nothing!

Mothers eyes are dry
as rivers without water!
Are dry the eyes of all women!
They are scorched by anger
that you are hearing the cry
heroic Mariana:

"Outside! Out of here!
Not anymore tears!”

Come! Come, clarines!
Come! Come, campaigns!
Come, fire lilies,
to greet your own flames roses!

 

Verses.

Navarro Moon is your name
and this revolution,
you are the pure song
and the voice which proclaims the.

Man of exemplary lineage,
in your radiant universe
is the dimension of the verse
your odd sincerity.

Owner of clear paths,
of mountains and rivers,
you never felt cold
in your real war.

You sing about freedom
as neither in your land
and that is why Sierra
eternity comes.

The auroras which provided for
your pupils and your songs,
they have already defeated the brokenness
and they flourished in a hymn.

The waters of your friendship
they are never murky neither cold,
and I have not seen yet
greater generosity.

Surrounded by youth,
hopes and longings,
you've known the sleepless
in sowing of cleanliness.

May not no dagger
hurt you by the side,
because you're the faithful soldier
a light that will not turn off.

The life you've lived
like a polished star,
is to die for her
with good upright chest.

May the years pass
and your breath succumb,
more you're route to follow
and light to walk.

 

Our father

(28 in January of 1953)

Generous Homagno,
Our father...!
Fine homeland,
transparent and radiant;
the nation United and strong,
without masters and yokes;
crystalline and pure homeland;
honda homeland,
honored,
prosperous,
happy,
respected
and beloved…
Which forged the blood and the metal of your dream…!
“The homeland with all and for the good of all”…
We will have it one day…!
Yes, Father, will have it...!
We will have it…!
It still works your blood;
even the heartbeat of your thinking
They resound in the shade revealed;
still feel the fire
of your spirit opening yearning grooves;
Yet the campaign of your brow,
with the opulent voice of its lightning,
an accomplice of silence undermines the blackness
rotten and dark; even your burning Word
crosses as a flurry of Suns,
as the swollen and firm steps of wind;
as a tumult of free flags;
as a young clarines flame;
as a resonant dawn of libertadores steels…!

Father,
Our father…!

Everything you see,
what you are seeing,
all the rot that is displayed to your eyes;
the infernal carrion trailing living;
not to be the star because it lights up and kills;
but the yoke to yoke the yoke
because with him enjoying; the miserable bodies
that escaping star you betray it
increasingly more sunken in infamy. Blacks
banners of the mud; the “rich and wide oats
and the hot straw”…The heinous ways
he erected the conscience of the shadow and crime…
all this
You'll see it shot down by irate star,
on the shoulders of your people in…!
The same star that shone on your brow
already you of foot in the yoke,
Our father!
In front of the Red,
open and deep wound of your dream;
against the pain in which black ditches
the nobles banners down
like wings destroyed, the chest rises
infinite light even driving your front
burning rings of death, the fences
evil and the shame…We'll see
knocked over the shadows; and the vile,
that sore homeland sank
in disgrace and in the mud, all
they will burn in the flame, in the terrible fire
it pours out the star,
Our father…!
These Livid columns of crying;
These pools of horror where the ragged
children are playing with tears;
This homeland with these skinny men and masters,
(farmers who are waiting, only, the injustice
that is to come, within, to throw them to the Middle
roads, Our father…! (So many libertadores
to those who has stolen their land abroad…!

These roses of horror;
These cold slopes of hunger,
and contempt
own siblings, the Black Brothers…!
As if anyone could be larger than Antonio,
the great captain
who to tip of courage, shame and blood
Homeland delivered, for in it
They planted their heartbeat whites and blacks…!

Behind this vast darkness;
the cup of horror, of the horrendous
landscapes of death, the monster rises
that your eyes saw
and whose heart you lived...!The monster
imperialist choking towns
disunited and weak, delivered by accomplices
invader, sold to the thirty pieces of silver
Judas received! Our homeland
betrayed, dying on the cross;
but you know that you have not left it
and that your own voice and your own hard
they are in the final battle, While your temples
delirious illuminate the redemptive path.
Father!
Our father…!
What long this agony…!
What serious and deep sufferings…!
What bloody fall...! What cowardly betrayal…!
What grim snares and what devious hits…!
What wells and what infernal foams…!
What shadows and silt flows…!
But on the blood, on pain, about death,
with our own sore breasts
and aided by ti, standing in the yoke
We raise the homeland, We raise it,
already free of misery and betrayal,
above all the dark stalking;
forged by your blood that guides us;
by hope, on edges, of your dream;
forged by your endless light…
Generous Homagno!
Our father!
