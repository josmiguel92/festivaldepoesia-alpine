Introduction to the panel by the centennial of Samuel Feijóo

By: Alpidio Alonso Grau

Source: CUBARTE

Readers who love the work of Samuel Feijóo We should be grateful to the international book fair the possibility to be able to celebrate the centenary of the poet as part of his programme of activities; scenario that allows you to resize the extent of the tribute that this great figure of our literature and our culture all deserve.

Well looks things, few times it was as relevant to celebrate in a space like that leads to this fair, animated by a truly popular spirit - which claims that condition of shared celebration from your own design, free of any kind of elitism which can move it away from the roots of people that distinguishes it from similar in other parts of the world events-, the centenary of a poet, of a great artist, whose legacy rests right on that link, and whose discoveries in the intellectual order are the result of a completely organic relationship with the creative genius of the people, rather than the, Perhaps like no one else, could see.

It would seem that even the natural environment that offers this scenario, populated by trees and plenty of outdoor spaces, agree with the intention of paying homage to the tireless Walker of our fields, that - powered our rural landscape sensitivity of these wanderings, and in contact with humble beings that inhabit these places-, left us a work that, at the time that credits a heartbreaking subjective integration effort, It is an infinite trip to nature and man soaked with the gifts of your environment.

At the same time, I think it's very fair that we celebrate in style this Centenary. Deserves it not only Feijoo, but they need this. And they deserve it and they need readers, both those who know it, as those who now (heat-what to do) they will discover it. In that balance of rediscoveries and revelations that raises, they are the profound meaning and the usefulness of this type of Jubilee. In general, around the closed anniversaries of a writer, his works are reedit, discussed and analyzed their books, sometimes, even, published texts that had been unreleased for years, that reveal new areas and expand the orbit of what until that moment knew of his work..., forms all call the attention of readers and specialists about the values and the place which ultimately occupy this book and this author in specific context.

In this case, an elementary sense of Justice and responsibility (I say not only artistic) leads us to recognize the need to reposition the work and figure of Samuel Feijóo in the field of our artistic and literary creation and assess their work as one of the greatest and most original spiritual realizations that met the Cuban 20th century. Little is not what we should still do for that purpose. Taking advantage of its prestige and resonance, the fair can contribute considerably to this, not only helping to reposition to Feijoo among our readers, but doing everything possible to give you a continental projection, as his work deserves it.

I feel that we are very far from have you done justice not only to the poet, "to the lyric par excellence", as called for by Cintio, owner of a monumental poetry of hondisimas human resonances. It is necessary to assess a multilateral work that extended also to the tale, the novel, literary criticism, Theatre, research in diverse fields and the trial (What would have to join their magnificent reflective cartoons of Frank lyrical disposition and his diaries), all genres (along with the multiple translations of English and other languages) in which their contributions are still waiting for an estimate that monitor them and give the hierarchy they deserve.

Also, his performance in the plastic arts, as an artist, painter, Illustrator and photographer, inseparable in turn of a phenomenon as singular as the movement of popular artists that contributed to articulate in the former Las Villas province - with so many points of contact with the cutting edge and, by the same, so different from the rest of the so-called modern primitive painting; absolutely live and with multiple exponents, especially in Villa Clara-, they are clamouring for a greater dissemination and study among us.

Separate chapter would deal with his legacy as the editor, magazine and organizer of companies associated with the promotion and dissemination of the book and literature, field in which you let us, simply do not find second to none in our culture. It would be enough just to review some (just a few) titles that make up the extensive catalogue of what, starting from 1958, It was published under its auspices as remained at the head of the Department of publications of the Universidad Central "Marta Abreu", of the Villas, to realize the dimension of this work. Let us stop, even a moment, to think that at the time represented (and what then were for Cuban culture) the appearances of the argument between Cuban tobacco and sugar and history of a Cuban fight against demons, Fernando Ortiz; Havana Chronicles, Julián de el Casal; of poems I have and the prose in a hurry, Nicolás Guillén; return to the dawn and skirmish on the eve, Raúl ROA; the extraordinary treaties in Havana, of José Lezama Lima; Idea of stylistics and stationery, Roberto Fernández Retamar; Bufo Theatre, first compilation of works of this theatrical form that (from the effort of a group of authors) saw the light in our country; of the book of poetry blasts, Rolando Escardó, poet much admired Feijoo and that always recognized in debt; of Cuba in poetry, of Cintio Vitier; martianos trials, American meditation, Contemporary and eight notes Aníbal Ponce, of Juan Marinello; Tales of appeared, of José Seoane Gallo (that another great researcher that shared interests and searches); Memoirs of a cubanita that was born with the century, Renee Méndez Capote's; José Antonio Saco's. Study and biography, authored by Manuel Moreno Fraginals; from José de la Luz y Caballero as educator, of Medardo Vitier; or townspeople and the Narrator, Onelio Jorge Cardoso, which also celebrated its centenary in this figure 2014. What would have to add your essential and multiple compilations of traditional popular culture and various personal anthologies of his poetry.

What say, on the other hand, pawnshops as the incarnated in Islands magazine(1958) and signs(1969), marked down to the minutest detail by the boldness and the mood of his remarkable artistic personality, true intellectual feats of our time, some of whose numbers, pursued by specialized readers and bibliophiles of in multiple places, they are authentic jewels of our graphic arts and publishing?

More, within this multi-purpose contribution and in so many ways disruptive and only Samuel Feijóo - that with a greater degree of specialization addressed surely other panelists-, would not want to leave to refer (Although it is run, in this early celebration of their centenary) the humanist's legacy dimension, aspect that is often overlooked and which consider to be central when it comes to ask a full assessment of the totality of its proposal.

Cintio Vitier - who personally I have a different Feijoo - poetry reading was, to know where, the first (and one of the very few) in bringing us the certainty of that vocation that runs through the whole work of the author of face. A sense of irreducible dation is based on their "great love", He found asylum in one (by mystical moments) Communion - in flesh and spirit - with a God who is always the God of the poor, and with that many men and humble women who inhabit his poems (“I am my love, the real me is my love, my love is my greatest strength, is my only true force”("Verses II", Poetry. Samuel Feijóo, PAG. 193. Selection and prologue by Cintio Vitier. Editorial Cuban lyrics, Havana, 1984).Their pain before human wickedness and cruelty conditions in him a sense of Justice that ends specifying with a Marti taking sides by "the poor of the Earth" and feeding, at the same time, a sense of mercy that, out of José Martí (I have already said so elsewhere) It does not occur with such force in any other Cuban poet.

That solidarity with the destitute - substantive especially from face, and its embodiment will culminate in his notebooks end the wild thinker and the bread of the bobo, feasible to be assumed as true poetic wills-, will be extended to all nature. Like this, a poem that confesses:

What hunger

I am of innocence

Human!

 

O my children!, my

lonely old,

My clean peasants, and

my good heroes!…

 

(Fragment of "Hunger of the lyrical bobo", PAG. 255)

 

passed to others (many other poems of indescribable tenderness) where this hunger, that love, now manifested by the nature, and in particular for animals (birds, butterflies, cows, goats, monkeys, donkeys, lizards, indigenous, bees); texts where even the simplicity of the form seeks to become what, in your case, was not only literature, but also an attitude, a choice of life:

 

The birds come

eat my gifts,

hour after hour:

bread crumbs,

cheese sticks,

bananas

I leave each morning

in my window.

 

So I have songs,

hum of wings,

and secretisima joy

feed them

(not everything is going to be

stone or bullet).

 

By a green corridor

You also get.

 

(Excerpt from "Monstuosillo", PAG. 247)

 

Or that other, very simple, in your air, waving to say, seems to leave us all a poetry:

 

When the butterfly

flies,

outside,

towards your flower,

If you look at it

and the amas,

already

the

you're flying butterfly

in,

in your flower.

 

If you do not have a flower in

the butterfly will not enter

in your

chest.

 

("Between the bee and the flower", PAG. 250)

Once read these small "Psalms", I cannot but ask me about the value and the validity of the thinking underlying this poetry, in an era in which irrationality and violence starring shamelessly a daily show and where, at the same time, the God of the poor is embodies in a much clearer way, especially in our continent. Also I think that at a time in which both spoken of promoting a more harmonious relationship with nature and the environment, well it would be worthwhile to pick up that legacy this great poet, founder among us of a spirit that, far from see exhausted its validity, he arrives in time to set ourselves up for his humble Word and offer us in his "huge love" which are clearly the only way out is already.

The short time that I have not let me no alternative but to (at the risk of being schematic) list some other edges (clear that limited and incomplete) congruent with this posture assumed by Feijoo, both in its writing and artistic endeavor in his life of everlasting montés "Wayfarer".

A quick look at this work would lead us to repair, at least, in:

1- Its infinite faith in beauty, that was devoted in body and soul. As Martí, It was a refined art and culture and its powers ability convinced to ennoble the human being. In that connection he always favored the direct link, fruitful feedback with the creator subject, What we now call "community culture". Few like it can boast of having founded a school and have left so many disciples.

2- His defense of the memory and the identity as living entities, pulsating, in perennial germination. He was one of the developed in weighing the importance of our intangible heritage. He treasured a wealth of folk wisdom that capsized on their books with only a rigor and passion. Legends, Myths, narrations, Customs, sayings, riddles and anecdotes, tenths and quatrains collected by him in his endless walks in our fields, they were saved forever as an expression of the genuine creation of our people.

3- The open character, his artistic proposal universal. In all his work (especially in poetry and the Visual Arts) peeps, far from what you could expect from a worshipper of the traditions, an open attitude, as few experimentation and the transgressive avant-garde gesture-sensitive (It is to say, that's the real, that never stood as a mere dazzled asentidor). This dialectic of creative assimilation in which, without prejudice of any kind, they found the same space that got him settled by tradition, that more bold gestures of the avant-garde (including the contribution of young), He founded both the uniqueness and modernity of his artistic proposal, as this universal approach to which we have referred. His work is anything but a frozen block. Even your pain, There is a hymn to freedom in everything he did implicitly. Today we would say that, in your search, it spared no nothing what could help you with that "human ring" which, at the time, Vallejo claimed for poetry.

4- The consistency of the sincerity and the claim of freedom that we find in his work, with their everyday actions and their civic honesty. Not a few difficulties brought you their "hobby" say openly what they thought. At the same level of what we can deduce from the readings of his poems and texts more attached to the testimonial, the rich anecdotes which left in its wake (real or apocryphal, No matter), gives accounts of a Feijoo enemy of the absurd formalities and bureaucratic and aristocratizantes attitudes. Nothing further from the simple man (on more than one occasion nonsensical) and laborious that was. As well as his books are full of allusions and poems dedicated to a beloved legion of friends and anonymous heroes found the passage in the epic of its plain everyday life, It was the terror of the bureaucrats and the incense. His booming creative capacity - naughty and cheerful in so many drawings and surprising "Follies" - is in itself the denial of closed borders and mediocrity and, in that way, a call to the imagination and the best of human beings.

I'd like, to close these hasty notes, read here, on the threshold of this fair we remember this great poet of our, the last poem of the bread of the bobo, his last notebook. It is a brief text, in their few lines showing it us full-length in their vocation and their infinite faith in the usefulness of poetry. Is titled nothing less than "The bobo poet epitaph"; There says:

 

Who among millions of books

read yours?

Million books,

generation after generation,

forests and forests of verses,

generation after generation,

mountains and mountains of pages...

Generation after generation...

 

And you writing little,

naive,

believing that you alumbras

with your lamps

the lonely soul

Perhaps you found...

 

And millions of books will come

and there will always be a fool

with its versito.

 

 

(Alpidio Alonso-Grau words in the introduction to the panel by the centennial of Samuel Feijóo, in the room of Nicolás Guillen of the XXIII international book fair, in the fortress of La Cabaña, Havana, the 14 February's 2014).
