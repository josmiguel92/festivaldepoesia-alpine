the shift of the offended with eduardo ambry

Categories:

The Tower of Babel

Eduardo Morales Embry, born the 28 October of 1938, It is a native of Valparaiso, Chilean poet.

Up to 1973 He had the ability to reconcile its worker training and employment in the tobacco company in Chile, with an intense work of writer and cultural agent in the press, radio and television.

He shared the bohemia of Valparaiso with the painter Jorge Osorio Tejeda, who founded the workshop of Daedalus, that was the imprint of both artists. This workshop came the first Jorge Osorio woodcuts that illustrated pages of poetry of Eduardo Embry. One of the most famous of these publications, It is a brief collection of poetry of Embry that won the Premio Municipal de Valparaiso, on the occasion of the celebration of the anniversary of the founding of the city: "Stone and feeling of" (our Virgin) "Port clear". It was a prize sponsored by the Union of municipal workers.

Another publication of the Daedalus workshop, was “Paraguerras” collection of poems and woodcuts - size "Mercury"- both artists. Paraguerras, It was created to expose the horrors of the war in Viet Nam. One of these pages is displayed - with glass frame – in the interior of the Bodleian Library, Oxford, England.

Embry, together with Ennio Moltedo, After winning the prize of poetry Nicomedes Guzmán, of the Federation of students of the Institute educational of the Colon of Valparaiso Street, He frequently shared the literary activities of this Institute with teachers and students, among these, Renato Cardenas, Juan Ruiz, Sergio Badilla Castillo, Fernando see, Jose Varela (The brief narrative mention award), Fernando Moreno, Luis Iñigo Madrigal, Nelson Osorio Tejeda and many more, those who are currently, leading practitioners and academics graduates of this historic teaching of Valparaiso. By that time, This group of teachers and students began to get the Stone magazine, which, according to a critic of the moment, Manuel Escorza, was a young irreverent magazine, What “He pulled stones"to the cultural environment of the region.

Is mentioned as one of the members of the generation of 1965.

He participated in 1971 at the first meeting of young poetry that was made at the Pedagogical Institute of Valparaiso, currently University of Playa Ancha, where they met authors who are now very renowned as Oscar Hahn, Waldo Rojas, Floridor Pérez and Gonzalo Millán.

After the coup d'etat of 1973, He was a political prisoner on the ship Lebu.

Freed from the Lebu, in 1974 He left for England supported by the movement developed at the level of higher education in Britain, called “Academics for Chile”. You receive an invitation to work at the University of Glasgow.

After a brief stay in Scotland, Professor Ian Michael, the University of Southampton, in England, He admitted him as a student of the course to obtain the degree of M.Phil in the Faculty of Humanities of the University. In that faculty could study and work in the Department of Spanish.

At Southampton he covered the square left by the Peruvian poet Antonio Cisneros as a reader in the Department of Spanish. In its early years English, He taught language and Hispanic literature in Southampton, in the Portsmouth Polytechnic, in the Wormwood Scrubs prison and at the University of Bournemouth.

Then, Embry traveled to Venezuela, settling in the city of Cumaná, in the State of Sucre, where he made special for the deanship of the University of Oriesu working stage in Sucre. There was ' history of journalism in Cumaná (Venezuela) of 1825- 30’, and Andres Bello and the journalism of 1825. In addition, He served as Coordinator and editor of a local newspaper until the Decade of the nineties.

Back in Southampton, Professor Henry Ettinghausen, it honours him as an honour Resercher Fellow of the Department of modern languages, School of Humanities, He still is linked, as an independent researcher. It was also, a Hartley Researcher Fellow of the University of Southampton.

He was a special guest of the 6. International poetry festival, Caracas, June and July, 2009.

The 2011, He received the award from the art critics circle of Valparaiso.

Assessment of his work

Professor Eddie Morales, the University of Playa Ancha, He says that the speakers present in the poems of Embry joined the poetic craft developed by medieval poets of minstrelsy and the clergy, especially in the poetics of the Archpriest of Hita. The irony, humor, the iconoclastic, the self-confidence, masking and Carnival, among other discursive elements, We showed them in the poetics and the discursivity of Embry.

It adds that Embry is a conversational poet in the tradition of Spanish medieval lyric, and, In addition, post European medieval lyric.

For Eddie Morales, Eduardo Embry - ultimately speakers, they are none other than be coated a different flesh poet – own poetic game-, occur to the reader as degraded subjects, i.e., I's, they are in different experiential situations ("switching off of me", they have called it). The lyrical embriano tends to not be taken seriously - influence parriana, no doubt-, and characterized by the ease facing the different moments of welcoming the world.

The Brazilian professor Teresa cabins, the Federal University of Santa Maria, It notes that the "switching-off of me" is one of the basic features of his poetry, allowing Embry, on the one hand, develop on a ruthless stripping action, What, without a doubt, reminds us and wide the path initially drawn by Nicanor Parra. Thus the image that consists of this I is more of an anti-hero, subject which passes through all the gradations of the medium: sometimes half papanatas, other amilanado and timid, some good-natured and poor Devil, almost always rogue.

The Spanish teacher Carlos Mata Indurain,in terms of research, It highlights its efforts to rescue the poem description of the greatness of the city of Santiago de Chile (1740).

If I leading it back a thousand years,

the horse of the conquest would say ,

If I could go back a thousand years,

be a good horse,

to lift mountains

as Mountains rise up to the sky

the Eagle and the condor;

If I leading it back a thousand years,

walk a thousand miles by dry-Screes,

damp marshes, resonant rivers,

silent snow,

in the living stone of the Inca,

write thousand poems

about gnashing of clouds

it crushed my shoes walking;

I would like to recite a thousand poems

on the verge of a thousand cliffs,

put melody to my verses

with sacred water that comes

at the foot of the mountains;

If I leading it back a thousand years,

drink thousand pints of beer

filled with sand and flowers,

under seas and seas of fiery stones,

I would like to kiss and kiss

thousand of beautiful and ugly girls;

Ay, horse of the conquest,

horse flying on black clouds

lower than Earth's moon,

No I want to feel your laugh crazy,

as a freight train that passes,

on the human body,

under the blue sky of a thousand autumns,

thousand winds and rains thousand, and a thousand other things

that for now I'm not saying them

to not scare the ears

Ay, horse of the conquest,

When a thousand times I am still,

thousand times without reading the press, with thousand eyes

If I could go back a thousand years;

thousand times, Madam, in your beautiful legs,

beautiful legs of all my friends

nose and mouth that kill birds;

in a jumble of second-hand,
