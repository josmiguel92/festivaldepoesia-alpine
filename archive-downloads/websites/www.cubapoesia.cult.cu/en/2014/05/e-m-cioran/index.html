Slavoj Žižek likes to tell the story, Perhaps apocryphal. During World War I, a German position sends a telegram to their Austrian allies: “Here the situation is serious, but not catastrophic”. The response of the Austrians is not expected; says: “Here the situation is catastrophic, but not serious”. For Žižek, the anecdote defines our time: the world is going to meltdown, but we do not take the debacle seriously and do not take measures to prevent it. Žižek may be reason, Although, given my instinctive distrust of the auguries of historical revelation, I tend to doubt it. However, There is another way to interpret the story: According to her, the Austrians of the telegram are not irresponsible, but a few types so astute to understand that, Since anyway the existence of men is catastrophic, the best thing is to not take it seriously and laugh at it. My confidence in the auguries of existential revelation is absolute - not in vain I check daily that the people have the infamous die-custom, so this interpretation seems to me to be unappealable. Of course, it would be the interpretation of Žižek, but that of Cioran.

Emil Cioran, the philosopher of despair and failure, dies in Paris at the 84 years

The Romanian writer lived only and back to his celebrity and aging

Enric González

Death, for him "the conclusion of a madness", reached yesterday Emil Cioran. Romanian philosopher and writer died to the 84 years in Paris, where he resided. He suffered from Alzheimer's disease and left some time ago his attic close to the Odeon to extinguish in a hospital. Hopeless and pessimistic, alien to the official culture pumps, Cioran gave very few interviews. He preferred to talk about his work and be consistent with it. “I have never worked”, once said; “I have preferred to be a parasite to exercise a trade. I have agreed to undergo a relative pittance just to preserve my freedom”. His books, sometimes contradictory, always full of beauty, they dealt with the failure, the indifference, the lucidity and death.

The man who for decades walked by the Latin quarter of Paris, wrapped in a raincoat and with messy hair, alien to his celebrity and his own aging, He was born in Rasinari, a village in Transylvania (Romania), the 8 April of 1911. His father was Orthodox pope and family, According to his own memory, era “tormented, anxious, always negative about the existence. The childhood of Cioran, However, was happy. Aside from his morbid fascination with death (He went to the local graveyard skulls, to play soccer with them), at that time kept a memory of mountain landscapes, games and unconcern. In an interview with the philosopher Gabriel Liceanu in 1983, It was the origin of his pessimism in step to adolescence. The key was their insomnia: “He came to live for weeks without a wink”. “I realized”, You told Liceanu, “that life is bearable thanks to sleep; every morning, After an interruption, a new adventure begins. Insomnia, However, Suppresses the unconsciousness, forces to 24 hours a day of lucidity. ( … ) Life is only possible if there are forgotten”. Without forgetting, the young Cioran, submerged in the obsession of death. Their secondary studies, membership at the Faculty of philosophy and arts of Bucharest, a Bachelor's degree, they were for it an excuse to read without brake and reflect on death.

Friedrich Nietzche was his philosopher's header, but he also studied with relish the work of Bergson, Hegel and Husserl, and he maintained a lifelong devotion to Shakespeare and Dostoyevsky. His youth was marked also by the fascination with the Iron Guard (the Romanian fascist movement) and, starting from 1933, When he got a scholarship from the University of Berlin, by Nazism. You have seduced the “will of all” and the “collective mysticism” of totalitarianism. It was an uneasy fascination, without total delivery. But enough to embarrass you years later. “I'm immunized against all, against all the creeds past, against all the creeds future”, wrote on 1946 to his brother Aurel.

Prior to that, in 1937, Cioran had come to Paris. The polyglot dor pensa (Romanian, German, English, Russian, Italian and Spanish) I wanted to really move further, to Spain, a country that felt a tormented attraction: “I was made to Spain, for the Spanish language the”, declared in 1983. “He was a fan of Saint Teresa of Avila, and I remain.(…) BA she fascinates me excess, coming from this particular madness too, unmistakable, of Spain '. In my youth, I was sorry not to have been Spanish. I was fascinated by Spain, to offer the example of the most prodigious failures. One of the most powerful countries of the world, sunk in such decline!”.

Cioran visited several times Spain. But stayed to live in Paris, where attended the entrance of Nazi troops with indifference and, with the same passivity, the release. In 1947, decided to change language. He had already written six works in Romanian, but the first in French, Precis of decomposition (Breviary of rot, 1949), was an immediate success. His pessimism, their indifference, his contempt for the circumstances of life, they had a huge impact in a French society that embraced Existentialism.

Poor and lonely

Starting from there, the life of Cioran changed little. Scholarships at the Sorbonne he chained to the 40 years, to let him free to eat in the University dining; read, He wrote and malvivió in youth hostels youth until a loft next to the Odeon, with a ridiculous hire; remained poor and lonely, It rejected all the honours that were granted, and despised his own glory. The Gallimard editorial recently re-edited his complete works. Yesterday, It was a spokeswoman for Gallimard, who announced his death.
