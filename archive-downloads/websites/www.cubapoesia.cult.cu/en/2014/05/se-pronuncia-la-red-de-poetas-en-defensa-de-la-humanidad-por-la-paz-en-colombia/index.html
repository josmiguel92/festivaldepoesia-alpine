May 30 2014

pronounce the network of poets in defense of humanity for peace in colombia

Categories:

delivery quick/fast delivery

By: Camilo García Lopez-trigo.

The International poetry festival of Havana 2014 He opened their spaces to the encounter of poets of the Cuban chapter of the network of intellectuals in defense of humanity - expanded with foreigners who visit us-, It met on the morning of Wednesday 28 may in the solidarity lounge of the hotel Habana Libre.

By giving them welcome the Festival Director, Alex Pausides, He spoke on behalf of the poetry festivals network of Latin America and the Caribbean and the world poetry movement in favor of peace and stressed the historical importance that has this hotel in the future of the revolution and momentous moments in the formation of national culture.

To indicate that "poetry is the natural state of the mind", He stressed that the poetic action can serve as a vital tool to configure a Livable World and in harmony. "The poets live the challenge of the majority silenced... and if people do", Poets should be together", said convinced.

As immediate tasks of the Network noted the need to explore avenues of cooperation between the various projects gathered there, plan with realism as possible within the necessary, own or concerted actions on the basis of peace and solidarity, support projects in all possible media, among others, always with a democratizing vision of culture and knowledge to defend the unit in the most absolute respect for difference.

It stressed the importance that the poetry serve others - and ourselves - in the creation and enjoyment of the beauty that nature gives us, but from a committed sense. "Convinced of the potential of the words, poets can be part of the solution", culminated.

The Colombian poet Fernando Rendón, Director of the international poetry Festival of Medellín and alternative Nobel Prize, the remarks corresponded him and praised above all true and deep peace that exists in Havana, along with the certainty of the way that the Cubans are traveling as an example so that the peoples of our America to achieve their freedom and independence, next to sacred as solidarity values.

He shared his concern for what currently happens in Colombia, that has lived in war the vast majority of the history of the Republic, and it has meant a persecution against the artists and most progressive intellectuals. In this sense, He stressed that the international poetry Festival of Medellín has become a forum for spiritual and cultural resistance which is emblematic for the fight that they must engage the poets everywhere.

Why it considered that action must be directed out of the confined space, consumers scholars, and to achieve that poetry turn on in the heart of the village, in the soul of each individual. "That's the mobilizing force of the human spirit", said, and he asked that as a result of the meeting it would, more than a statement, a practical commitment to action for peace in Colombia, in Venezuela and throughout Latin America.

Another remarkable intervention was that of Omar González, Coordinator of the Cuban chapter of the network in defense of humanity, who made a detailed explanation of the history, the emergence and development of the network, their main themes and actions that has developed in the last decade.

He stressed his form of horizontal organization, Cross and cross - walk to society in all its layers and goes against discrimination of all kinds-, as well as the important role played by the network in its first stage as a mobilizing factor of public opinion in favor of progressive processes in Latin America.

He indicated that, today, the network is in need of revitalization, increase the representation of young people who are able to mobilize the consciences, defend the ideas - a much-needed concept to resume - opposite the simplifier and unsettling effect that has involved the neoliberal globalization. He noted also the challenge that means the use of new technologies in this endeavour and the role that may play poetry as spiritual expression of the human.

From this moment, microphones for the Exchange opened with the numerous audience, under the coordination of the leading Cuban poet Waldo Leyva. Their reflections important ideas were heard him like that "- is imposed the action through the word - that is our weapon above any discrepancy, because what is to save humanity".

Several foreign poets took the microphone, as the Mexican Yuri Zambrano who - with passion and intelligence - developed their certainty of the need of "poetic activism", a fundamental weapon for the energetic fight against any action that violates the life, the integrity and health of our people. "Every poem is activism by nature", expressed with passion, and it should have the power to transform our dreams into reality through the office of the word. "Commit or not commit, that is the question", highlighted.

At the end of the meeting, based on a proposal of the Colombian poet Patricia Ariza, participants adopted a final declaration with the name Manifesto for peace in Colombia (is reproduced below) that considers one of the main concerns expressed by the present and, in practice, it becomes a concrete action of poets for peace.
