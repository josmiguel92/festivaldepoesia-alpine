INHERITANCES AND SEEK NEW PATHS
by Silvina Friera
(Página/12, AR, 29 April)
Invisibility represents the barbarism of blindness to the end. The problem of the
circulation and visibility is a central issue in the landscape of literature
Latin American. New technologies reduce distances, but the atomization borders
towards the elusive margins, in the second or third levels, a concert of voices and
frames, a powerful tissue or poetic seedlings, narrative, dramatic and essay. Four
Cuban writers, born in the Decade of the 60, they will participate in the 40 International fair
Buenos Aires book, 2014,in various activities: Writers of dialogue
Latin American, at the Festival of Poesiay also in the presentation of classical works of
José Lezama Lima and Virgilio Piñera. They, NARA Mansur, Zaida Capote, Edel Moralesy
Ernesto Sierra, each one with its rhythms and its intensities, their perspectives and obsessions,
they are part of the heterogeneous map of Cuban literature. Mansur, living in Buenos Aires
from 2008, perceived about Latin America as a "very natural" while was in the
Havana. "Now I feel everything is more scattered and elusive, to a large extent by the disorder
identity - raises the poet, playwright and drama critic to Página/12-. The present remains
being of enough mutual ignorance, We still know the stars and
taking them as absolute references. The issues are so wide that it is no longer effective to speak
only identity and memory as spearheads. Every writer or artist comes with a
Heritage, but with a huge desire to invent his own tradition."
Capote and Morales presented on Thursday 1 may, to the 16.30, Alfonsina room
Storni, Call of the willing, poetic anthology of José Lezama Lima posted by
the editorial Colihue. "Lezama was a grand and ambitious writer, and also a
Joker", features Capote (Havana, 1967), researcher at the Institute of literature
and Linguistics. "In our Institute there who remember how intervened in"
the assemblies: If one spoke about the punctuality, He was referring to the idea of time in
the Egyptians, and just as well with the seriousness of the meeting. With your poise
intellectual, had fun teasing us. Now, While we write the
Dictionary of Cuban works of essay and criticism, seeing what was called ' whiners
Barbados of encephalitis lethargica ' and 'half-hearted letargiricos' critics that
denying the value of an anthology of poems of origins, I go back to have fun
reading it." Morales (Cabaiguán, 1961), author of poetry books as seeing cars
move towards the West and the memory game, He says that Lezama Lima is "a
time of essential growth"in Cuban literature. "That Cuban world
Lezama enriched with the American, with the Hispanic, the Greek and
Universal, It dialogues today with the world a perspective of the culture itself. Sometimes
We also experienced a lezamanismo dazzled by the rediscovery, that is
He made epigonal and lastró own eyes", recognizes the poet invited to the Festival
International poetry and dialogue of Latin-American writers.
"A monument to the Cuban spirit." Thus it defines Lezama Ernesto Sierra (Guines, 1968),
philologist and essayist, author of the double adventure of Adam and apprentice of America. "In
my case, I assume his Magisterium Lezama, his authentic artist lessons, its
conscious dedication to give them form and splendour to the notions of insularity, It
Caribbean, about Latin America, from a universal watch. Lezama is there for
emulate his lessons as a writer, not to imitate him in the concrete style. That would be
as die before birth." Mansur (Havana, 1969) says that Lezama the
Since your imagination, his passion for José Martí and his invention of the
Cuban. "His poetry I don't think that I've marked both - clarifies-. I studied
Theater in the Instituto Superior de Arte, so literature is one of the
learning about theatre, and the theatre contains everything: the body, the word
as fiction but also as critical thinking, light, color… I want to be
a writer who puts the body as an actress, a performer, beyond
the ideas or the formal construction", confesses. Mansur was in charge of the selection
and the critical study of two old panics and other theatrical texts (Colihue) Virgil's
Piñera, that it be presented along with Sierra on Friday 2 from May to the 18.30 (room
Sarmiento). "This work speaks of the variables of fear, but it is also
intensely fun and adheres to own the theatre experimental flows of
his time. Pinera is the playwright more staged and valued in our country, and
also a huge poet. It is a great influence to me by this writing
multigenerica that characterized him."

Fictionalize the experience is one of the issues to be discussed in the
Dialogue of Latin American writers (Sunday 4 from May to the 20.30 in the room
Javier Villafañe). "Writing is first and foremost to expose a personal look, ' a form of
look in the window ', I wrote some poem - remember Morales-. For a poet,
fictionalize their experience, imagine it, return it from an inner rhythm and some
specific forms, It is an almost daily Act of creation. Narrate the experience
own, autoficcionalizarse in prose without losing sight of the references and your senses,
It is less common and a challenge, but by no means impossible or a
loss of reality. On the contrary, the burden of subjectivity extreme and that
sincerity that ultimately supposed to fictionalize the experience bring
also part of the required accuracy that another us reading may well not
find in political discourse to the use, Media, the
interests of large corporations, unbridled market advertising in
our global world. My story 'Memory of fog', for example, is one
fictionalization of the experience. The crisis and the Cuban resistance of the ' 90s can be
"be better designed from that story."

Capote ponders the "striking diversity" of Latin American literature. Enjoy very much reading the Chilean Narrator Diamela Eltit, also invited to this edition of the fair. "In
his latest novel, Special forces, It gets to narrate the intervention of the power in the body,
in the language, in everyday life, with an enviable smoothness." Sierra, on the other hand, sketches
a more skeptical view of the Latin American landscape. "The legacy of the ' 60s, the boom
narrative, is killing us. Those years we inserted in the world literature with all the
Onomatopoeic force of the boombazo. A series of political circumstances converged,
social, cultural and economic that called attention to the indisputable quality of
a part of our literature. But now I perceive a process vice versa - compare
Sierra-. The richness of our literature is diffused in the fragmentation. It is as if to the
Blender's literature, If there is, would have invested you the sense of rotation and before,
from the periphery, It ended up being attracted by a powerful Center, today, from that Centre, VA
to the periphery. Like never before, We have police literature, science fiction, of
Adventure; literature of gender in all its variants themes; a wide range of
topics, genres, discursive models. AND, However, difficult to identify the names and
that new literature titles. We live still in the shadow of the old parents. Of course, the
circumstances are other. Internet and the rapid development of digital media are
forming a new reader and, Therefore, a new literature. The relationship of literature /
market/media dictates sometimes fierce guidelines. The result is Middle map
Fuzzy ourselves. Latin Americans can not abandon our utopia of
Unity and brotherhood. In that quest always find faces not only a
literature, but one whole culture."
