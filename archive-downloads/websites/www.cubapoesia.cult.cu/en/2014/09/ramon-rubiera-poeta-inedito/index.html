Ramon rubiera: unpublished poet

Categories:

The Tower of Babel

An article by Josefina Ortega • Havana.

You look tonight since my belvedere,
where overnight stay and sing the death obsession.
My city, I could abandon my closure,
get out and see your stunning,
your nocturnal agitation , your women,
return to your uplifting pleasures,
to your frantic bustle
but I would be everywhere
with myself.
(City, Rubiera Ramon)

The personality of the poet Ramón Rubiera was a mystery for those who treated, even for his son Alberto, to whom I owe the interest by the almost unknown man of letters, author of a single book, The illusory stars (1925), with a circulation of just 200 copies, that it even came to a library.

His name appears in the historical panorama of Cuban literature, Max Enríquez Ureña, among the Group of poets that emerged in the Decade of 1920 to 1930, He warns the ascendancy of French symbolism, as a modernist survival.

Cintio Vitier gives us an image of Ramón Rubiera in his autobiographical novel of Peña Pobre, in some character that portrays physical as "a dispelling blood, the stubborn head embedded in the torso bulge, supported by short, strong legs, arched back as in some grandullones children…"and describes it as"a poet of the 1920s, journalist or anonymous translator since", to who attributed, as his "most tenacious features", "the hispanofobia, anti-clericalism and the erotomania".

Little is known of this poet unpublished ―como calls it his son, even the date of his birth with accuracy ― (was around 1890) ― as he himself used to removed the age until shortly before his death, that occurred in 1973. Nor was born in Havana ―como are aseguraba―, but in the municipality of Nueva Paz, in the sticks; He also attended university studies in pedagogy and languages, ―segun be creia― nor traveled through Europe, Although I knew Paris as the Palm of your hands, What more than one Frenchman gave faith together evoke the city light in a Havana cafe.

It was an almost complete self-taught, just did some studies of primary, but became a journalist and learned French to perfection, allowing subsequent access to the title of the famous Berlitz language school, and a Chair for the teaching of that language in the Normal School for teachers of Havana.

A few weeks out of the illusory stars print, in 1925, his friend, the poet and revolutionary Rubén Martínez Villena published a memorable article on volume in Chic magazine, where ―afirma Alberto Rubiera― "penetrated like no one else in the heart of those twenty poems and placed them forever in a prominent place of Cuban letters".

Ramon Rubiera, like a tree dry
Leonardo Depestre Catony, 16 may of 2008

Years ago was annually celebrated in Havana province a literary contest that evoked the figure of Ramón Rubiera. I know if the contest stays current, but it was a deserved reminder and tribute to a not very well-known poet, Although Yes careful cleaning and with an interesting work within the third decade of the 20th century Cuban lyric.

Rubiera published a single book, in 1925, When was little more than thirty years. The text, entitled the illusory stars, did not pass unnoticed. A year later, When Félix Lizaso and José Antonio Fernández de Castro prepared his anthology modern poetry in Cuba 1882-1925, They included several compositions that copyright, then a promise, other books that were expected of who… they were never.

The reader may not leave this page without knowing one of them, "Ideology of the dry tree", that it might portray the philosophy of its author:

While the lunar spell damasquinaba
Skeletal members, tree meditating:
I, that springing up from the bowels of the Earth
I feel close to the Astral meadows...
And because of my tendency up and benign,
Whenever I saw two wings desorientadamente
Fly, I showed my tough sympathized shoulders,
Where fruit nests and edges...
I, I managed the cabalistic metamorphoses
Earthly juices in artistic films
And gems taumaturgas... In my abandonment agreno,
Today it look if my dreamy veins...
Ah, Destination! Thus the exemplary commitment you tips
Anyone who manages to magnify their hours!

The poet was born the 9 of June of 1894 and died the 11 may of 1973, by these dates 35 years. He studied pedagogy and languages and began to work in the Bohemian magazine in 1917. He was one of the commentators of the Martí coffee, where were in the Decade of the twenty peers in then: Juan Marinello, Andrés Núñez Olano, Enrique Serpa, Jose Zacarias Tallet, Eduardo Abela... By that time his signature also appeared in the journal of the progress. Between 1929 and 1960 He was in charge of drafting of the own weekly Bohemia headquarters ―como who says, all a vida―. And in those same years he was Professor of French at the Normal School for teachers of Havana.

Of that book mentioned at the beginning is quite wrote. Critics welcomed it, and those who knew the author established a certain correspondence between the content of those verses and the existence of the poet, of an inner life intense, rather unsociable and re-concentrated, and with a work that, Despite being unfortunately scarce, revealing era of early maturity. His literary silence was long, several decades, but between 1965 and 1968 wrote three books: "Sediment of time", "Sample versicolor" and "Arbitraria harvest", still-unpublished.
