The collection South of the Union of writers and artists of Cuba has just published a beautiful book of twenty-five hundred pages, containing the poetic work of Manuel Navarro Moon. With a beautiful design of covering to Elisa Vera cricket Manager, design interior and layout of Onelia Silva Martínez, under the direction of the poet Alex Pausides and the editorial coordination of Teresa Cuesta and Marlene Alfonso, the volume covers works published by the author Ondecorate, from the early date of 1919, up to 1965 -year in which published his last poems-, plus a still unpublished set up to 2009.

Wings of the heart could be called to the gigantic poetic work of Manuel Navarro Moon. More, that label would be incomplete; the other half would be missing - for example-, the reason lightning. But it would not be comprehensive enough signal. And is that, on a huge footprint, poet, How is it Luna Navarro, a mark cannot be set, an emblem covering it in all its magnitude. It has been pointed out as the poet of agrarian reform, the poet of the revolution, the most important figure of the poetic art in Cuba, but we know that it is much more. And such assertion, In addition to his poems, the criterion of imminent Cuban critics and writers from different latitudes has been captured, that over many decades you have been able to rightly value his work.

And gathering texts of these authors, It is the South collection manages to present to its readers a comprehensive bibliographic content book, In addition to guide him in the deep knowledge of the work of the eminent poet, It shows valid examples of what should be criticism (literary), absent or biased today in the majority of our media.

Since Navarro Moon published his first book, Mourners rhythms, the 1919, many were the opinions and many authors who were. Among them, Bonifacio Byrne, Agustín Acosta, francisco Rodríguez Mojena - who wrote the foreword to the scoop-, the Puerto Rican José Joaquín Ribera, Higinio J. Thereafter, After the publication of heart in, in 1922, and, especially, the books which were exiting, supporters of the work of Navarro moon grew. Among those who felt about it as of that date, they are: Enrique José Varona, Emilio Ballagas, Raúl Roa, Juan Marinello - prologara pulse and wave who wrote articles about the work and life of Navarro Moon-, Juana de Ibarbourou, Rómulo Gallegos, Pablo Neruda, León Felipe, Rafael Alberti, Henri Barbousse, Jorge Amado, Carlos Pellicer, Juan Ramón Jiménez, José Antonio Portuondo, Eugenio Florit, Juan Chabás - who pronounced on the wounded land and pulse and wave-, José María Chacón y Calvo, Roberto Fernández Retamar Nicolás Guillén, Cintio Vitier and Volodia Teitelboim. And nearer the time: Joaquin G. Santana, Arturo Arango, Maydolis Garcés Remon, Maydolis Ferrer, Sócrates Nolasco, Heberto Padilla, Joseph North and Virgilio López Lemus, among others.

In my opinion, This book represents a publishing innovation. At least for today, devoid of such serious and comprehensive efforts. Few times readers have the possibility of receiving into one body the work of the artist and his critics. This makes the volume in a kind of small library, in an indispensable compendium, not only for the enjoyment and study of the work of the poet, but also to the knowledge of an important part of the history of our country. "Whole cuba is in his poems" - says Cintio Vitier-. "Navarro Moon is not the voice of a man", It is the cry of a breed screaming hunger for a relentless truth"- says the Chilean Volodia Teitelboim. And it is that the poetics of Navarro Moon work, regardless to the various facets that are developed, It is the living testimony of several times. And that, joined the criticism that goes with it, It puts us at the possibility of looking at a past that already fades and tends to confuse new generations with objectivity.

Rather than speak about the work of Manuel Navarro Moon, as well treated in volume that huy presents the southern collection, I wish to bring to collation criteria of important figures who did at the time of the publication of his books, or in later studies. We all know the hatching which represented Groove, their impact within the context of Cuban poetry. About the same, I would say Raúl Roa on 1928 -date when the book was published-: "my opinion about Groove, I have to say that it is something very serious. With regard to the, I will try to, to the extent of my strength, a review of our poetic values of avant-garde, to locate it to you on the site that unquestionably deserves: at the Summit". And in equal tuning, Roberto Fernández Retamar ensures: "Groove is a huge interest in our poetry book. I must say of him what someone said jokingly of God: ‹SI did not exist we would have to inventarlo›. It is the most obvious example of our avant-garde".

Upon release pulse and waveform in 1932, Juana de Ibarbourou expressed: "Songs and Elegies of pulse and waveform are endearing problems that man faces at all times". And León Felipe: "This Cuban poet's poet until the same implication of his life, and runs through her singing a Psalm that makes strong and powerful poetry wind". And as for the definitive record of the greatness of the Bard manzanilla, stresses Rafael Alberti: "Navarro Moon", as a man and as a poet, is it located in the current meridian of the world and time". And to reach the wound Earth light in 1936, Rómulo Gallego expressed: "it is simply the anthem which rises to denounce the tragedy of social injustices in a desperate beauty". And referring more to the formal, writes Juan Ramón Jiménez Navarro Moon: "Great poetry yours", where the incredible richness of images runs with a majestic and resonant musicality". And with this familiarity of the friend, but the friend who speaks knowingly, with the heart and mind in harmony, with the full power of scholarship, informs the poet José Antonio Portuondo: "After the wounded land, No one can deny that you've been in Cuba, for many years prior, the poet of the agrarian reform". He would never be too elaborate to express my gratitude as a reader, having a valuable book in my hands. One is by Pablo Neruda: "I am writing under the immediate impression that caused me the reading of his fair and beautiful Elegy. Beautiful by successive poetic finds, truly amazing; and just because it translates with unsurpassed mastery and depth more legitimate and torrential human pain". The other is that endearing friend and critic of his work, Juan Marinello, and reza: "Your Elegy really like Dona Martina. And though Navarro Moon began as an intimist poet and was then projected by other roads, next to its vocation of service, be compromised with the sufferings and adversities of the other, his political activism by the humble: never stopped singing love, Express subsidiaries deeper feelings through the high lyricism. That can be seen in almost all of his books, mainly in refuge and Dona Martina; but also Encorazon in, in Groove and pulse and waveform. The introspection that dominates most of these verses is concentrated in a lyrical subject in crisis. Are, almost all, love poems, and in some of them draws a life arc that ends in tear, in pain.

Delving into his biography, in search of possible causes for the ostracism that were wrapped poems as well is, Arango exposes an intimate note of the love life of the poet, -moving me from my main purpose in these lines - playing by the need for believe me express myself in this regard:

It would be good for the young reader - and for all those who have not delved into the life and work of Manuel Navarro Moon - approaching it with depth; as does Arturo Arango, as others have done: projecting lights and shadows that formed the human being and the poet; as well as the era in which you had to live. But it is good also to clarify, inserted in the times that run and returning our gaze towards the past, that the case of Navarro Luna should not be seen as an isolated fact, nor as a weakness of spirit. In libertarian wars in Cuba and in every manifestation of change since long before 1868, When there was nation still no, were leading many Cubans and Cuban belonging to the wealthy classes (called small or medium bourgeoisie, or with any other name).

Socialism aims to end poverty around the world. In Cuba currently emerging new social structures, driven by the need for a sustainable development and the inevitable confrontation with highly known obstacles. What can lead - and favors - heterogeneous human bonds, labour, commercial, economic, and incidents in the society that had already been overcome. But beyond any sociological analysis, love - true love - is indomitable; and never choose where fit body, but where the soul is more fulfilling and adventurous. That may have been the choice of Navarro Moon. And his contemporaries, maybe confused or blinded by the social atmosphere of the time, they committed the sins of envy and of tort, own weak minds and ignoble spirit. Therefore it must be the reader of today warned. It is important to be able to distinguish. And how it makes us see Arango, appreciate the greatness of the poet and of human beings as - is and will be - bardo manzanilla.

Books like this, Obra poética de Manuel Navarro Moon, assure you, they may sit Chair in schools and libraries; serve as a suitable material to guide young poets and scholars of the letters, in a general sense, to enrumbar his work in these conflicts. A work of this magnitude is also an invaluable stimulus for the cultural heritage of the country and its historical and literary memory. And it is also, settle a debt with an author who gave the best of himself as a poet and as a man, a debt with the past and the present, and a bridge of light into the future; because, as policymaker Nicolás Guillén speaking of Navarro Moon: "Although the years pass and the centuries from clumping, his voice will resonate impetuous. It will mark one of the highest and deepest of the Cuban lyric moments". And without a doubt, I know know what rating the Cuban reader, knowledge of its roots and its essence, lover of poetry, judge impartial and attentive.
