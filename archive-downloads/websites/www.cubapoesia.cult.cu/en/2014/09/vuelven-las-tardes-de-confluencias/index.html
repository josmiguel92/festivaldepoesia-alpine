return the afternoon of "Confluence"

Categories:

The Tower of Babel

Roberto Fernández Retamar and Sergio García Zamora: a luxury start to confluence…

By: Camilo García Lopez-trigo

 

Photos: Abel Rojas

A short audiovisual presentation of Visualeer - which reminded of the verses of the famous poem «Normal happy», Roberto Fernández Retamar, and "Strangers in the night", the young Valdes poet Sergio García Zamora - marked the restart, on the afternoon of Friday 19 September Rubén Martínez Villena UNEAC room and with quarterly frequency, poetry space missed by many: Confluences.

Emerged years ago as an initiative of the Hermanos Saíz Association, It had become an old dream to be retaken and one of approaches expressed during the recent 8th Congress of UNEAC, as space Confluence of poets of two generations who continue bringing in our country from their particular styles and experiences.

The presence of the two poets at the meeting was rated as a start "of luxury" by Alberto Marrero, President of the poetry section of the Association of writers of the UNEAC, main sponsor together with the Instituto Cubano del Libro and the AHS. The presentation was conducted by the writer Jorge Angel Hernandez, who managed to maintain a fluid and informal dialogue as host with guests.

Retamar: "Any situation can be reason for approaching poetry."
“Had the small bourgeoisie,
The bourgeoisie buyer,
The landowners,
The proletariat,
The peasantry,
Other classes,
And you,
All tremor, all illusion".
(Madrigal, Roberto Fernández Retamar)

Jorge Ángel presented the work of Roberto Fernández Retamar (Havana, 1930) how "essential in Cuban literature", that has transcended from their beginnings to become "a work that it brings to society while starting from the same creation, in its evolution both in poetry and in the essays".

President of Casa de las Americas and member of the Cuban Academy of language, In addition to the Real Academia Española, He won the national prize of literature in 1989 and numerous awards, as the official Medal of Arts and letters of France, the order Félix Varela of first grade and the Jose Marti order. Founder of the UNEAC and the Centro de Estudios Martianos, He holds a PhD in philosophy and literature and philological sciences, He has published and lectured at numerous universities in Cuba and the world, many of which have been awarded honorary doctorate.

The presenter highlighted in his poems which the own Retamar defined as poetry "circumstance" - that serve as a title for one of his books of poetry in the years 70 -, because they may discover inspired by "perhaps not so poetic situations, too personal, even local or ephemeral… "but with the passage of time and reread them now doesn't matter so much circumstance".

In this respect, the poet answered that the title of his book of poems was an intertextual game, in relation to the "poetry of circumstance" as a poetic creation Center. However, He reaffirmed his conviction that "there is no circumstance, object, reality, for small or ephemeral or may appear, It is not worthy of being touched in a poem that perhaps, If the gods are favorable, survive this situation".

Asked about the possibility of extinction of the so-called "social poetry", on which Retamar has deepened his studies and has been reflected in his poems, It was considered that there is no any kind of poetry that are necessarily endangered: "there are circumstances and more propitious times for certain type of poetry… and I do not think that this is a time for it."

The crowded room Villena had the privilege of hearing him read some of his poems, as Those who are married with rented costumes, Appeal of the blind, Biography -dedicated to Julio Cortázar-, As to them and If you want a stupid know. But before reading thanked share the afternoon with Sergio García Zamora, who read his work in the latest issue of the magazine Amnion and earned him to appreciate it as "a true poet, who is also young".

Sergio García Zamora: "The poet is due to being"
On a beach in Miami or Tenerife
talk to convince them and convince you
about the futility of your return, i.e.,
against the man chickweed
It claims to search its root.
They will not fall into the easy nostalgia:
school yard where you played to the spinning top,
Palmas views from a train at full speed.
Don't feel what they call homeland.
Already do not feel
(The other, Sergio García Zamora)

The first question of Jorge Ángel to Sergio García Zamora (Hope, Villa Clara, 1986) was aimed at highlighting that, Although his work makes "winks" with what is in the literary environment of his generation, is distancing itself from the trend. The poet responded believes not be neither very different or very same, "just worry about being; To write, writing and writing… as a way of life". And he said that he was much pleases to do so, faced with the page blank, and "at the end of the night, meet the Epiphany".

Today, Sergio is one of the most successful young Cuban poets in the country, receiving the spring poetry awards, Red mangrove, Calendar, Foundation of the city of Santa Clara, Emilio Ballagas, La Gaceta de Cuba, among others. He is a graduate of Philology at the Universidad Central de las Villas "Marta Abreu", He has published numerous books in Cuba and has collaborated with magazines of Honduras, Puerto Rico, Guatemala and Mexico.

On internal philosophical force which can be seen in his poems and deep culture that characterize him, He confessed to being a fan of the history of philosophy at the University, to such an extent that when he left the classroom I felt that life had changed him. "Philosophers are an inexhaustible source of poetry", reaffirmed.

But, beyond that, He indicated that his desire for knowledge have been inexhaustible and "from the shipwrecks of Alvar Nuñez head of cow to the texts of San Juan de la Cruz, that they did not intend to be poetry and were", fascinated you know everything: Physics, of the new currents of thought in the world of science, complexity theory. "A perpetual astonishment is that I want to live", He was convinced that poetry and philosophy "are ways to understand and encode the world, equally valid and beautiful".

Several of his poems - read as «Fullness of plum», "Portrait of the mother with peacocks", «Tilos forest at night», «Thinking about Goldfish»,«Knowledge», «Recruits» and «The other» - and sold books by both authors.

At the end, as in the first minutes of the meeting, also the music of guitarist Rodney Howard Vázquez was heard to conclude an enriching evening Confluences that it will once again open its doors next December with another attractive proposal.
