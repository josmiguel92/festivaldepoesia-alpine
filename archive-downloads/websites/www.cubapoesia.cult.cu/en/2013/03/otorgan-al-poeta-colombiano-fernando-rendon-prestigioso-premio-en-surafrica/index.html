awarded to the Colombian poet fernando Rendón prestigious award in South Africa

Give the Colombian poet Fernando
Rendon prestigious award in South Africa

Mkiva Humanitarian Foundation, based in South Africa, it unanimously elected to the Colombian poet Fernando Rendón, Director of the international poetry Festival of Medellín, as one of the recipients in 2013 Mkiva Humanitarian Foundation Award to the main Cultural icon (2013 MKIVA HUMANITARIAN AWARD AS THE FOREMOST …CULTURAL ICON), "for being a prominent cultural activist in Medellin, Colombia, that has influenced millions of people around the world through the platform of the international poetry Festival of Medellín, the largest balloon". So he informed yesterday Fernando Rendón, Zolani Mkiva, Imbongi Yesizwe Trust President, which gives this award annually since 1999.

The ceremony of this recognition of the continental level have held the 18 April at the Walter Sisulu University, Butterworth Campus, Cape East, in the course of half a century of African Unity celebration, 50 anniversary of the creation of the Organization of African Unity (OAU), whose Secretary General Saliem Ahmed Saliem will also receive the prize of the Foundation humanitarian Mkiva this date.

In 2012 the awards ceremony was held with the assistance of the Premier Noxolo Kiviet, Ministers of the nation, and members of the Executive Committees of several provinces, between different personalities.

The Mkiva humanitarian awards were established in 1999 as part and section of the Mkiva humanitarian Foundation programs. This noble idea in the context of the celebration of the life and times of the late Richard Mkenke Mkiva from the village of Bolotwa in Dutywa who was a community activist and a fighter for the rights of rural communities.

Richard Mkiva was highly respected and recognized as an icon of the town. He died in 1959 and his funeral was overcrowded by multitudes of people the length and breadth of the eastern part of the country. It was a global thinker who acted only locally as a result of the restrictive and repressive laws from the colonial powers.

The first African who received this award was South African President Nelson Mandela in 1999. Ken Gampu, the world renowned actor and cultural activist, also received the humanitarian award Mkiva.

Other recipients of the Award Foundation humanitarian Mkiva this year, In addition of the Secretary General of the Organization of African Unity and of the Colombian poet Fernando Rendón will be:

Imtiaz Sooliman philanthropist, whose Foundation contributed humanitarian work in Haiti following devastating earthquake that struck that country's beaches, one of the first to go to Somalia and other African countries, hit by shortages and hunger; Obert Mpofu, Minister of State in the Republic of Zimbabwe, renowned philanthropist from Africa, who has used their own resources and fortune to help projects in your country, and Mbongeni Ngem, world renowned music composer and producer of theatre.

Fernando Rendón directed since its inception, the international poetry Festival of Medellín, that was the alternative Nobel Prize in 2006 in the Parliament of Sweden Award, "by assert and express the human values of beauty, creativity, freedom of expression and for his work with the community, in opposition to fear and violence prevailing in Colombia and the world still today".

Rendon has been one of the pillars of the creation and consolidation of WPM (www.wpm2011.org), who is currently with 230 festivals and poetry organizations, and about 1.300 Poets of 131 countries of the world, and that it actively supports the dialogues and the aspiration for peace of the Colombian people.

In April of 2010 was awarded to Rendon Bashrahil Prize, of Saudi Arabia, "for a high cultural achievement".

In June of 2012 Rendon had also received the international prize of poetry Mihai Eminescu "as recognition of his prodigious poetic activity nationally and globally and for the promotion of international culture".

Gloria Chvatal
Director of communications
Festival Internacional de Poesía de Medellín
