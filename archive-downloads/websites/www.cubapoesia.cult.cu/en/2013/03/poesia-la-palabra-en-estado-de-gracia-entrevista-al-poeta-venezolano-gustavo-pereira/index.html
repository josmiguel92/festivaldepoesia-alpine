poetry: the word in State of grace, interview with the Venezuelan poet gustavo pereira

16 March, 20135 May, 2013 Cubapoesíathe shift of the offended

(Interview Alex Fleites and Alpidio Alonso
with the Venezuelan poet Gustavo Pereira).
Taken from Amnion, 10. 2012).

For the Cuban reader fortunes, the name of the poet Gustavo Pereira is being increasingly more familiar. The publication by the Fondo Editorial of the House Americas of his poetic anthology About wild and the putting into circulation by the Publisher Art and literature a new edition of his book of articles about poetry The worst of the trades, They offer the possibility of approaching the work of this indispensable writer of the Venezuelan and Latin American literature of the day. Born in stone tip, Margarita Island, in 1940, Pereira also cultivated the criticism and the essay. With more than thirty titles published since since the 1960s hither and deserving, among other honors, National Prize of literature in 2001, all intellectual activity has been marked by a sustained commitment to help sharpen more fairly in the contemporary sensibility the face of those "invisible beings" who has given in its expression and expand-- to "the four horizons of the sky" - the hopeful voice of the humble. Below are the fruit of our exchange with the poet

Chronologically speaking, What is the first fact of poetic meaning in your life? And in absolute sense, What would be the most significant?

The image of a child of eleven years writing a few verses on board an oil tanker anchored in Lake Maracaibo, While the reflections of the city lights were shaking in the waters, It is the first thing that comes to my memories as a fragmented by the time film, of which only survive mysterious pieces of tape. As for the second fact, my long struggle against vanity includes the whole idea of transcendence overcome, radioactive thread of smug stupidity of the complacent and, that you do not suspect, to put it to the Pascal, that it will not even be there to see it. I'd rather whisper with Dario: «Oh misery of all fighting by the finite!»! / Is like the butterfly wing / our arm leaving written thought».

According to José Balza, at an early age you stumbled upon la poesía de Bécquer and Dario. What represented then those findings?

A revelation that still lasts me. The handling of language —magnificent and stunning architecture—, precision, Magic, absolute delivery to a desire for perfection and coupled with all that, to put it in figurative language: "good feelings". Although not frequent them as in those years, of them I have the imperishable lesson of the rigor: a fiery soul activated by a cool head.

Another author who is frequently mentioned by scholars of his work, as a beneficial influence, is the Chilean Vicente Huidobro. What he did in creationism?, how much of that survives in its poetic current apprehension of the world?

En la poesía de Huidobro I found in my teens the freedom, in the same way as in the Whitman, Vallejo and Neruda its burning breath. Huidobro had watered at Emerson, who said that new forms of expression they corresponded to each new period in human history, that poem rhythms but the creator thought of rhythm do not: a thought so passionate and alive that, as the genetic spirit of the plant or animal, conceives of its own and different architecture. Creationism, invented by the Great Chilean poet to defiance of the alleged paternity and ingratitude of Pierre Reverdy, was, as we know, only one more between literary avant-gardes calls at the beginning of the 20th century that imantaron almost all young Latin American poet. It supported the autonomy of the poem before all bondage: «It invents new worlds and cares for your Word», reads a verse of "Poetic art" Huidobro, who there same slogan this one, instructive: «The adjective, When does life, Mata». Such freedom, It was not exclusive attribute of anyone but coetaneous need generated by the new historical circumstances and the great spiritual crisis of the last century's first postwar, Since then constituted inalienable praxis. Poetry could rid as well secular string tying it, for example, to the singsong, no to the music; No tone or rhythm inside but the pre-set mould; no feelings but the sentimentality; not to the paradigmatic tradition of rebellion but the adocenamiento, submission and lack of imagination. I do not think that creationism, and even the sum of those insurgent movements —with the exception of Russian Futurism and Surrealism— has bequeathed us a poem of the magnitude of Altazor, which, at this point, us still spreading their dance of Galaxy in gestation. Throughout the live I was learning what deeper us moves tend to be the most free of the adventures of the spirit, and that what we know as spirit is not but an expression of sensitive awareness. The work and the example of the poets who were able to go beyond that made me understand that about every lyric poet should have first and foremost what Jonathan Swift attributed to Petronius: Liber spiritus.

Benito Pereira was an active trade unionist. What influence did in the young Gustavo political ideas of his father? What is the memory more endearing to that saved him?

Among other things he taught me to discover the doubt in all apparent certainty. And in the heart that divides the closest thing to certainly. Workman, self-taught, communist activist since his youth, tireless reader, co-founder of the first trade unions and participant in the fights against the trusts oil tankers in Venezuela when be unionist East meant persecutions, prisons, unemployment, risk, But first of all value, companionship and dignity, with the unlearnt what school, the official manuals and the established establishment I insuflaban. That's why among the endearing memories I have of those days are the for me endless hours that I read made me listen to good music or I taught International between the noise of the machines in the factory where she worked.

In 1956 published in Puerto la Cruz his poetry collection The sound of light. What was the poetic landscape of Venezuela at the time? What authors were recognized as gods trustee??

We lived at the time of the dictatorship of Marcos Pérez Jiménez (1948–1958) and poetry —and literature in general— they were, as in all tyranny, confined, limited by the aestheticism and entrelineas them (from there that first time teen notebook be called thus, Although the title was prophetic in particular because a year and a half after the dictator would be overthrown and I could log in to the University). Tyrannies, you know, they may not prevent the insurgency of poetry, in the same way that not all poetry is insurgent, in no sense. In those years, However, Juan Sánchez Peláez published almost in secret (in 1951) one of his two books of lyric and fundamentally subversive, Elena and elements (the other is Custom animal, 1959) and young people read My father the immigrant and The warm spaces (published in 1945 and 1952) of Vicente Gerbasi, who had belonged to a group formed after the long satrapy of Juan Vicente Gómez, Friday, Advanced aesthetic misunderstood at that time. Other subversive poetry books, politically subversive, published in exile by their authors, circulated clandestinely, and that was how I could at age thirteen read poems of Carlos Augusto León and Miguel Otero Silva in concealed and wrinkled pages, copied by invisible hands in the National Library and passed to machine so they circulated as insurgent presences. It incubated, disparate under mandatory, the renewal that would explode in Venezuela in the next decade, and so, even though he is says the best poetry is often recollect step within walking distance, the awakening of the new poetic consciousness was forming in those years of spiritual and political resistance, as I guess happened in Cuba pre–Revolutionary.

 

Several decades of experiences and evaluated in the light of the current Venezuelan reality, What was the most significant and fertilizing to left Sardio and The roof of the whale?

The reaction against the vacuous verbalism and the quaint simplicity, the rejection of the folkloric remanents expressed poetically dial, selected sacralizador nationalism, the questioning of the moralising discourse, but at the same time the assumption of a commitment to the historical reality, in all its aspects, ethical and aesthetic. They tried not to only Sardio and The roof of the whale. Sardio was founded as a magazine in 1958, the fall of tyranny, and the Group belonged since fundamental names in Venezuelan literature (as Ramon Palomares in poetry and Salvador Garmendia in narrative) It integrated then The ceiling. In parallel to emerge in Caracas and other cities groups as Table round, In Haa, Forty degrees in the shade, Tropic one o City Mercuria, manifestly insurgent literary and politically. In general, It was, in the early years of the Decade of the sixties, a poetic guerrilla equivalent to the policy, whose purposes ' change lives and transform society ' not be avenían using mechanical recipes because it was a dialectical question: for a given time and a given country, resources from the fight due to a need, as it expressed one of the manifestos of The ceiling. This called for action: a poem and a painting that contribute to dynamite the mossy poetic and artistic structures and attacked the moral rot that turned the country on its foundations, Thanks to the treason of their rulers, in plagued neocolony of misery and exclusion. But as a former I proverb African, with thousand cats a lion can not be. Starting from 1959, coupled with the response of the forces of the left against the repressive policy of the Government of newly elected President Rómulo Betancourt, the Cuban revolution will become a part among us–waters. I remember that in a trial of the 1960s Ludovico Silva characterized that generation —that I had come at the wrong time, Seventeen— pointing out that the regime, in his fierce I siege to young people, those who repressed by wear long hair or use beard in the style of the Cuban guerrillas, He had been told: Enmerdez-vous!, and the young poets learned the lesson, a lesson in social coprología which translated the taunt, sarcasm and scatology in amotinada dissonance. The defections, betrayals and betrayals auto would then, with the predictable defeat. Can you offer our readers his personal anthology of Venezuelan poetry in ten names?

Let me name just poets whose major works were published between the last third of the 19th century and the first half of the 20th because, to be fair, I could quote at least thirty: Juan Antonio Pérez Bonalde (1846–1892), Francisco Lazo Martí (1864–1909), José Antonio Ramos Sucre (1890–1930), Sallust Gonzalez corners (1886–1933), Fernando Paz Castillo (1893–1981), Andrés Eloy Blanco (1896–1955), Enriqueta Arvelo Larriva (1901–1962), Luis Castro (1909–1933), Vicente Gerbasi (1913–1992), Juan Sánchez Peláez (1922–2003).

 

We are going to point out four points on the world map that tell us what associations cause you: Isla Margarita, Puerto la Cruz, Caracas and Paris.

«I was born on an island to which the ocean gave tenderness», the first verse of a poem of my book says Sentimentario, that quote with a hint of blush. And since then the perennial nostalgia of the premature banished, eyes bound forever to the sea. Incorrigible addict, because the firm formerly called Costa, in Puerto la Cruz, three blocks from the once quiet Caribbean Bay, I spent the rest of my life. During the "interregnum" Caracas was forced habituation of the timid parochial to public life, studies, amores, partisanship, solitudes. Paris, later, the dazzle of the maladjusted indiano, four alleged years which became two before repetitive nostalgia for the primordial exile for those waters.

 

What are the somaris, you get into that kind of lyrical composition? Is it a way by which plan to follow?

The somari is a short poem, without mandatory prison, not more than a sonnet, turned in the beginning of the repeated deletions of a larger poem and after the basic need of expressing what I thought and I think can be said in few verses. The name is a neologism that I had to go when the small device was made in my sacred recidivism, constant to perpetrate, probably under Baroque overflows to overcome impulses, or rather cumbersome, some ruthless poetry. This claim is far from new, It is ancient, to such an extent that in some languages poetry is equivalent to concentration, as in German, whose Word Dicht, that means dense, concentrate, is the word Dichtung, poetry. However a somari has nothing to do, from the formal point of view, with the haiku, or with the tanka, or with the epigram, or with the copla, or with the redondilla, or with Limerick, or with the tenth, unless all these poetic forms do honor as soon as, the character concise, the power of synthesis in a world of overflows.

What could say about communication in poetry? What value vested you in his poetic work?

Although not always helps us to name the ineffable —that tends to be beyond language and closer to the inner visions— to explain the unexplainable, the word was created by the human brain to communicate, to express ideas and transmit feelings. If the word is not used for that is transformed in babbling, and in such case shall be preferable to silence. Still in the experimental poetry, as in the Calligrams Apollinaire or attempts at Tablada, We found communication, and not only, of course, plastic, It was the intention of both. We live in a time —at least in the so-called Western culture and its area of influence— where insecurity reigns, where everything is fleeting, transient, and life goes so rapidly that sometimes I think if indeed we expect a future whose unique tradition will be the empty, including, of course, the vacuum in the poetic language. For me, Poetry will remain the word in State of grace, No matter that when the occasion warrants it is insubordination, which is also a State of grace.

 

There are those who deny you salt and water to the poetry, rhymed in the literary writing of today. Quite a few believe it an antique without the possibility of tuning with the sensitivity of the times that run. Beyond their preferences, What is your opinion?

Although I do not deny him salt or water, I count me among those who are convinced that in poetry the metric became not only prison, but prison predictable and lethargic. When it appeared many poets hosted free verse as an instrument of liberation, to the extent that Mallarmé —as an Aesthete— He came to say that for the first time poets sang not tied to the lectern. The own Martí spoke of the sincere expression of free thought to renew the poetic form. One of the great virtues of the generation of the 27 Spanish was rid, in the case of their most distinguished poets, a tradition —a great tradition— it weighed —and yet weighs— in them as avalanche. Up to Garcia Lorca and Alberti, for those that tradition was sacred, they wrote books so daring and deep and poignant as Poet in New York and About angels, Perhaps under the still fresh effusions of the dark glow of the Neruda's Residence on Earth. The history of poetry is the history of the development of the societies where it is born, grows, transforms and reproduces. And to every type of society it is a language. It is true that much of the so-called free verse hides bad prose segmented, but poetry ends always claim it. VALLEJO said that in a poem, the important thing was the tone, that it is untranslatable, and he called it verbal life sentence. Put the example of Whitman, who only ideas were translated, rational quality and philosophical accents and very little of their strictly poetic quality, on the imponderables of the verb. In all other respects, tone can not learn by pure reason, it comes from a mysterious dictate of the unconscious, educated —It can be— or rude, for the reason, responsible for hindering, I do not know whether for right or wrong, its natural flow.

From there I have to avoid them, face them or deal with them. A posteriori, When writing, or rather, the dictation of the poem has concluded, am pleased to adulterate, delete —mostly delete—, delete, to try to rebel against who dictates, because even the submission of itself by itself is acceptable.

From time to time, insofar as the pendulum of the world swings from left to right (and vice versa), taken or abandoned the controversy over the role of the intellectual in society. Living in the center of the political volcano which is the Venezuela of today, What do you think of this issue?

Old and repeated issue which reached dramatic proportions almost from Sartre. Years ago raised it me this way: is it take to the society in which it is immersed in which full responsibility, of course, the literary work is included, or only partial responsibilities, pragmatic? I.e., do you owe, for example, poet escape in his social contingencies which, in one way or another, have helped create it? Eliot, who was politically conservative, in a beautiful and amazing booklet today disappeared from bookstores, The critical function and function of poetry, He wrote that the development of the poetry was in itself indicator of social changes and that any radical changes in the poetic forms was a symptom of much more profound changes in society and the individual. Could you tell us briefly about the current state of Venezuelan poetry, main thematic lines, styles, aesthetic currents?

Venezuelan poetry was complying, especially in the 20th century, as a great choir can be heard in the, not outstanding soloists universal as occurred in neighboring countries that had, for example, founding poets or part–waters such as Dario or Neruda, but a set of voices of various registers and tuning forks, which not a few may include with honour in the most rigorous anthologies. Against them ever conspired insulation, inconstancy, the quasi clandestine editions and, derived from this, your little to almost no broadcast, even in their own country. For many years abroad, when there was talk of Venezuelan literature just mentioned the name of Gallegos, who had managed to transpose the invisible fence thank you, I think, not into his great novelist as for his involvement in political contests that allowed him to be elected —and a few months later overthrown— The country's President. It will then be, in the last decades, When timidly comes carried out a process of promotion of reading, with an emphasis on the national, that has made it possible, to the surprise of many, find in bookstores a profuse catalogue of Venezuelan poets whose editions, in some cases, are they exhausted despite the nothing shy runs. Various topics and trends encourage this mixed choir, only harmonized by from the matrix involes heartbeat. Like this, the memory of Earth, the splendor of popular in the mestizo SAP, the resolana charred land, the urban vibrations, the combination of irreverence and contest, Ethics and aesthetics, the agonizing commitment and social, harassment of everyday life, the labyrinths of the unconscious, disturbing and desacralizadores humor and irony, the Ethereal weight of the absolute, nursing homes in religion, the loving intimacy, historicist shake or insurrection of the revelations, come together in that all heterogeneous.

Many intellectuals have called attention to the devastating operation of cultural recolonization launched from the centers of power against our peoples. Specifically from the aesthetic point of view, How appreciated you manifested this phenomenon in the field of poetry written in our region today?

It's a true crusade of decerebrate intended to inoculate the values of the system, as we suspect, they are usually wrapped in paper candy. This crusade is your RAM higher in mass media, grouped in media oligopolies, radio, film and television, collusion in all over the world to hide, manipulate and distort what affects the interests of the stablishment and turn human beings into androids and, above all, voracious consumers. Do to what extent the current crisis that the world turn also poetry? AND, in the reverse direction, What lighting do that it can bring poetry to help humanity beyond the darkness of what is shaping up as a dead end?

In fact at this point in my life I don't know how poetry can contribute, as she passes through non-conventional paths —those of the soul— and these trails are generally not always lead to safe harbor. In all other respects, I think that every man or woman sensitive, poet or not, It has the moral obligation to provide voice and action to denounce the horror, resist domination, Unmasking the effrontery, oppose those who in their unbridled insanity and their mercantilist greed inficionan life, they perpetrate crimes and other atrocities and causing the planet to secure devastating hegemony of their capitals.
