Carilda Oliver Labra

“IF YOU STRUGGLED AND I EXPLAINED MY LIFE, AS I WAS GOING TO GO AFTER??”
by Silvina Friera.

Carilda Oliver Labra has 90 years. He started to publish in 1949, When surprised the literary world.

From Matanzas

“I am not a lark, I am OWL; in the evenings I am happy”, Carilda Oliver Labra said, one of the most important voices in American poetry in the second half of the 20th century.
Now that the almendron - a taxi-collective- take the via Blanca and begins to leave Havana bound for slaughter, comes the memory of the telephone talk, When accepted the interview with Pagina/12 with the only condition that was not early.
The landscape, as it enters the Mayabeque province, becomes more rural: cows, bulls and horses graze so campantes that produce a feeling akin to envy.
A ferret crosses route and dodge the almendron just in time. “The Earth” It is a beautiful poem by Carilda that comes to mind:
“When my grandmother came
brought a little bit of Spanish land,
When my mother went
took a bit of Cuban soil.
I not keep me any little homeland:
want it all
on my grave”.

Camilo, the driver, degree in mathematics than even 1994 He taught in secondary schools, serves as tour guide.
Suddenly it says Havana Club rum factory, in Santa Cruz del Norte. Pupil levitates bewitched by the grace of the Valley of the Yumuri and Bacunayagua bridge. It has 110 meters; It is the highest in the country and divides the Mayabeque province of Matanzas, “the city of bridges”, the Athens of Cuba.
In less than a rooster that sings, but after two and a half hours of travel and 90 km route, you will reach the barrio Pueblo Nuevo, la Calzada de Tirry 81, the street that leads
towards the world of the Cuban poet who loves “time and its cosmic transfigurations”.

The House where he lives Carilda is of 1885. “All of the stained glass windows are Italian. This stained glass window - designates one between the dining room and patio- It is the only remaining entire Matanzas, but in a cyclone broke a little. I put a note of those who use the set designers, of the same color, and is not.
But as it has rained has been dropped. Everything is ancient. The quarrymen are the Foundation of the House; they are still because we have not wanted them to touch.
We moved in 1926. I was born in ' 22. Or I've been 86 years of living here, because I turned 90 in July. I'm nearly as old as the House.” You have to see to Carilda smile; slowly shaking shoulders and cheeks blush slightly. At his feet is accommodated a dog sausage that is fifteen years old and is combing rods. “As it was very what and you could eat Raydel socks, Stalin put him. But he responds to the name of 'Daddy'”, reveals Carilda. “Papito” boost ears and wagging his tail, celebrating the timely clarification.
In the courtyard, a handful of cats of all colors and sizes run, they jump through the air and seem to be the Kings of the balance when they walk through the edges of the pots. The cat family was expanded: two of the cats had breeding; now they are 16.
“I've always had cats, from nina. I first had one single, wonderful, that when I went to the United States to visit my family, fifteen days nothing more, He refused to eat and died. When I returned, my mother-in-law had buried it in a Mason. Ghandi was called. I cried a lot, I was very wrong.”

Matancera poet will present at the next book fair of the Habana a woman writes (Editions Matanzas), an anthology of his poetry prefaced by Raydel H. Fernández, the fifty years younger than her poet husband, made to celebrate the 90 Carilda years.

As a magician, open hands and displayed the copy disappears powder, a miniature yellow Hardcover Edition and prologue of Antonio Piedra, posted in Spain. “Look how it is; had them saved one another closely. I don't know what has happened!”, complains. Moisture, as always, fulfilled the role of exemplary student.
The charming miniature Cap Peel. “This book is related to personal problems, do you understand? I was banned, I was not published for a long time, between the ' 63 and ' 78. In ' 78 I took a risk to go to the contest 26 July. I was given a diploma and I am scheduled to the site in Havana where given the prize. From the moment that I'm going to award, It is because I see a favorable situation.”

Silvina Friera - what was happened?

Carilda Oliver Labra -Nothing… Mira: nor the same Fidel I think you know what happened. He did not directly have nothing to do with that. It seems to me that was a problem among writers, clear that writers with political power.
If I had written Canto to Fidel when Fidel was in the Sierra! After the triumph of the revolution, was satisfied; was I a dream fulfilled, a suction, a need to see my country free. Then the revolution comes with its reforms, their new modes; many prejudices are bringing down.
It is a birth of a whole new era. To me that does not I call poetry and jump over that. But I'm quiet, a time without writing or writing things that had nothing to do with the revolution, more about my personal life, loving; all the erotic poetry to, Of course, early had that line. The time has passed and there have been studies, meetings; We have been invited by the same revolution, by leaders, to explain what happened.
In no time it occurred to me go me in my country because I not published. That never happened to me mind because Cuba must always be above all, You understand?

SF - why it does you, Carilda?

COL -I'll stick by love, girl, because I love my country. If I struggled and I explained my life, How could I go next? I could not go because what I wanted was in the Government, do you realize? Now I have my dissensions with various actions of the revolution.
But I do not feel able to discuss them because I am a natural revolutionary. I'm not a person that I governed by certain rules. I am always very free. I've complied with everything that I see that it has been wonderful because the revolution has given changes in the culture and in every way. With disappears dust begin again to be recognized. So it is not that they understand the revolution, but the revolution to me understands me.
Seems crazy that I am telling you, but it is. Many of the writers who are now targeting were banned. What does it mean? There is not only a decision of conscience of the writer, but the revolution. And those that looked like enemies never were because if not today could not be in places that are. And that, girl, It is a sociological thing.

SF - while writing erotic poems knew that it was going to bother, not?

COL -That Yes, I have exercised my freedom, I feel woman. I loved the freedom and also wrote that gave me the win. I remember that at the beginning clear hid my verses because I was as penalty, but not because it contained nothing too daring. This is a work of so much intimacy with the paper, with ink, it comes to be a trade that seems mysterious and that is not always.
One knows when comes the verse that serves and when another that must be disposed. Poetry is a prodigious visit when it occurs because sometimes you spend much time and not get anything. A verse is not always poetry. And poetry is everywhere, not only in verse. I started writing after the first books; I began to have, without realizing, an own language, a way of expressing myself.

SF - do you remember how did the poem “I desordeno I, love, I desordeno I”?

COL -I wrote it very young. Neruda told me a very nice phrase that wrote it in a brief letter: “In ' I desordeno, love, I desordeno I’ is the water on the fire”. What I desordeno…?
“I desordeno I, love, I desordeno I
When I go in your mouth delayed”.

Look who says “you play with the tip of my breasts”. But that happens because then at the end it says… (think) Wait for you, Show me… I know that memory, but sometimes I forget. “And although I want to kiss you kneeling…”

What does it mean the kneeling? The people interpret it wrong; He plays that I am kneeling and looks where it touches the mouth (laughter). And here comes everything that comes back, but it is not.
“And although I want to kiss you kneeling
When I go in your mouth delayed
I desordeno I, love, I desordeno me.”

It means that it is not kneeling with passion; is with veneration. But however, sex is pronounced. While you want to kiss kneeling, I desordeno me when I Kiss. Comes the sex. Note that is the nature of women, young girl, that starts. It is a poem by the discovery of the young, but I do not write it was to no man.

SF - Really?

COL -Yes, that poem I write when low in a very nice place here, called Monserrat. There were some balls to dismiss those who were going to marry. I was alone with an aunt who accompanied me because he had to climb the Hill, take a cart, all that sort of thing. The evening fell.
The people was drinking very gently, sounded pretty music; Waltz, that was what was danced. And there was a very young girl, much younger than me, she danced with a young man. First they danced further apart, then more United, they were then fully glued. The person who accompanied her chaperone rose, He took her arm and told her: “Let's go”, because he saw what was happening.
A little while I went because nobody was left after twelve o'clock. I agree that going down the stairs of Monserrat saying: “The girl is disturbing”. But I do not know what that verb, because no one was using it. “The girl is disturbing” and the mother realized. And I got here and I wrote it.

SF - Many visited in this House, as Rafael Alberti and Hemingway…

COL -Hemingway, really, did not come to see me, fix you. Rafael Alberti came to see me with his wife, María Teresa León. There is a very nice story because when would, I told him:
“Make me a memory”. Then he said to me: “Give me your hand”.
I gave him a hand, He took out a pen and drew me a dove. I told him: “It is the peace, isn't it?”. And he said to me: “Until she touched your hand and returned from the war”. It is a thing of poets (laughter).

SF - The myth that has been built in lathe of your figure, It is said that Hemingway was in love with you.

COL -That is not true… He had what his gallantry. That was in the fifties. I was thirty-year peak and was already divorced. But it had a very blond hair and the most beautiful eyes; looked good but that already happened…
No me da pena say it because it is true that it looked good. It turns out that Hemingway came to Cuba in the Ile de France, It was the boat. Del Ayuntamiento de Matanzas a cable passed him to the boat where he said killings declared it “Guest of honor” and that they wanted to hand over the key to the city.
I was the poetess of Matanzas - had already won a prize to the South of my throat-, they gave me a great wrench in a wooden case. The key was nickel steel, was very crude. I then prepared a speech of a paginita in English. He fell from the boat and came in a boat to the port. There the Mayor spoke to him. When I got to me, I read the words and tell you the key. Hemingway told me, in perfect Spanish: “Not that llavecita you needed to open my heart”. What rogue Hemingway! (laughter). And we found ourselves two years in El Floridita, in Havana.
But you know how journalists are. When we were at El Floridita, appeared as three photographers and began to portray me. I don't have any photos because I do not know or what was done with all that. But people soon invented a romance. To me, have invented many romances.

SF - Gabriela Mistral complimented her on how it ended the sonnets. Is the sonnet his preferred form?

COL -The sonnet is easy, It is not my favorite, but there are things that come in sonnets. I like free verse, but I understand that I am not very good at that, seems to me. I met Gabriela Mistral in the casa de Dulce María Loynaz. She invited me when Gabriela came for the centenary of Martí. Called me on the phone because he had gone out in the newspaper alert mia a tenth to Martí, It is very simple and it says:

“What most alive dead dead!
What dead,
God, less dead!
What so awake asleep
The definitive Martí!”,

is that I do not remember me more. Gabriela saw her in the newspaper and said to Dulce María: “Who is this poem, This poet that says here that I do not know it?”. Then Dulce said: “It is a child of the field”. Of course, This was camp. And Dulce María was an aristocrat of lot of money. If you see the House where I was! It had 19 all black-clad servants. It was very rich, but she was very plain and simple. Dulce María invited me to go to his house because Gabriela told him that he wanted to meet me.
And when I took him to the South of my throat, Gabriela said: “But how you close as well verses, close them as well!”. I'm writing the sonnet and when the best verse comes I put it at the end; then I track is below p’ top.

COL -Not, Sometimes I start it up or do it full. And sometimes, When it can't find the end, I do not finish the sonnet and leave it because the end is very important. It is impossible for me to stop writing. Now I am reviewing my work because I have to leave what I think it's less bad. Poetry is very difficult. Expunge that is a task that I am doing with much work by the problem in the view that I have. If I write, that I left long ago, I know that you looming otherwise. Every few years I like to change.
