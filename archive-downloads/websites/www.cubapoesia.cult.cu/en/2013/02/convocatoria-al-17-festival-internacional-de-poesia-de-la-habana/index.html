Feb 24 2013

call to the 17 international poetry festival of Havana

Categories:

delivery quick/fast delivery, Festival

The Havana International Festival of poetry of under the auspices of the Union of writers and artists of Cuba, the Office of the city historian, the Ministry of culture, the Cuban book Institute, the Center for Exchange and reference. Community initiative (CIERIC), Charity Andalusian society and the Asia-iberoamerica Cultural Foundation, will be made of the 26 to the 31 may of 2014, in its dedicated Biennial Edition Nancy Morejón, orality and poetry written by women.

 

International support Committee

Thiago de Mello (Brazil), Marcelino Dos Santos (Mozambique), Antonio Goncalves (Angola), Zolani Mkiva (South Africa), (Ecuador), Wolsan and Yang Samil (Korea), Juan Bañuelos (Mexico), Hildebrando Perez (Peru), Cristian birds (Ecuador), Fernando Rendón (Colombia).

 

Honorary President

Aitana Alberti, Pablo Armando Fernández, César López, Nancy Morejón, Miguel Barnet, Eusebio Leal, Waldo Leyva, Virgilio López Lemus, Alpidio Alonso, Jesus David Curbelo

 

MAIN ACTIVITIES

 

Veiled word in the world, Prize Rafael Alberti

Laboratory of Scriptures: Poetry to the poetry written by women and oral

Colloquium: The work of Nancy Morejon, in his 70 anniversary:

Elbows on the bridge, on the bicentenary of José Jacinto Milanés

Panel dedicated to the centennial of Samuel Feijóo

Words written in the sand by an innocent seminar, on the centenary of Gastón Baquero

Meeting of managers of international poetry festivals of our America

Itinerant Cubapoesía: City of Manzanillo

 

Home: Monday day 26 may

Closing: Saturday day 31 may

 

 

Coordination general of the Festival

Alex Pausides, Pierre Bernet, Pedro López Cerviño,

Carmen González, Karel Leyva, Jamila Medina, Sinecio Verdecia, Jorge Bousoño,

Teresa Hill, Marlene Alfonso, Betsy Red, Tania Echavarria, Lourdes Diaz.

 

 

 

Union of writers and artists of Cuba.

Street 17 # 354 between G and H, Vedado, Ciudad de La Habana, Phone 832 4152 (53-7) 833 3158 Fax
