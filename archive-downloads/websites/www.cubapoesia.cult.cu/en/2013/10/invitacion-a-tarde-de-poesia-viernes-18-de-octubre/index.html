Alberto Marrero Fernández born in Havana, 1956. Poet and Narrator. In the year 1986 He published his first book of poems with the title Tilt balance under the label publishing outside the walls. Author of poems The pit and the pendulum, published in the first edition of the new pine collection, Cuban lyrics, 1994. With Salvation and the eclipse Julián de el Casal UNEAC received mention in the contest, in 1991. In 2001 He obtained the first prize of the contest of poetry Regino Pedroso, newspaper Workers. In 2003 He won the national prize for narrative brothers Loynaz with book Last March wind, subsequently published under the label of Ediciones Loynaz in the 2005 and reprinted in the 2010. His book The drowned in the Tiber deserved in 2004 the story of Luis Rogelio Nogueras Contest award, the center of the book and literature in Ciudad de La Habana, published by Editions outside the walls in the 2005. That same year editions Union unveiled his poetry collection The infinite proximity. In 2007 He published the book of tales Babel effect by the Cuban Editorial letters. Julián de el Casal of UNEAC poetry award 2009 with the book The abolished time limit and the award of tale of La Gaceta de Cuba also in the 2009. His poetic and narrative work has been published in numerous national magazines. Poems and his stories appear in the following anthologies: Poetry today in Cuba, Publishing Zero, 1991, Spain; Tales of tobacco conaroma, Popular publishing, 2008, Spain; Between the pores and the stars, Casa Editora Abril, 2009; Smoke trails, Editorial Oriente, 2009. Master's degree in history in 1989. Member of the UNEAC.

Ivan Gerardo Campanioni born in San Cristóbal, Pinar del Rio, in 1942. In 1959 He joined the brotherhood of hope Association, of young Masons. Founder of the Association of young rebels and the Union of young communists. Poet signatory of the manifesto "we speak" published in the first exit of the bearded Cayman, in 1966. His poems appeared in the volume Five young poets, posted by UNEAC, in 1965. A founding member of the Brigade Hermanos Saiz. He worked in the newspaper today, the Popular Socialist Party and the newspaper Granma of the Cuban Communist Party. Mobilized during the invasion of Playa Girón and the October Crisis. Typist, Cutter shank during the harvest of the 10 million, militiaman, Archivist, master, Thanks, stores Manager, internal trade official, constructor, Union leader.

In 2006 He was distinguished in the competition Regino Pedroso of the CTC. He is Chairman of a subsidiary of the Chair of the elderly. Delegate is of the Popular power in a district of la Habana Vieja. His poems have appeared in journals Union, Casa de las Américas, The bearded Cayman, the Gaceta de Cuba and other magazines and newspapers. In 2009 the Editorial José Martí published his book The creature.

A TRIAL ABOUT THE AUTHOR

I would say that theirs is an intensely conversational poetry, that he disdains not even street language. But if it looks only like this, I think that it is losing the essence of his way of approaching the poem. It has accused this poetry its neglect of the metaphor. In effect, While the text lezamiano is a constant metaphorical progression, This poetry seems to despise the central trope, so important for some avant-garde poets.

The interesting thing about the texts of Campanioni is seeking the effect that could produce the metaphor through other ways of language. .. When life has given one the satisfaction to see the work of that "group" of dreamers... Among them, without leaving the slightest doubt, in the first row, is Iván Gerardo Campanioni.

Guillermo Rodriguez Rivera.

THREE POEMS OF ALBERTO MARRERO

ONE DAY

One day I realized that it was only.

It should happen in the early 1970s

When my mother announced that there would be no rice for a long time

and I discovered that Homer was blind, memorious,

Impotent Poe and syphilitic Nietzsche,

and then I started to use the recycled clothing

that little by little it was inheriting from my father.

One day I realised the irreversible sense of the past

listening to a rock band that climbed to the sky.

Front should happen a pintarrajeada wall

he proclaimed a radiant and endless future.

 

DOOR

There is a door that I've never played.

A dark streaks and moldy hinges door

through which you can hear voices of angels.

As a child, I knew that I would not cross it,

I do not know if by horror or simple caution.

We all have a door that never open,

Although on the other side be heard voices of angels.

CLOTHES ON THE LINE

 

The vivid moments seem to

the clothes hanging from the cord.

A frayed, old spots

that the detergent has failed to delete,

new ones, bought at crazy prices.

In the courtyard the freshly washed clothes dripping

about creeping pumpkins and shepody,

and one knows that time is ruthless,

that thought can also be ruthless

When the arrogance or the drowsiness bewilder,

and the existence seems to be so wavy

as the clothes of the chalk beaten by the wind.
