invitation to poetry in motion: Augusto blanca | Friday 11 October

Leave comment

Poetry in motion, with Augusto Blanca

The Association of writers of the UNEAC and the Havana International Festival of poetry of are pleased to invite you to participate in your space poetry in motion. Guest: Augusto Blanca

Augusto Blanca. born in Banes, Holguin, the 24 of June of 1945. Founder of the Nueva Trova in 1972 and it has remained ever since as one of its principal exponents.

Linked to the theatre, He has won awards in action, direction and music. His name is attached to “Teatrova”, Cabildo theatrical Santiago, Puppet of Santiago of Cuba, Group “Okantomi” and Theatre Studio, among others. She has given concerts in Latin America, Europe, Africa and the Caribbean in different events of music and theatre. She has shared the stage with Víctor Jara, Joan Manuel Serrat, Daniel Viglietti, Victor Heredia, Angel and Isabel Parra, Nora white, Silvio Rodriguez, Pablo Milanés, Noel Nicola, Vicente and Santiago Feliu, Sara González, Miriam Ramos and Lazaro Garcia, and actors like María Eugenia García, Corina Mestre, Jacqueline Arenal, Yanara Moreno y Renecito de la Cruz.

Discography: “Gift” (1978); “Back” (1982); “A handful of seeds” (1986) and “This tree that we planted” (1999), Award special jury Festival Cubadisco Awards. Their songs have been included in anthologies of the Nueva Trova.
