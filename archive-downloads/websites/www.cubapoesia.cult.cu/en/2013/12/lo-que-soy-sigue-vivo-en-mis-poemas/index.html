what I am still alive in my poems

Categories:

delivery quick/fast delivery

By: Yoandy Cabrera | Madrid | 20 Dec 2013.

Dolphin Prast

The Delfin Prats poet reflects on his life and his poetry as a result of the critical edition of his work published by the Hypermedia publishing.

They met 45 anniversary of the publication of silent language, first poems of Dolphin Prats. As a fitting tribute, the Hypermedia publishing collects in one volume all his poetry written between 1968 and 2013. In the interview on 1968 make you La Gaceta de Cuba, Dolphin answered the question of who was: “what I am now is in my poems”. Currently reaffirms, by stating that “what I am, what I was, still alive in my poems”. This part it makes friends, readers and scholars who have contributed to his work is one of the most recognized in the Cuban cultural sphere, Despite the years in which censorship official he wanted to silence and disappear their poems.

This man passes, without any transition, self-identification to the appreciation and otherness as extensions of himself.

The last sentences of that interview of 1968 They seem today to reality; weather, his frank testimony given reason and the backing of the new reader, something pointed and persecuted since the proposed leave testimony “about the time that we live, about that story where gears of transition are projected towards those who they will justify them”. Part of that justification, confirmation of this story, they are words that follow:

Who is Jorge Iran? What differentiates you and you look like today it?

There is a desire for otherness. I suspect that with the new name trying to get me from the long hours sitting at the Bureau, translating texts far removed from my growing claims for making me a literary culture. My Covenant with the new name was short-lived. He later appears is Hiram, user ID under which the soldier with Bohemian leanings was hiding. There is an anecdote that I always remember and do it with much pleasure: I sent four or five poems of those who was writing to Gazette and one afternoon I get a call from Nicolás Guillén, who was interested in them and assured me his publication in the next issue of the magazine. That was more than what I would have expected, was very stimulating. The poems were ever published. Only one saw the light, the “Georgian song”, under the pseudonym of Iran Jorge. But it was gratifying and still today the Jorge Iran that has accompanied me throughout life official thanks to the poet for that friendly gesture. Yes, Obviously, the fictional character still live in which I am and suggests to me in a low voice a desire to everyday choose, between the plurality of things, the most beautiful. The self is the river heraclitiano of unstoppable creep.

Precisely in that number of ' La Gaceta’ appears ' Georgian song’ illustrated with a bare-breasted woman; However, in the poem refers to Kolia, a male first name.

Is there a real human Kolia reference, name that appears in this one which was, In addition, the first poem of 'Dumb language'?

Yes, as part of our training as language professionals, intended to teach it in the Caribbean preserves, He sent us to places where were alone among Russian-speaking. So it was like I went to a camp of pioneers in the vicinity of the city of Tula and was there where I met the teenager. We engaged a beautiful friendship that lasted until my return to Cuba. Then we do not write more. A poem with the same title appears in Lermontov, the great romantic poet. Georgian songs allude to Nhân, that is how they named this Republic of the Caucasus in Russian, where I had the privilege of being near the end of my stay in the USSR when I accompanied as interpreter to a delegation of Cuban engineers. The beauty of the place, unusual city and the memories of the abundance of fruit in the markets have accompanied me always.

With just 15 years participate in the Cuban literacy campaign of 1961. Any special memories of this period?

Many memories. It is especially the discovery of the wild and the coexistence of steep slopes and deep sea in the southern part of the Sierra Maestra. I won't hide that I am a romantic, but my stay in places with unusual beauty had eventually that yield their fruits. I had grown in the Plains and up to this moment only experienced had extensions in the plain. Then I discovered that there was another kind of landscapes. Also another kind of men, forged in difficulties. I learned many things, among them to milk the cows. This month stay at the Uvita, that is the name of the seaside resort where I alfabeticé, I enriched ostensibly.

What value do has on your life and your work stay in the USSR?

A great value. Note that I had grown up in the brief and then of coup was discovering the vastness, the endless. I also discovered the diversity of men, as in shelters lived with people from different countries. It was also the diversity of odors from foods that they prepared. I shook links, especially with my closest neighbors and my countrymen, a group of boys and girls who wanted to much and who I still have images in the pictures I have. In those years I was in possession of a great naivety and didn't see nothing threatening in the environment, everything seemed to follow a normal course, as if the historical current was one of those vast rivers that I had met during my stay in the mountains. On the other hand, and weird as it may seem, I began to discover my country from the USSR, through the magazines that came over us and conversations with those who already had knowledge of our culture. I began to run toward Cuba from that temporary point of view between soup of cabbage and travel by tram, things that seemed unusual to me at the beginning, but to which I got used then. Yes, Obviously, This stay of two and a half years in the USSR has a great importance forming in my nature, then in a formative State and without a doubt, He influenced what could later write.

What was your relationship with the Group El Puente and the first 'bearded Cayman'?

When I returned from the USSR and I joined the work of translator at a military unit, already the ediciones El Puente had closed. I was working eight hours and then - this is what I remember - slept in military unit. But the regime was not as rigorous as to not allow me to leave, Open me to the night. I started to frequent the Vedado area, the ramp and its surroundings. He also visited libraries, as he had already germinated in me that seed of desire by knowledge of the national culture that had Moscow origins. This is how I have gone, little by little, knowing the figures involved in the adventure of the bridge. I met many of which had been published in the publishing house and the most important thing is that I started with José Mario a relationship that was very positive for the enlargement of my horizon of cultural expectations. This teaching and discipleship friendship lasted until the departure of the poet to Europe. In that moment, in the heat of these talks, begin to problematized my relationships with the circumstance and it is then that the political implications of literature, and culture in general, they start for me to show their status as two-faced Janus. The knowledge of what they had been those issues and enter as the literary arena had made to be born in me nothing Orthodox concerns, I have to have then done a job of choosing between the trends of the moment and I have, in some way, chosen the path that was going to follow my writing, flatly rejecting the ad usum oversimplifying, I have - a little perhaps unconsciously - reasoned that poetry was the opposite of the historic speech and my writing could be a testament to my anguish and an affirmation of my personal memories, of my hesitation between the splendid of those nights and the unfathomable chaos of history.

I want to, before moving on to the next topic, refer me, even in step, another figure who greatly influenced my training, Reinaldo García Ramos, Another very important puentero, with whom early established a friendly relationship that lasts until today. Thank you, Reinaldo.

Paradoxical as it may seem, I, at that time, I read the poems that began to appear in the Bearded Cayman and I know some of the young people participating in this adventure. Seems that it exists as a dialectic in the cultural process that makes healthy relieved of groups and publications that represent them or, at least, fight each other while the blood reach the river. As for those poets, I've always regretted not having become friend of Wichy, It is obviously the outstanding figure of the Group. Different reasons prevented that friendship, homophobia among the main. Others went friend then and some I am friend still. I gave a tribute to the group in the 94 When, in the event the entire island, It was held that year in Madrid, I presented a paper entitled “We speak”, where I advocated - then still did - by the unity and diversity of Cuban poetry of two banks.

So what is the generic swing, variable use of the masculine and the feminine of one edit to another in your books and specifically in poems such as ' but in the wind your rumor came’ and 'don't you come back to places where you were happy'? Do you have any relationship with censorship or social homophobia?

The poems were originally aimed at a male subject. Then, When publishing them, I changed the gender of the referent, later have been published both in both ways and has had a positive. Yours do both boys and girls and enjoy them without that bias that can be generated in the initial homoerotic motivation. He would be pleased if this definitive Edition these poems to regain momentum, Greek in essence, singing an ephebe on the burning beaches of Santa Maria. There is something that I have never revealed and you trust now: the name of this collection was with the same intensity of an old, and no one better than you for rating a script that tries to emulate Theognis. So things are, colleague.

TO 45 years of award-winning silent language, What does this brief but intense collection of poems personally?

It is my life then and now, I get up, drink coffee, exit, walk, touring the city, return to the “site that is as well being”. In terms of specific writing, It is the support which supports everything I did after. I was someone to whom poetry was easy, but when it was, did rush. That is already in my first poems, in this booklet.

You are persecuting melt your intellectual concerns with the natural environment, is there a limit between one and the other?, differences you them?

Remember that in my childhood it was environment. There is a strong gravitation of weathering and helplessness in everything that came after. At the beginning it was only the hut and its surroundings, the finca, a stream surrounded by trees, what I call “forest”. Those terse entries have always accompanied me and until today no I could separate them from what they've done. Of course, later came other extensions, the beaches, the plain. Is between spaces and prospects which opens the night where my writing flows, the poems flow over the spaces, words and space delimiting the prospects of a lyrical subject that digs in the circumstance chasing response with agonizing or full voice of hope. I see no boundaries between the places where he studied my life and words that form it. I am those beaches, those night spaces, the plain of the Cauto, Gibara. Only my voice redefines the places, It adapts them to an always new appetite. It is an erotic dance where the body has the space as the last of the alternatives.

Your writing has a concision and a successful say in some texts, and while in others there is a cathartic indulgence, teogonico with pretensions of apprehending the environment, appoint in an only body 'the splendour and chaos'. How does this process in you as a creator, If you plan and intends to capture a thought through the word?

Write a poem is an act of magic. There is nothing at the beginning on page. But from the outside comes an avalanche that the poem is forced to order. There are happy occasions when the poem is written only, as dictated by a daimon. They are those texts that do not even have to be corrected. Forged in the interior light creatures, them esplenden on their own, in the heat of the beat. Sometimes the work is complicated. What will be the poem involves an enormous amount of material derived from different areas of experience, they are above all the relicts of the culture, that you have received readings, of the arts, cinema, the pictures you have ever seen. Not every cultural reference used when constructing a poem. In your editing you are forced to do without many, which open up new spaces only be admitted in the end. A poem thus seems more to a picture or a documentary than a conventional text where the voice of the words tries to be defined, autoconfesarse, fumbling with the contradictions. The result of this type of experience is exposed to great risk of permanence and reception. You can ruin the expectations of the best reader with this accumulation of disparate ingredients. But I think that I've often gone gracefully in this bold Enterprise. There are four rocks. I managed to say more things in those tumultuous texts that had never done in years of reflective writing. In the concise texts and write linear, congratulations. They are the texts that most use in public readings. Sales easily pass reading them for an audience of casual lovers of poetry. I reserve the chaotic texts to face a more demanding public readings. They are poems to poets and critics.

How do you understand the relationship 'poiesis' - nature so peculiar and important in your work?

The original nature of the creature is one with the cosmos. Participate in the social life blurs that weak point of cosmic light that is your Center. All the desires raised living is void if you could escape into the interior light that is one with the light in the center of the universe. The error here could be if you consider nature as an antagonistic otherness of your own being. Is in my poem “waters” This dialogue and interaction with the outdoors reaches its zenith. Here the lyric speaker seems to flow from a point in space to another fertilized by the Eros of the contemplation of the physical beauty and gives that body well-balanced and never reached a superhuman dimension. Spatial instances are intertwined with the coveted creature and finally give this poetical body, flowing and contradictory, also worthy of an old. It is a focused on the natural Eros, in the dance of the seasons, in the infinite. Poiesis and nature are inseparable in the best that I have given me so.

You recite your own verses like no one else, with a precision and clarity that is missing from the print editions. Relationship set between orality and your poetry?

This way of saying comes with one. I have a record and exploded it, especially if the occasion is worth. The great texts need not the voice of a declaimer, good to be, but the nuance or nuances that the author can bring. There are poets who leave us disappointed when we listen to them read their poems. Today a way of saying that it is sheer monotony is widespread, the text is perceived by the auditor as a bland sonsonete, There are few differences between this way of reading and how that would read a text in prose whatever. I am rooting for emphasis, by the old pathos, the poet then retrieves the capacity that the ancients had to take ownership of a scenario and to excite an audience. I have witnessed the reading of Russian poets, Turks, who do very well and although we, unfamiliar in these languages, the meaning of the words escape us, If we perceive the enormous musicality of those constructions. As for me, so when we say a text strongly, I feel that it is enriched, powered by the swell of a sonority and rhythm-laden expression. And it is that modern poetry ceased to be interested in the sound dimension, ingredient sine qua non of the lyrical genre since remote times. Is there anything else: I've always said, and I repeat it now, my poetry will only reach its optimal dimension when I can tell my poems with musical accompaniment, a String Quartet, at least a violin and a piano. This would contribute to the Symphonic dimension of some of my texts is enhanced to the Max, “waters”, for example, and the rocks.

You remember how the Havana night of your youth, that has also left its imprint on your verses?

At the beginning it was this small area of the ramp and small clubs. I had a free night and employed them in the sound dissipation, the entertainment of youth which consists of leaving, expand and sightseeing. It is certain that he could not visit two clubs in the same night, but the truth is that them remember almost all: a bar, the drinks, the singers who cheered the night. This lasted quite, and returning to the theme of the Ediciones El Puente and its people, It is possible that in those places I departiera with all or nearly all that remained. Since then were also beaches: Santa Maria, Guanabo. We went to rented houses and there continued conversations, readings, the literary debate. That relationship with the beaches lasted years, but they were always different beaches, the landscape changed, There were variations of period period. The bar which existed across the Bay, where the route of the journey's rule ended, I met him with Mario José, also the bars on the coast in old Havana. I remember an exciting name: Two Brothers Bar.

The years of “wave” they were mainly of open places, travels between the Carmel of road and the Hotel Nacional. I remember the groups, varied people, the hustle and bustle at night, laughter. This is the time that I recall with pleasure, many friends of both sexes, passengers love affairs, squeeze night until exhaustion and new beaches, Sunrise in Santa Maria. There was a whole stretch seaside village with huts of canvas that we rented. How? With what money? I could not say. All I know is coming there to finish all that hustle and caminadera vedadense. In the years of the wave my attendance of the small bars declines in favor of our expansion to the beaches. Many years later I get back for years the beaches. I got a job in the tourist company and enjoyed the sea permanently. It was a cumbersome and little paid work in the dining room of a hotel for workers, But let me free day every two. Beach with all its splendor and sensual incentives was mine. Havana night was distant and was, Obviously, another. Although still had not reached the dollarization, Vedado clubs were more expensive, and they were no longer within reach of my Pocket. It's my time of greater proximity with my famous coterraneo Reinaldo Arenas. He was not a drinker, the bars should not have never interested, nor is it connected in no time with wave, their interests were others, We were visiting people and places, We ate in a restaurant. Clara Morera in the Wall Street House and the Episcopal Church of Vedado are the places that occupy the foreground in my memory. And again the sea, of course. In summary, my long relationship with Havana, day and night, beaches for many years and influenced greatly in what then and later I could write. The cities are traps, but they offer outings that afternoon or early us launch into unusual spaces, in the light and brightness of enduringly settled in memory locations.

In 1981 You definitely return to Holguin, City in which you still live. What mean Holguin for you?

There is a kind of destiny that binds you to certain places and compared them, you have two options: you abandon them and rehaces your circumstances or you stay in them. I chose to stay or perhaps karmic force superior to my will imposed me the permanence. Holguin was a strong attraction in my childhood, was the village, where it had to go through almost everything, for everything which does not occur in the finquita or not I could buy from street vendors, that you were then already lot. Then, my link with Holguin begins from childhood. My grandmother lived in the city, in one of its suburbs. We were going to the Carnival. Also lived in her other relatives that we visited. Then came high school that I attended as an intern in two different places, seventh and eighth. Already the ninth I studied in Havana. After the USSR. On my way back the changes already glimpsed, Perhaps some of the missed progress; It had improved the transportation between my neighborhood's birth and childhood and Holguin.

I cannot write about these things unless a sadness indesterrable dips, threatening to stay with me. Is for non-permanence, by creep of time hauling losses. I went to study at Havana, I traveled to the USSR, I was working in Havana, but always there was the joy of the return. My relationship with Holguin continued and has been prolonged until today. Periods here, years beyond, always returning. There's a moment when my mother moved to Holguin, leaving my father and my brothers on the farm. My time at Holguín's time with my mother. Comes a return of fairly long permanence that is when I enter into contact with the cultural environment, where was the emergence of the eighties already brewing, in which I participate with full. In the 1980s I'm already full time citizen, I take part in the literary movement, they publish in the Havana to celebrate the rise of Icarus, awarded me the critics Prize, I traveled to the USSR to the Pushkin Festival with Dr. Luis Álvarez Álvarez. From this moment the publications occur one after the other, public always the same poems in books that are completed by new texts that were joining a work-in-progress. What was it that characterized Holguin as literary development center? A desire to emulate, compete with Havana and other cities of the country at a time when literary creation grew the warmth of an authentic cultural opening after years of stagnation that shape what historiography calls as a “Gray quinquennium”, It lasted, Obviously, more than five years. Holguin was - and still is - the headquarters of important events, its writers were awarded in contests, they were involved in activities in the capital and other cities. And I was going with them, I was reading my poems to audiences of young people who were on my work and person a partial knowledge. I went from black sheep of the seventies to be one of the creators that new generations welcomed then without hesitation.

Holguin, In addition, opens onto its municipalities. I participated as a juror in literary events of these small cities. Some are rooted in the course of my memories: Gibara, Banes (It demands to be headquarters of the birth of Gaston Baquero), Mayarí, MOA.

Many of those who are now my best friends participated in this cultural adventure, I do not quote their names because I've avoided these conversations include living persons. Yes I can tell you that the ties that bind me to the creators holguineri - absent and present - are solid and very rewarding. The city is also my very dear, many times I welcome the fact that my city cavafica, that which persecutes you wherever you are and wherever you go.

What value had personally reunited in Madrid in? 1994 with authors such as Manuel Díaz Martínez and Heberto Padilla and interaction with Gaston Baquero?

That event is the further stimulus that I've had since my early days in writing and until today. I went to Madrid certainly inhibited by so many brilliant names that would share in the Conference. Already arriving in Havana I sensed that this was a very important meeting for writers that would travel. In Madrid it became clear that the thing was going big. The newspaper El Pais published my picture among frontline figures: photos of Lezama, Baquero and Padilla me above. “An inevitable political controversy marks the meeting of Cuban poets in Madrid”, read the holder. The event was prodigal in pleasant surprises. The figures, I said it, they were first-line, above all, and foremost the presence of Gaston Baquero, then a living myth, bound by birth to Holguin. Díaz Martínez was closely linked to Holguin, He was one of the members of the legendary “Generation of the 50” most visited us, providing advice to the fledgling literary movement of the province. With Heberto linking me a friendship born from my prize in the 68. We must not forget that it was his wife then, Belkis Cuza Malé, who gave one of the two votes to my book. She was not at the meeting in Madrid, but my encounter with Padilla was warm during the days we were and we shared daily. I made copies of books of the best poets of the holguineros delivery.

As for Baquero, I therefore welcomed with love. I always do the story of our last meeting, already completed the event, next an estanquillo of newspapers and magazines. I was in a car with Queen María Rodríguez and a friend; We saw Gaston alongside the estanquillo, I seem to recall that he browsed a magazine, I got off and went straight up to the, to say goodbye. He bought me a journal, He handed it to me and there we said goodbye. It was the last time that I saw him. However, his teachings still accompanies me. To return to my country, I was transformed by those dazzling days. It was the realization of a dream.

Receiving the award David, afirmabas: 'what I am now is in my poems', is it still so?, how much and how that idea has changed until today??

All that is contradictory in a man is in his writing. That first book served to demonstrate to myself that I could fight with poetry and beat it. They came after years of multiple life, assimilation of knowledge, hundreds of readings, thousands of days to accumulate under the Sun, looking for your configuration in the words. What I am, what I have been, still alive in my poems, It has aged exterior appearance, but the heart, brain or whatever, therein lies where my most intimate essence, remains and will remain present, with overriding force, in my experience as a poet.

How defined today Dolphin Prats?

Someone who has managed to pass through the barriers and age valladar. I have been taken from one place to another, from one city to another by the Blizzard of circumstances. I have established relationships of friendship and camaraderie with many people, my Facebook page is filled with the faces of those friends who have been my biggest gain over years. To summarize these conversations with you, very expensive editor, I would like to take this opportunity to greet my friends from the world again and to thank them for their regular. I've rejected and reject the poison and bad intension from wherever it comes and I have struggled to achieve a space, a place, a high place for writing and magic. I consider myself a lucky: publishing houses and magazines have opened their doors to me, anthologies I have hosted. I want to thank for this, foremost, the Ministry of culture of my country, one of the few countries where the culture is among the priorities as an everyday food. I also thank the UNEAC, in whose editorial I posted some of my books. I thank the workers of culture and of my hometown UNEAC (Holguin) for their permanent support, the light Publishing House for publishing my books and editions Holguin, to the AHS for sponsoring a disc with my voice Edition: The brightness of the surface. I should also mention my appearance in documentary films. The directors of those movies my thanks. And to the editors and translators who published anthologies with my texts: Alex Pausides, Luis Aguilar, Fabio Aristimunho and Lourdes González. My texts, poured into Portuguese by Fabio, they exceed in beauty to the originals. Is also the translation of the splendor and chaos Italian that my friend made the Paduan poet Francesco Manna. Thank you, Francesco. I term these lines, with them I conclude by responding to your questionnaire, expressing yourself the most sincere and warm appreciation for your edition of this book that brings everything that I have written over more than four decades. It is true that I would have liked to get rid of some texts, but, In short, this is a book conceived and carried out by you. ES, in a nutshell, your work, my poetry gathered for you. For you, Repeat, my most fervent appreciation.
