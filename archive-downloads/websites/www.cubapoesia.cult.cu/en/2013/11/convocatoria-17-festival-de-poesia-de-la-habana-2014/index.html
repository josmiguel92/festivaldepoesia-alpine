call 17 international poetry festival of Havana, 2014

Categories:

Calls for proposals, delivery quick/fast delivery, Festival

The 17 International poetry festival of Havana under the auspices of the Union of writers and artists of Cuba, the Office of the city historian, the Ministry of culture, the Cuban book Institute, the Center for Exchange and reference. Community initiative (CIERIC), Charity Andalusian society, the Asia-iberoamerica Foundation of Bogotá and WPM, will be made of the 25 to the 31 may of 2014.

 

Main activities

Word in the world, simultaneous reading in cities of the planet. (6-25 may)

Itinerant CubaPoesía: Reading in all provincial capitals (6-25 may)

Colloquium The work of Nancy Morejon in its 70 anniversary. (26 may)

Elbows on the bridge, on the bicentenary of José Jacinto Milanés.

Be faithful: Tribute to Samuel Feijóo on their 100th birthday.

Seminar Words written in the sand by an innocent, on the centenary of Gaston Baquero.

Inaugural evening: Minor Basilica of the convent of San Francisco de Asís, La Habana Vieja (27 may)

The award "Rafael Alberti". (27 may)

Meeting of managers of international festivals of poetry. (27-28 may)

"Poetry and citizen in the current time action meeting: Women for gender integration". (27-28 may)

Laboratory of writings ( 27-30 may)

Cabildo of orality (28-30 may)

CUBAPoesía itinerant in the provinces, municipalities, communities, schools and tobacco shops.(28-30 may)

Presentation of the "Wolsan" award-CUBAPoesía". (30 may)

Concerts: Basilica of san francisco, National Museum of fine arts, Teatro Mella, Theater Bertolt Brecht

Closing: Concert of the Camerata Romeu, in the minor Basilica of the convent of San Francisco de Asís (31 may)

 

CubaPoesía Itinertante:

Provincial headquarters: Pinar del Rio, Isle of youth, Artemis, Mayabeque, Matanzas, Villa Clara, Cienfuegos, Sancti Spiritus, Ciego de Ávila, Camagüey, Holguin, Las Tunas, Bayamo, Manzanillo, Niquero, Pilon, Santiago de Cuaba, Guantánamo.
