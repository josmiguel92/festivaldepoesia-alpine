remembering fayad jamis

11 November, 201314 November, 2013 Cubapoesíathe shift of the offended, delivery quick/fast delivery

Just when the great Cuban poet Fayad Jamís would be 83 years, It will be unveiled Tuesday 12 November to the 6:00 PM, a commemorative tarja or building and 27, where lived much of his life.

Friends and admirers of his poetic and pictorial work will meet there, convened by the International Festival of the poetry of Havana, under the auspices of the Office of the city historian.

Poets and actors will give his voice to Fayad Jamis in a project long-cherished by journalist Andres Castillo Bernal.

Pablo Armando Fernández and the Group of Buscón theatre and the Mimoclam actors Theatre will give shelter Tuesday to prominent Cuban intellectual disappeared now 25 years.

Fayad Jamis was born in Zacatecas in 1930. Remember it is to celebrate one of the most intense of Cuban poetry and original voices.
