The 19 and 20 November in Medellin

Between the 19 and 20 November will be held in Medellín the international encounter of poetry China-Colombia The dragon in the Valley of poetry, organized by the Festival Internacional de Poesía de Medellín, the China ZhongKun company and the WPM.

Luo Ying poets, Tang Xiaodu, Zhang Qinghua, Lin Mang, Have Zhuorong, Jiang Tao and Yang Ke; and writers Zhang Ling, Yi Ling and Wang Guangming, of the People's Republic of China together with the Colombian poets Juan Manuel Roca, Guillermo Martínez González, Rafael Patiño, Samuel Vasquez, Jairo Guyzmán and Luis Eduardo Rendón will take part in the event, It will consist of six readings of poems and two Chinese writers with public talks.

The meeting is part of a cycle of international poetry readings that seeks to promote understanding between the poets and poetic traditions of the peoples, in a city that is considered to be a world capital for poetry, with an extremely sensitive public to the highest form of language.

Entrance to all events is free. The meeting will be held with the support of the municipality of Medellín, the municipality of Envigado, Trust cooperative box, Coofinep, the Confucius Institute, Telemedellín and Publik.

It is the first visit to Latin America and Colombia a wide of poets and writers of China delegation, you will only have contact with the public in Buenos Aires (Argentina) and in Medellin. The planned schedule is as follows:

Poems by three Chinese poets participating in the big event scheduled in Medellin.

 

LUO YING

LUO YING

Born in Lanzhou, China. He has lived and worked in Beijing and California, United States. He is a poet and mountain climber (He has climbed Mount Kilamanjaro, Muztahgata, Aconcagua and Chou Oyu, among other peaks on five continents and has made expeditions to the North Pole and the South Pole). He is Vice-President of the Institute of research of the poetry China contemporary of Beijing University; It also is a member of the Association of writers of China and director of the poetry society of China. His poetry publications include: The Green Tara Series; Empty Glasses and Empty Tables; 7+2 Mountain Climber completo Journal; Diary of an Educated Youth and Water - Charms. He received the literature prize of the Association of writers of Mongolia in 2008. His poems have been published in English, French, Spanish and Japanese.

FATHER

A frayed skin of deer in the forest

I suspect that it is the skin of my father

After three hundred years of meditation, just got up and went

The deerskin has been worn by the weather since then

And as there is no mother who order their lives

He probably picked up a useful black bear skin

and with the appearance of a bear he hunted and fished

He never answered any of the philosophical questions of history

Secretly he amassed rocks to spray them

He quickly uprooted entire forests

It fermented wine of a Lake 300 years later, drunk a drink

He then drew his long sword and chopped five great mountains

He slept a hundred years without blinking

and not once did my name

He says you have forgotten my childhood nickname

At the time that I made a wild boar

 

JIANG TAO

JIANG TAO

Born in Tianjin, China, in 1970. He studied biomedical engineering at Tsinghua University and obtained a doctorate in China modern literature at the University of Beijing. He currently teaches at the Department of Chinese and literature of the University of Beijing. He was editor of the popular magazines of poetry Poetry Communications and Offset. His first book, Bird Sutras, It was published in 2005. He also published books Four poems and Mourning for Sometimes. He won the prize Liu Li'an of poetry in 1997.

PALAEOPITHECUS TRIBE

 

the forest was full of fallen fruit, a scarlet rug

whose origin lies in the geological change

the water had receded, Saber Tiger tooth was rotten

around the empty mud we discussed the future

the old freshly dragged from the evolution,

hoisting his old fists

young people already could not remain silent, they had gotten

be the first to eat sika deer: the ambition to move

a mountain, with deficiencies,

While they could cross the river, to the North and the South

the camps were only a dining table

the so-called Republic, very noisy,

However, the despotism of the fall away flies and mosquitoes

Fortunately we were all standing,

able to see the stars, fight with empty hands to free ourselves

of the food chain

but the October labour force

still leaning towards the surplus: There is no need to paint our skin,

or Cook

with meat for firing, only the males remained

putting head female, singing that beauty

until, at least, with spring flowers and autumn moon,

say ' I love you’ aloud it would take still two million years

YANG KE

YANG KE

He is a poet and editor Chinese. He was born in Guangxi Province, but since 2008 was established in Guangzhou. He published the 1998 Yearbook of China's New Poetry. He is the Vice President of the Asociación de Escritores de Guangdong. This year he participated in the Icelandic-Chinese Poetry Festival. One of his best-known poetry books is Relevant and Irrelevant (2009).

He has been University Professor, He has given lectures on “Appreciation of the new poetry”, in compounds of secondary and University. He has traveled to gatherings and conferences in Japan, Korea and many other countries.. He has won sixteen major awards, among them the first prize in Guangxi Province.

CHANGE OF TIME IN SUMMER

 

Trains depart ahead of time

Mature girls

Before time birthday

Candles are extinguished

And in a well dithering murder

A glowing knife comes clean

And spotting of blood

Ahead of time

 

However chicks refuse to break its shell

The failure to be fully bright moon

As usual at midnight

 

However a realistic writer running on a morning Street

The first bus has killed it

He started ahead of time

Then the Black Humor and the school of the absurd

They have been included at the end

 

And the guy who goes to the appointment to the old place

Old when he meets another girl

The cremated dead

They got a wrong age report

And since one hour of Sun and air

It was stolen them while they warned him, the men are stunned

Is it just the time?
