Serafina Núñez

By: Julieta Garcia Rios / Juventud Rebelde / 4 September of 2004.
I come from being alone
with the Goblin inhabiting me,
my blood went to the appointment
with his dragon and its waves.
I come from being alone.
I cancel name and saeta;
the quiescent candle my fire
night of the named Goblin,
are the time
peace and the secret light.

Serafina Núñez. Cuban poet, It is one of the most powerful and representative of Cuban poetry of the 20th century female voices. He received in 1995 the national award of the Literary criticism. Mercedes Serafina Núñez de Villavicencio y Ortiz was born in Havana the 14 August of 1913. He graduated from College of pedagogy, at the University of Havana in 1949. His literary career began in the hands of the Spanish poet Juan Ramón Jiménez, who included her in the Cuban poetry in 1936 and it strand with her a sincere friendship. Precisely, It would cover costs of the first book of Núñez, Sea captive (1937), and prologaría vigil and secret (1942). After more than 30 years without publishing, in 1992 the successive kingdoms book saw the light, Edition tribute to the author. Since then anthologies on the serene banks stand out (1998), Land of secret transparency (2004), and selected sonnets, in posthumous Edition. He died in Havana the 14 of June of 2006.

Been to Serafina Núñez as to those beings that suddenly felled him its landscape and don't know what to do, How to inspire you. “Here I've been able to write. I don't feel in my sleepless unification. Is the secret of my house, of my room, My mystery where I create”, I confess this woman's 91 years now inhabiting the House of her daughter, where has gone to be cared for after having fallen off their feet. Serafina transmits peace. A huge peace. Fifteen minutes of conversation enough to feel that know she has always. It is nice to talk to this woman who gives off a faint smell and remember the odorous insistence of the nocturnal flowers. Lying on the sofa in the room of her daughter Mercedes, in his memory.

  • ¿How it started in poetry?

With the arrival of Juan Ramón Jiménez to Cuba. He called on all those who wrote verses, they were trying to be or believed to be poets, because he had the purpose of starting an anthology of Cuban poetry.

“The project involving Camila Henríquez Ureña and doctor José María Chacón and Calvo, both aided by the Hispanocubana institution of culture led by Don Fernando Ortiz.
“I had some made poems and presented seven of them. When we met with Juan Ramon at the Lyceum of Havana, He said to me: ' You are poet, Serafina and am going to take his seven poems to the anthology of Cuban poetry we are going to do '.

“On the terrace of the Hotel Vedado, where he stayed, We talked while never assume poses of Professor. They were friendly dialogues. We talked about Spain, poetry.

“Then came the publication of my first book in 1937, Captive sea, a small book, not very profuse, and for which my friends helped me, especially Juan Ramon, who put his own money which at that time was scarce.

“In 1938 arrived in Cuba Gabriela Mistral. I went to receive it with all the people interested in being with her and his poetry. I remember that on the deck of the ship that brought it to the island were journalists and outstanding poets, with a recognized work, and I kept a little remote because I seemed still had no track record to get to chat with Gabriela.

“She saw me and told me: ' Here comes my girl; Why removed me both?’. I had in my hands my second book which had just published, Island dream. She took it and gave me the address of the guesthouse near the tip, where would be hosted.

“Gabriela took my affection. I wanted to visit it every day. I wrote a letter that was published in the journal in the year 1939. It was a letter that exalted, He told me so many good things about my poetry, I felt very optimistic with what was doing. Already I felt safe with the opinion of Juan Ramon. Gabriela once asked me to accompany her to visit Juan Ramon. He received us, especially when she, with a very warm hug. She offered to children orphaned by the war all rights that give him for his book logging, next to publish. Aguaron to it up to the eyes. They were very cordial relationship despite the legend to the people that they not treated.

“Naturally I was learning, It would feel more secure. After Juan Ramon prefaced me my new book: Vigil and secret (1941)”.

  • He has expressed that he began to be afraid the literary environment after your fourth book, as the political situation in the country led to a bad promotion.

The fourth book, Landscape and Elegy, of 1956, with a foreword by Luis Alberto Sánchez, It was released in Mexico in the Excelsior newspaper printers.

“It was not fear as what I felt. Rather I realized that everyone was in a kind of rina. All were trying to push, as one who says, the screens of the publishers.

“That atmosphere did not like me. I kept writing but it did in the silence of my house, unveiled, because I've always written early in the morning. I I wake up at midnight. It is when the ideas come and I write”.

  • What is the secret between you and the night?

I feel like I came a huge peace at the same time I will be forged in the mind images and themes that will shape my poetry.

  • At some point he reviewed that it was more linked to the painters of his time as the poets. What was this?

Is not exactly most linked but had more painters friends poets. Although I was a friend of Florit and Ballagas, and right Santo Rodriguez, My encounters with the painters were more frequent. I was visiting the Círculo de Bellas Artes where they were. There we talked about painters, poets and poetry.

  • How is your relationship with Fina García Marruz and Cintio Vitier?

Since we met we feel sympathy. Ours has been a friendship always, because we learned to adapt to the circumstances that had made us the Group of friends of Juan Ramon. Without praise or fatuidades, We could say that for me fine and Cintio are the marriage model of the Cuban intellectual movement. They are two in one.

  • I understand that you modeled so that Valderrama painted one of the images of the ovals of the room of the Chamber of the Capitol. Is it true?

Yes. It was exactly in the same circle of fine arts. Valderrama, relevant artist who directed the Academia de San Alejandro, worked very quickly. He asked me that I wanted to paint me torso, rather the face.

“Then a painter who decorated the American churches of German origin, who resided in the Hotel Inglaterra, He also asked me to serve as her model and I accepted. But I made tontada living a week without prompting when leaving, so he left and did not let me portrait”.

  • What were they in his face?

Good, They said that I had the face of the typical Cuban, the brunette classic. It did not wear glasses at that time…
It was presumed…
Yes, really it was very vain. Maybe that is not good, but I went.

  • Is it still it?

It is like a custom one…

  • He lived more than three decades without posting to it never stopped writing. Why do??

A bit of discouragement by loving and family problems. And another little because the atmosphere did not seem conducive. I didn't like how things were developing. After Luis Suardíaz was the gesture of search my poems and make a small anthology published in 1992. Since then I went back to join the literary world. Then, When I wrote Stained glass of time, awarded me the national prize of the critics in 1996.

  • What did mean that for you?

To realize that I was actually with my voice in a good route of poets. Because always influences I had at the time. But despite that my voice is rather different.”As for the sonnet, Fine once said: 'Serafina is sonnet women'. Dedication to the sonnet is not very common”.

  • Why it says that?

The sonnet is a very difficult thing. We must lock in the 14 verses thinking and give a finish to not let it down.

“I'll give an example: When I worked at the public school of Guanabacoa was a well. It was dry. In its rim stood a bird which in my mind I represented it to me as a Nightingale. And so I called him. The bird looked for my classroom and sang and sang. Thus arose the sonnet to a Nightingale dawning on the vigilance of my night.

  • Returning to the nearly four decades without publishing. What is the silence for the poet?

Actually I didn't feel the silence because I expressed although it was not to publish. I knew that at some point you could share it. I had this hope.

  • Serafina is a recognized but not well known poetess…

I am a person of meditation rather than a racket.

  • What is your concept of poetry?

Is like the wing of a bird, very soft, We have the front and makes us feel full.

  • And the poets?

Very sensitive and very desire people of their homeland, light, of the beauty of life. Although they suffer many frauds and many illusions fade, and many wounds hurt.
What is the homeland for Serafina?
The place that I can never forget. Where I am a participant of all its secrets, its mysteries and their fight.

  • Once advised the young poets that they entered themselves…

I do not believe in the surrounding area you can find the force that has to save the poetry. If you do not feel a while to think, to meditate, to study your feelings, what you would do, how you will be able to move forward.

  • For you the main topics of the man are love, death and life in general. Why do??

Love makes life pleasant because it gives the substance that life needs. Death is inevitable; We are not owners of our lives, God has and in an instant we lose it. “The life of each man is a beautiful venue that holds the secret that sometimes we do not conceive it may be beauty, goodness, intelligence, creation…”.

  • At the height of his? 91 years can tell us what is the best way to live the life?

Having understanding for all, believing in our own forces, and trying to exert a beneficial influence on others.

  • If asked who is Serafina Núñez, what I would say?

A dreamy woman who lived the dream and still dreaming.

A Nightingale dawning

Sweet Lord of the realm who falls in love with
inventing the statue of the sleepless
by the endless water where flight is already
the granada departure of its aurora.

To the alcove of implores God?
wounded that sky diamond
dripping on your throat?…What high
your song moves at brasa, flows and dora,

perfect Alba in unheard music,
and supports the ideas of dew
and it stops the death at its discretion?

An angel in your voice raises its Choir
and dwells in the serene banks,
in pure snow spilled gold.
