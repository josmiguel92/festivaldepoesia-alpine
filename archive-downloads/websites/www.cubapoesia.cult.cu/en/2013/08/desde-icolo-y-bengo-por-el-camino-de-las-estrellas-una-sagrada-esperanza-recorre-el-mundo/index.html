Aug 27 2013

from ícolo and bengo, the path of the stars, sacred hope travels the world

Categories:

The Tower of Babel

By: Jesus Sama Pacheco.

Sacred hope

 

It is an honour for me, and also a considerable challenge, introduce the book today Sacred hope, Antonio Agostinho Neto. A man who was a paradigm of its time and is, and it will be a symbol of struggle for present and future generations, loyalty, of love and commitment to poetry and his people. Luckily for Angola, of Africa and of all peoples who yearn for peace and total independence, was born the 17 September, in a village of Ícolo and Bengo region, some 60 kilometers from Luanda.

I shall first deal with the net figure as an individual, as a fighter, leader and, At last, as a poet. Although I know that these aspects are inseparable in a man who put to the service of his people, in the fight for their freedom and independence, and subsequent progress, all your love, all their energy and all their talent.

Those who knew him closely, they loved it and fraternize with him at various times, We speak of love that he felt for humanity, his modesty and audacity. He was a man who knew the smiling face of danger, He knew no fear or doubt. It held no hatred in his heart. Suffered firsthand racism, turned it on anti-racism. It was for many the father of all Angolan children and the son of all Angolan mothers. But that delivery by his people did not prevent that you attend their most intimate affections

various obligations. According to Marga Holness (from whom I've taken many of the data listed here, as well as Basil Davidson and Lúcio Lara, one of his brothers in the long struggle that held, and Davidcherician, late Cuban writer who translated for the Cuban reader the Sacred hope), NET was able to be a child, father, brother, spouse, friend, affable family in whom one could always find a warm word.

Visionary leader, very early he joined youth and student organizations, militating in several of these, in which it deployed a hard work, in Angola, as student of medicine at Coimbra stage and, later, in Lisbon. Its prestige and sagacity, among other qualities, they do that to become representative of the youth of the Portuguese colonies along with a Portuguese youth movement, the juvenile MUD. In Luanda, How many young people passed through its office of the working-class district were transformed, under his guidance, dedicated Patriots and prominent leaders of the struggle of the Angolan people.

In 1952, with maritime workers, He founded the African Maritime Club, pulley transmission between the Patriots Angolan in Portugal and which, in Angola, they created the conditions of the liberation movement. It was founded in 1958, together with Amílcar Cabral, Marcelino of the Saints and other youth in the Portuguese colonies, the anti-colonialist movement (MAC), that work in order to build national awareness of young people and, at the same time, denounce the fascist nature of the Portuguese colonialism outside Portugal.

By your incessant power in the struggle for liberation of his people, NET suffered several prisons. The first in 1951, had then just 29 years, When preparing to participate in the plenary session for peace in Stockholm. The second imprisonment took place in February of 1955 and held for 28 months. His release came in June of 1957 product of an international campaign in defence of their freedom and their life, that already was in immense danger in the hands of the requests (Portuguese political police, known for its brutal methods of repression that made her comparable with the Gestapo in Germany I was born. One of those who raised their voice, next important French writers, as Jean-Paul Sartre, and the Mexican muralist Diego Rivera, was the Cuban poet Nicolás Guillén. By that time, NET was already recognized as the most important poet and one of the most famous revolutionaries of Angola. His third arrest and imprisonment takes place the 8 of June of 1960. The director of the requests went personally to stop net in its office in Luanda.

The 4 February's 1961, distinguished date for the Angolan people, the prisons in Luanda were assaulted by men armed with machetes and some firearms. What is known as the first action of the armed struggle. Some police officers died in that confrontation. The next day the colonial authorities issued false news about an alleged plan to attack the funeral of policemen who died during the assault, followed by a massacre which killed some three hundred staff. He continued it this something much more horrendous, that raised the death toll to more than thirty thousand. Among other criminal actions against the people, grouped people and a buldocer passed him over, villages are bombed, strafing is and to those who sought to escape such genocide attack with napalm.

In those days the news and graphic testimonies of such barbarity toured the world. Precisely, by showing a photograph to a group of friends in Cape Verde, that appeared a group of young soldiers Portuguese smiling to the camera, one of them with a stake that had impaled the head of an Angolan, It is that net is imprisoned for fourth time, in October of 1961. Was then launched another international campaign for his release. Already recognized net as a representative figure of the nationalist awakening in Portuguese Africa, the voices of leading intellectuals of the world they feared rose again for her life. His poems had already been translated into seven languages, appearing in literary magazines and anthologies, alerting people of many countries on forced and miserable living conditions imposed by the colonialists to the Angolan people. The MPLA launched a vigorous international campaign for the release of Agostinho Neto and other Angolan political prisoners. The fascist authorities were forced to release NET in May of 1962. The Amnesty International had recognized it in 1961 the political prisoner of the year.

His unwavering confidence in success, in the victory, which was intrinsically linked to the masses, It took him to reach the objectives at each time. He created schools struggle for the people. During the restructuring of the MPLA, he was appointed Honorary President of. And in December of 1962, in the first National Conference of the movement, is elected President. Create the second and the third Military Region, showing convincing signs of boldness to imperialist maneuvers, in 1963.

NET was chosen repeatedly by African liberation movements to be its spokesperson at conferences of the Organization of African States and the non-aligned. In August of 1974 creates the FAPLA (Popular liberation of Angola armed forces) and in October of that year, during the second war of national liberation, After the agreement of truce with Portugal, the personality of Dr. Antonio Agostinho Neto reaches consideration from all over the world as a statesman.

Now approach more his personality as a poet. He has said on more than one occasion, that net poetry has its historical roots in the long tradition of patriotic Angolan literature. Many important voices, including those of Pereira and Cordeiro de la Mata poets are, They bequeath that tradition would be somehow post-conflict poets. On the other hand, Mário de Andrade sets the birth of Angolan literature in 1901, with the publication of the book The voice of Angola crying out in the wilderness.

Affricate National League, founded at the beginning of the second decade of the 20th century, arises the Association of natives of Angola, that begins to edit in 1948 the literary magazine Message. It was a group of writers in this viewfinderload, among them were Mario Antonio, Viriato de la Cruz and Antonio Jacinto, they gave concrete expression to the cultural renaissance operated in the colonies and native students that were enrolled in studies in Portugal. We will discover Angola It was the unifying cry of these writers. Poetry became the main vehicle with which they sought to re-establish contact with the roots of his people — after long stages of censorship and persecution-, writing for the people, doing it with your own voice, creating a modern national literature that give legitimate expression to the sufferings and aspirations of that people. The colonial authorities, before such threat, They chased and eventually ban the magazine. It then continued in Portugal, where had a group of students and intellectuals of the colonies founded a centre of African Studies in the early years of the Decade of the fifties. The founders of this Center were Agostinho Neto, Francisco José Terreiro (poet of Sau Tome), Amílcar Cabral and Mario Andrade. The Cultural Society of Angola in Luanda and the House of the writers of the Empire, in Lisbon, they became centers of the activity developed in the newspaper Culture and the new magazine Message. NET and other members of his generation also became contributors to these publications.

During another onslaught of fascist colonial power, Antonio Jacinto and Landino Viera were arrested and sentenced to 14 years in prison. In authentic challenge to censorship and the fascist politics, Marga Holness and Carlos Erverdosa got, sponsored by the House of the writers of the Empire, compile and publish small volumes with works by the most important intellectual angolanos. The booklet of poems of net gave the possibility to verify as they were of close ties between the people and the poet, and the wide acceptance of his poetry. In a single afternoon, the entire Edition was almost entirely absorbed by the inhabitants of the capital musseques. NET poetry had become the town flag.

Sacred hope, label that should replace the first editions: With dry eyes, It is superior for its strength and emblematic image. Frase-Templo, Frase-Fortaleza, in extraordinary synthesis reveals the character of the author, his actions as a leader and fighter, the individual in all their dimensions, identifying for ever the regio poet that was and is Agostinho Neto, who deserves to be read and remembered by all values containing his work, both from the point of view of intellectual, literary, as a human.

Some have wanted to pigeonhole the work of net with nonsense title political poetry. Well we know that, somehow, All literature is political. The literature of entertainment, for example, It avoids conflicts of society where is brewing, also, by default, is political literature. In a serious study, deep, on the poetry of Agostinho Neto, on medium and circumstances in which it was created, carefully observing all its nuances, There are other more important aspects which point. In the preface to the Edition in Portuguese, published by the Angolan writers Union back to the physical disappearance of net, Basil Davidson proclaims: his poems are beautiful, finely worked. And is that in a truly critical analysis, We observe that the poetics of net is not rigorously topologic chaining or the joint, multiplicity of images and the use of an essentially metaphorical language. The poems of net, in most of the cases, offer us a comprehensive picture, achieved through a wise construction of poem, aided by the synthesis, the rhythm and the blunt force of his word. Word that springs from the Earth, heart, the blood of the poet and becomes with the hearts of the people communicating artery, It recognizes it as his genuine voice: the voice of the musseques, villages, factories, schools; the voice of life, centuries of suffering and oppression, and also, the voice of the definitive awakening.

Most ancient poems of the book we are presenting today date from 1945, When was the poet 23 years, and most were written during the Decade of the 50 and many in the prison during the year 1960. First published in Italy, in 1963, It was also released later in Yugoslavia in a bilingual edition - Portuguese and Serbo-Croatian-, In addition to publications in Russian, Chinese, Spanish and other languages. In 1970, during the 4th. Afro-asiaticos Writers Conference, He was granted to net the Lotus Award, in recognition of the prominent place occupied by his poetry in the world revolutionary literature.

Nighttime is the most recurrent theme in his first poems. But not sweetened as something mystical night, romantic, haven of peace and calm; but overnight in the musseques, in the villages, in places where people were living desperate lives in miserable huts.

Others of the keys of his poetry are in the words hope, certainty, Victoria, light, love. It is not poetry, as has been said, that it leads to complacency, but from an objective poetry that goes straight to the essence, It relies on an infallible using the exact word. It is said that Neto is a poet deeply Angolan, recognized as one of the most important symbols of the angolanidad, and deeply African, a poet of universal stature.

At last, I want to tell you one of my most eloquent experiences related to the issue that we are trying to. In Angola, at the end of the Decade of the 70, I had the ability to feel with my eyes, with my ears, with my chest, the deep affection and admiration that he felt the Angolan people by Agostinho Neto. I was surprised by his physical death, together with a group of my work mates, in the center of the country. Immediately, a part of the procession departed for Luanda; the other, in which I was, He toured several provinces and cities during the day the funeral. It was an experience that will never be erased from my memory. On every street, in each sidewalk, Road, site where we rode, the deep and resounding silence of the penalty could be heard, pain, the wound that he felt in his heart every man and woman, each child, each elder. The people we saw were standing, upright, solemn. They wept, I can assure you that they wept. But it was a cry, a huge cry, without tears, with dry eyes. Era, unquestionably, the affection, the love of an entire people, the fruit, the harvest of one Sacred hope the poet, the leader, the fighter, man, It had been planned for a lifetime. It was the poetry of an entire people upright, in silence, as an oath.
