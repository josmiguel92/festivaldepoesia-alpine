MAHMUD DARWISH IN THEIR LANGUAGE OF LAND OR OLIVES OF DISCORD.

By: Jamila Madina Ríos.

The homeland of the words, of Mahmud Darwish

If olive trees crowned the triumphant warriors and the Olympic winners, in Greece, which remains as a vestige in sports medals; If the olive tree adorned the spouses, similar to the dead by whip, in Rome, as well as the front of the emperors, and it has left its mark on coins and shields; Yes the classic biblical scene of the Dove with the olive branch, as a backdrop to the flood, It is a symbol of peace and of the United Nations; on the other hand, This tree was perhaps the first source of contention between Israelis and Palestinians. The disagreement was daughter of European ambition and guilt. Dating back to the early decades of the 20th, When the ravages of anti-Semitism drove the Jews to migrate to ancient Canaan, and when after World War I, under the British protectorate, It is said that those began to acquire the land from the place, regardless of the Arab peasants were groves of this tongue of land owners.

Is true or not the anecdote, the dispute over the ancient olive groves (by their fruits and oils) It would be only the tip of a ball which has become avalanche of wars escisivas, associations, treaties to comply, interventions, walls, checkpoints, despair and blood. The poetry of Mahmud Darwish (1942-2008), from Olive leaves up to As the flower of the almond tree or beyond -passing by Lover of Palestine, At the end of the night, Birds dying in Galilee, Nuptials, Elegiac sea siege, I see what I want, Eleven stars, The bed of the foreign, Why have you left the horse alone? (o The deadly Phoenix), State site and Mural- It is a reinterpretation of the biblical and mythological tradition and, above all, a plural song about Palestinian exile and nostalgia, seasoned by ambitions, atrocities, fundamentalisms and fundamentalism. However, both texts of this selection, as the dominant attitude of Darwish to poetry and politics (reflected in three priceless interviews), they reveal a literature committed to the terroir, and first of all with the homeland of the words, with the life of its inhabitants, with their voices and desires. EPIC lyricism said Yannis Ritsos, to refer to the mosaic of beings that populate the verses of Darwish, who has built, just, a mural with those efforts, rages and hopes, who has left "the coffins of the catastrophe" and "the Gorges of the dead" ("safe and sound") resonate and "take the floor", but he has also portrayed the living: pray to the mother, the father, the brother, the martyrs, Ora village and its well, Ora refugees, Ora to the outsider with his jacket of shiny buttons...

While the translation does not allow us to access the musicality of the Arabic language, his metaphors and repetition open us a plagued realm of symbols and allegories, We are and we are not alien. The hungry, the angry dignity of an exile ("without homeland / without flag / without address"), having only the bread bag carrying with itself and some rotten trunks which dragged through the streets, but whistle at girls, layered on the walls, in the glare of their 20 years, While he assured parents that feels good, wrapped in his only costume (already mending) and wondering in the evening by the fireplace and the threshold that left, the night asking if you will recall a refugee without burial. Blood as a Ribbon of oil that feeds the lamp of liberty. The hope of honey, the vineyards, the tablecloth and the door. The Evergreen naranjal, locked behind the walls. The country's return as a bird lost and crushed, He still is expected to sprout feathers: Palestinian eyes and tattoos, "House / to blood cloths", "handkerchief of tabs", vitualla for travel and "unbreakable" rock, that it is not abandoned; as a name who knows 'mouth, dry and dusty", "wine aged in jars". The rose out of the dictionary: germinated abruptly in front of the rocks. The lock of hair of the mother, holding the poet as "a thread that glows in the tail of" [s]u dress". Bye bye handkerchiefs: as Dado. Villages mowed as Kufr Kasim and his "fifty bleeding melodies", dreaming of violets, spikes and weddings of pigeons. The hatred Bush planted in the soul and eyes, in the Sahara of the heart, After that they called at your door, in all its doors. The wings of the Sparrow, forgotten greet, detained. "Spongy flesh of the shadow", where dwells the wooden horse, broken. And under "boots of the night": the call of the willing, Dreaming with the aroma of almonds, Dreaming with the aroma of almonds, and asking not "a load", but "a powerful back". "Horses of the barbarians", saddled in the Valley, without that we know why. Two moons: in the deep and high, flying over the pit. Arab hands palm Orange skin; its Sun Orange segments, and they remember how out of home: "as the wind in the door". The Crow of God by the cracked Earth, to show Cain where to bury his brother... "Ripped out words, like feathers"and a curb that leaning to drink to howl with the dead. The exiled as a zombie, as a dead in life, lying on the bottom of the well, where throw you banana peels. The afternoon, as mount sky and smell the Arabic coffee beyond borders, beyond "the hills exposed to the summer / and the stranger", that hangs over the Valley as "a ripe Eggplant".

Mahmud Darwish understood himself as a son of traditions (not only poetic) "Jewish, Greek, Christian and Muslim", and I thought the land of Canaan as a loft... "Every time my breast shrink" - he said - "like a swallow extend the wings". Now that this writer will not be allowed to join in a football stadium 25 thousand people so excited by his verses, I think of his preoccupation with the survival among the false branches of the olive trees, rifles of outsiders and the owls and crows nests... Against the barren, There are embroidered in your carpet, flying: The Nightingale (itchy mouth), the Sparrow, the swallow and pigeons algodonales. While Palestine and Israel, They fly over like birds on the carrion of land, rather than settle finally in los olivos and learn to live on her Valley as in a Dovecote, the most daring is that a writer Mahmud Darwish as saying that all Palestinian poet "has the right, even the duty, write a love poem "and follow whistling at girls, braids and populated eyebrows, "because the girls are pleasant / oh brothers /" (and) "without them / how much more bitter would be the life".

CNG IN THAILAND AND LEON MILK, FOR CICTRIZAR.

By: Alex Rodríguez Iglesias.

Hard office exile, Nazim Hikmet

I imagine a sweeper and I think, hard office sweep. I imagine a teacher and think, hard job teaching. Imagine a farmer and I think, hard office the wrought. Each of these offices, involves cleaning, the order.
On the other hand, I imagine a writer and I think, what you can do it, wretched, from a clean page, that is not known in what hands will fall. And also imagine an exile, become clean page, flying in the air, without falling here and there, without sitting head.
NazimHikmet, Thessaloniki, Ottoman Empire, 1902- 1963, not imagined a thing nor the other. NazimHikmet wrote, and was exiled, redeeming the imagination by the experience.
Son of a painter and an official, He studied at the French Institute of Galatasaray in Istanbul, and later in the naval school of Turkey. During the war of independence, He joined Ataturk in Anatolia and served as master in Bolu. In 1921, impressed by the Russian Revolution, traveled to the Soviet Union. He studied sociology and Economics at the University of Moscow and joined the Communist Party in Turkish.
Sentenced to 15 years in prison, found freedom.
The poetry was freedom.
Forced to leave their country of birth, also left, in search of new forms of expression, traditional forms of Turkish poetry.

I was fifteen when I read for the first time to NazimHikmet. Today I am twenty-eight years old, I'm less young, less innocent. Poetry limits me to see things not just as they are, But terrible, always. Where I put my book NazimHikmet? For now he is in a Chair, sharing space with my fan. Chair is white and the fan, Blue. Perpendicular to the bed, on the right side, where to sleep, my hand reaches the Chair and everything it contains. About the book of NazimHikmet, salbutamol and beclomethasone. Beside the book nail file, a calcium for nails, and, important detail, a tube of CNG in Thailand, to heal.
NazimHikmet, also, to heal, or to more open wound?

Almost seventy poems, I have read in a few hours, knowing that they come from terror, as much as love. Coming and going, repeating poems, reading aloud and the view, crying poems while I go from table to kitchen, cooking me in NazimHikmet sauce, like their tomatoes, their green peppers, their fried turbot, and a glass of raki (This is fiction, Lords) leon milk, and anise. From his awakening in the morning in Turkish, It is also my morning, and to hope.
Is it that there is no hope? The hope is the man.
I do not throw your network, NazimHikmet. Your network is prodigious and dangerous. I am afraid of your network as a black sheep. I read that and the wound in my tattoo gives me a morning sting: By the seashore on the threshold of the sea / as a network / rain around me / on rainy days / is the white flag at the mast / raining and suddenly it's easy to die / and wait for death is equally easy.
Already seduced, I want to be the bride of NazimHikmet. She is, as the homeland, in all the poems. The woman wife and mother woman. Joy and sadness of women, She the writer knows he is a man. Country woman: "You went out of jail / and then / left pregnant your wife / Thomas by arm / and the night walk around the neighborhood. / belly of the Lady almost reach you the nose / she wears with grace that sacred burden."
I want to be the son of NazimHikmet. The poems he has written, the years in prison, pain, the sea, and the sky of Turkey, are, will be, my inheritance: "I'm going but I'm calm / life that is dying on me / will continue for a long time in you / and in our town", eternally."

Many, like me, they have read and are familiar with this book. A few pages that so many have learned from memory. Above all the classical poem: "I love you / the adventure of the boat sailing towards the pole / I love you / the audacity of authors of great discoveries / I love you far / I love you the impossible / entered in us eyes as in a jungle / all full of Sun / and sweaty", hungry and angry / breath passion for biting in your flesh Hunter. / "I love you in the impossible / but by no means despair."

The book of NazimHikmet, your photo that air as a fan, and the index fingertip, gel of CNG in Thailand.And because of NazimHikmet, a wound, "washed with sea water / and wire brush / a bridge swept away by the wind / and in this village of the South", "without pause or break / the sun goes colouring and swelling of honey / apricots and the girls."

In the morning thank you, NazimHikmet, by the cream of onion that exist in your poetry, by your simple words sodium heparin, Universal, and by the final book allantoin, Since my hands thanks to the South collection, directed by Alex Pausides and sponsored by the Instituto Cubano del Libro and the international poetry Festival of Havana.
Thank you.

8 may of 2013, 11:00 am.
