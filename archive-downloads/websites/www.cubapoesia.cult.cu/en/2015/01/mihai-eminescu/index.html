Mihai Eminescu o Mihail Eminovici (15 in January of 1850 – 15 of June of 1889) was a poet of the late romantic. It is possibly the best-known Romanian poet worldwide, his works still best-known Luceafărul (The morning star or the morning star), Mai am a singur dor (I already Miss less just one more thing), and 5 Scrisori (The five Epistles).

It is unquestionably, the largest 19th century Romanian poet, and is still considered by the majority of the Romanian national poet because in his meditations, it reflects the most trustworthy of the Romanian soul concerns. His work has been translated into more of 60 languages.
