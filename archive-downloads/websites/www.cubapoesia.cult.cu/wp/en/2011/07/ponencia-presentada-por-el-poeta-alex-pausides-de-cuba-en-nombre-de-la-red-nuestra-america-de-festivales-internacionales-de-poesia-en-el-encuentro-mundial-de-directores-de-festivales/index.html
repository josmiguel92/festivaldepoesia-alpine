by Teresa

4 July, 2011

FROMOUR AMÉRICA NETWORK

TO THE WORLD MEETING OF DIRECTORS OF INTERNATIONAL POETRY FESTIVALS, IN MEDELLIN

 

1…MATERIAL POVERTY AND SPIRITUAL WEALTH

Poetry is the natural state of spirit in this part of the world. Our nations entered history by the hand of the poets. Poetry and imagination outlined our destiny as a people. Many of our greatest men of action they were also the greatness of his spirit. Our poetry expresses a unique way of being in the cosmos. Therefore, its existence has become a fact of life among us. Led by the International Poetry Festival of Medellin has gained momentum, often in the most precarious material conditions, a movement that is based on the transforming power of poetry and dedication to service of poets. The ivory tower was placed in the Museum of Man, liberty leading the people devastated our laziness and highlighted that the world exists beyond us, perhaps prey of disbelief, apathy or the most hopeful urgency. In our America poets live the challenge that run the majority so long excluded, silenced or ignored. Five hundred years of neglect is a bundle difficult to remove but if people try to do, we, the poets must stand by them. Living in that dream. The work is in that utopia. Or both. As José Martí or Leonel Rugama or Javier Heraud. As part of that destiny we try to work together. We need to work together. Keep unity in the most absolute diversity. in the most absolute respect for difference. the difference that strengthens our purpose in shaping a better future for men and women of our America. Tough, certainly annoying task that of finding the economic resources, to explore the ways of cooperation with regional or international entities, to envision priorities, to plan as realistically as possible within the necessary, those are tasks of the first order. To encourage or promote, personal projects, shares or concerted, regional or global. Favoring the development of audiences among children and youth, support all support publishing projects, ensure a presence in the virtual world, visualize and recognize indigenous cultures, alternatives to a culture of resistance, from a democratizing vision of culture and knowledge. It is true that the poet to his work had enough of the words, but to improve the world we live in is necessary that the word is sustained in a position marked by the ethical citizen, the most elementary understanding of human beings and a common sense that conjure up the danger of a return to the Stone Age. In Our America, despite being convinced of the severe limits of poetic action, believe that even with words –beautiful or terrible, many alternatives have yet to realize the simplest of our ambitions: that poetry serve others and ourselves in the creation and enjoyment of the beauty of nature and civilization have been able to collect and preserve.

 

2…PEACE AND RECONSTRUCTION OF THE HUMAN SPIRIT

The world today faces the dilemma man before passing into barbarism or rebuild on new foundations the human spirit. Wisdom must be saved from destruction. The universal values ​​that humanity has accumulated in its becoming the wealth should be paramount. The struggle for coexistence between different cultures, religions, ethnicities or ideologies might well give meaning essential to human life on earth. Set up a livable world in harmony with the rest of nature is the page pending civilization and poets can write that verse in the random blank page in our future. The banishment of weapons and wars, moral condemnation of violence and exploitation of men by others and participation in the design and conduct of their own destiny are the premises of the survival of the species. Poets, there is no second chance for the miracle of life in the universe, be alert.

 

3…RECOVERY OF NATURE

In difficult times, poets more than necessary are essential. Being a poet in tough times means paying attention to light things to come and the sacred name, reveal what they were and are and will. Witnesses of human history, his words transcend the here and now, and mark our place in the world. When we ask what the world of tomorrow, have to think that our world is not inherited from past generations, we have borrowed from future generations, which implies a strong commitment. The struggle against the depredations of nature, the devastation of the forests, emissions of gases that destroy the atmosphere has fervent activist poets. Poets are the bridges connecting, various sources of the same water that, as the poet Carlos Pellicer: Save the mystery always be mystery. Save the man from being merely a man.

 

4…UNIT OF HUMAN SPIRIT AND CULTURAL DIVERSITY OF PEOPLES

The New World, linked in with half a millennium of Western civilization, still faces the need to overcome exclusions, denials, omissions and silences generated by the metropolitan spoliation of the native peoples who lived here before the European conquest. Tierra, languages, cultures, worldviews: inalienable capital of the American people. With concern we see how greed prevails, intolerance, violence and greed on the plundering of Mother Nature. We crept carrying the legacy of shame, that shows the world the worst rates in terms of inequality, inequality, material and spiritual poverty, malnutrition and indifference to the generations that just arrive at our common home, Planet Earth. We do not know freedom, love, solidarity, respect, harmony, Happy Life, happy life, because the subordinate system al, we reproduce, perpetuate the. It is necessary to assume a responsible attitude of listening, bring attention to oral language, paralinguistic language, sign language; read the context, read community aspirations, listen with your heart not only with the ear, open our eyes to knowledge, return to our roots to transform our lives. It is necessary to assume the attitude that leads us not to know but to be, which allows not retain, or grab, but share. Back to our worldview in a relationship of the material and spiritual, interpretation of time, elements that coexist in our mother nature, in the macrocosm and microcosm. A must critically review the educational models that have been anchored in the school survives and reproduces the system. Still, at the dawn of another century, We walk on education based on memorization and repetition that overrides the lobe of the brain, which is dealing with the imagination, creativity and expression; that uniform and castrates the inherent capabilities; there, the cry is still enforced, prepotencia la discriminación y la. It is for this harsh reality that we are committed to cooperation, love for life, respect and unity in diversity, enhancement of the expressive capabilities, critical thinking, debate, dialogue and new relationships between people and nature.

 

5…ACTIONS IN THE GLOBAL WORLD

We assume the domain of communication technologies as a tool of the first magnitude. An incident in the antipodes in the public domain immediately. This simple fact opens up a huge possibility never before imagined in the history of mankind. Individual actions, make viable bilateral or global attempt to change the world. Poetry is a tool, a resilient body in his vocation to record the joy and pain of man, extolling nature and the human spirit in its desire for self-knowledge, master knowledge and science conquer. A poet's word against a shell can not but warn mankind about the danger and help shape a better future for man on earth. Convinced of the potential of the word and work together, in the global world we poets-over isolation and indifference, to be part of the solution.

 

Mtra. Norma Cardenas Zurita, Marvin Garcia, Alex Pausides
