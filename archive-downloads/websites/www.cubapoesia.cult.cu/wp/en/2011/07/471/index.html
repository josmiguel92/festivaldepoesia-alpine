AWARD-CubaPoesía Wols

The International Poetry Festival of Havana and the Asia-Latin American Cultural Foundation, Bogota, a shared desire to promote the creation, knowledge and dissemination of new Cuban poetry summon, co-sponsored by the Journal Amnion and Dulce Maria Loynaz Cultural Center, Award Wolsan-CubaPoesía 2011.

BASES

1. Participation of young people under the age of Cuban poets 35 years.

2. The theme of the works will be free

3. It will compete with unpublished notebooks of no more than 30 pages.

4. The works will be presented under a pseudonym, original and two copies.

5. The competing works will be delivered in the Coordination of International Poetry Festival in Havana at the Centro Cultural Dulce Maria Loynaz.

6. The jury, composed of three prominent Cuban poets, Books will select six finalists and award a sole otorgará, consisting Diploma, plate, al invitation Poetry Festival, the book and $1500.00 CUC.

7. Works can be delivered from 24 May 2011.

8. The deadline closes 12 October 2011.

9. The work awarded the prize will be announced during the International Book Fair of Havana, in February 2012.

10. Wols Award–CubaPoesía be delivered on the opening day of the International Poetry Festival in Havana in May 2012. The distinguished work will be presented during the International Poetry Festival.
