by Teresa

19 August, 2011

 

V Congress South Cultural Project

Havana, Cuba, 20 the 24 May, 2012 

 

"Never before has humanity been so on the brink of collapse as a civilization, life itself never has been so threatened, paradoxically, by those who have the ability to understand, study and reproduce the mechanisms of life. " These were the opening words of the call to the Fourth Congress SOUTH, made in Ayacucho. These words have become obsolete, This is not a merit, but a challenge to the art of finding solutions, a challenge for the ferment of a culture of change.

 

Another paragraph added: "As cultural workers we have the threefold mission of: deliver the keys to change, illuminate exit doors of the imagination in people and recognize the suffering humanity that cries a change. Without God we have the task of building work in six periods of the possibility of a different world. "

 

And that we, developed a, others set the pace and some clouded by problems without apparent solution. That is the reality or reality that we have for, with proxy wars, with hundreds of ways to say enough, with the outrages that since the powers are made on the life. A culture at the height of human intellect and human potential. Lack humanize humans.

 

Without being a partisan proposal is a policy proposal, or rather without a political agenda is a proposed party, for our party and our policy is to be human in their capacity as creator of new realities, in our task of creating the mechanisms for developing the potential of human creation and the task of equipping ourselves with a culture that respects differences and make all actors.

Large and complex the task, as complex as big now may be the new future of mankind. It is from our multiple realities that can, and we want to produce the miracle of what it takes to move forward together as creative entities.

 

We summarize the issues raised in the previous edition. “A culture of war must oppose the culture of peace for all; the culture of exclusion and contempt ninguneo, the culture of creative participation of new realities; the culture of fear, culture of the liberating truth; the culture of manipulation, fraternity culture; the culture of least resistance or the laissez-faire, the culture of the challenge of reaching the sky with the hand, culture not to put limits on the creative intellect. "

We call on all Sures, all good people, to any and all creators, and organizers to, the cultural and developers to be part of the V SOUTH active and merge from the thousands of actions towards meeting Havana, of 20 the 24 May 2012.

 

En Montreal, August 2011, in the path of the V.

 

International Honorary Presidency

South Cultural Project

 

Working guidelines of the V

The V Congress will open as pre-event of the XIV International Festival of La Habanay development will be parallel, the idea of ​​adding we join the Festival program. It will be a discussion, meeting and promoting what is in the world and the culture of life.

 

The Companions of Sur La Habanaasumen task: develop and promote Congress V SOUTH (of 20 the 24 May, 2012); schedule participation in the Festival of Poetry of the members and friends of South Cultural Project; organize Meetings organizations, personalities and institutions related (one day).

As they pose for previous Congresses, reiterate some initiatives:

 

  1. Spreading the call to Congress and invite activists and cultural personalities close;
  2. Search and find solutions to ensure some sort of financing for participants;
  3. Organize pre-conference events: I.- discussion forums with other organizations, II.- promotional concerts, III.- multidisciplinary initiatives in the traditional places, IV cultural assaults, etc..

In accordance with SURL Havana sent a plan and a draft of expectations.

As in every Congress, confirm the next and where do we solve the subsequent, It is set to the Sixth Congress in Venezuela, responsible for defining where to make the VII Congress.

The discussion is open. United in difference to add sumándonos.

 

Congress Regulations

  1. In Congress involved in its own right (1) members of the International Board (for now there is), (2) Presidents and national leaders in countries south there, (3) all the delegates of the various nuclei SUR and (4) special guests.
  2. Each core can participate with the number of delegates as possible, except that when solving, each core is a vote.
  3. The Congress is preceded by local conferences which will discuss the report to Congress V, to be delivered no later than the last week of January 2012 (three months before).
  4. In each Congress Core recommend taking stock of their activities, consider future actions, This means focusing on the task with a mindset that endorses and action that enables.
  5. At the International Congress every member of the International Board of Directors, SOUTH each President of the national and local core each must submit a written report. The cores or unable to participate in a delegate, must submit its report on the Internet with a minimum of one month before, to let them get to each core SOUTH.
  6. The discussion will take place through workshops and plenary.
  7. Each desk nominates a / a chair / a and a / a secretary / a. Discussion starts with a minimum document, to be delivered before the Congress, and the discussion is a summary to be presented at the plenary. The workshops and their topics will be resolved in accordance with all in the coming months Sures.
  8. The plenary define the resolutions of Congress.
  9. At the end of the Congress will proceed to the election of the international steering and confirmed define or redefine the place of the next Congress and discussed the proposals for the subsequent Silk.
  10. The resolutions will be sent to all later than one week after, same shall be disseminated by all means.

See you in Havana in every cultural action for a culture of change and respect for difference.
