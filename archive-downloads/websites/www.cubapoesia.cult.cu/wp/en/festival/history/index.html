Origin and essence

The International Poetry Festival of Havana emerged 1996 sponsored by the Instituto Cubano del Libro, the Office of the Historian of the City, Union of Writers and Artists of Cuba and South Cultural Project, whose own projection in Latin America has been very influential Cuba's annual event. Poetry festival so endowed with a network of sixty cities in Latin America, United States and Europe and now has an international support group that allowed the realization of 800 readings over 400 cities around the world in May 2010.

The Poetry Festival of Havana is an ideal place not only for the search of an audience, promoting poetry and poets citizen action, but to jump-start participation strategies, articular closer relations with foreign associations related, Promotional dynamic structure in a context that can not ignore the importance of communication and exchange, the usefulness of the cultivation of feeling, humanistic values ​​and the exercise of critical thinking in a world that is essential to transform the arms of the trial that, as José Martí said , the greatest Cuban, due to other.
